## Corrections and sources
1. **1979 versus 1980; 80 bursaries versus an offer of support.** The institution's [chronology](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/who-we-are/our-history) assigns its first UCT scholarships to **1979**. Independent [ProBono.org 25th-anniversary coverage](https://probono.org.za/the-open-society-foundation-in-sa-turns-25/) also reports a 1979 launch and mentions a recipient from that year. However, George Soros's [own 1 November 2016 account](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/voices/scholarships-launched-open-society-foundations) recalls visiting South Africa in **1980**, when he agreed to fund **living expenses for up to 80** Black UCT students; the government already covered accepted students' tuition. The source record supports an early programme but does not reconcile Soros's visit chronology or establish 80 independently documented completed student payments. This matters to the page's hero, narrative, timeline and key-finding card; all were qualified without rewriting the historical network dataset.
2. **The “more than $10m in confirmed university disbursements” claim was not proven.** The three existing visible USD figures are **$4.83m+, $2.6m+, $1.03m+**, whose printed floor values add to **$8.46m**. Such `+` amounts may denote lower bounds, but they do not independently establish either the actual sum or bank disbursements; no complete award-ID, recipient, fiscal-year and transfer schedule was present. The `$10m+` disbursement assertion was reclassified **UNVERIFIED** and the university graphic's *heading* corrected to identify unreconciled figures. The printed amounts were **not altered** pending original grants and accounts.
3. **Separate funder and accounting concepts.** Stellenbosch's [26 January 2026 institutional report](https://www.su.ac.za/en/faculties/arts/departments/journalism/news/grant-over-r20-million-cinia) describes the over-R20m IDRC award and work beginning in January 2026. Earlier 2025 reporting exists, but this dated institutional page is the source checked for the award here. The R20m displayed figure is not OSF philanthropy or evidence of a 2026 cash disbursement; it is separately attributed to IDRC. No sums across different currencies or funders were silently combined.

## Independent checks and adverse evidence
- The independent 2018 [ProBono.org anniversary record](https://probono.org.za/the-open-society-foundation-in-sa-turns-25/) supports the 1979 programme date, whereas the founder's first-person recollection supplies contrary **1980 visit** evidence. Neither proves a UCT transaction ledger.
- The [Guardian's independent 2018 historical assessment](https://www.theguardian.com/news/2018/jul/06/the-george-soros-philosophy-and-its-fatal-flaw) also dates the beginnings of Soros philanthropy to 1979, but does not provide recipient-level payment evidence. The discrepancy is preserved instead of resolved by speculation.
- The original [OSF-SA 2025 retrospective](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/publications/30-years-of-the-open-society-foundation-for-south-africa) records later institutional operations, but an institutional annual history is not a bank audit of named university awards.
