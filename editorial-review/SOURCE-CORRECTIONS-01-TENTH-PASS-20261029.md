## Claim-level correction — which foundation financed the Legal Resources Centre at its creation?

**Before:** In the “OSF Board to ConCourt Bridge” card, the article accurately named Ford, Carnegie and Rockefeller Brothers Fund as original Legal Resources Centre supporters, but then stated that “the same foundation ecosystem (Ford, OSF) ... funded the LRC's creation.” That construction incorrectly projects OSF-SA back onto a **1979** LRC founding. OSF-SA itself commenced in **1993** (according to the investigation's already documented institutional retrospective).

**After:** The original LRC founders were **Arthur Chaskalson, Felicia Kentridge and Geoff Budlender**, according to the LRC's own history. The independent Rockefeller Brothers Fund donor history records original support from **RBF, Ford, Carnegie and local South African donors**. Ford's later participation with OSF and Atlantic in the **2015 Constitutionalism Fund** is a separate arrangement; the fund's later US$29m programme total must not be treated as a 1979 LRC payment. Professional connections and grants warrant investigation but cannot establish donor orders to judges, amicus briefs or judicial decisions.
