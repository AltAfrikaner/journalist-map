## Claim-level correction

**2018 projection does not cap 2021 recruitment — CORRECTED.** The primary **4 December 2018** Rhodes University call announced five fellowships each year for 2019, 2020 and 2021, producing the historical *planned* fifteen-slot denominator. Distinct 2020 recruitment postings for the **2021 cohort** reproduced an amended advertisement for **seven 2021 positions**, one reserved for a candidate from another sub-Saharan African country subject to travel permission. The published page previously treated the original fifteen projected places as an enduring total. It now documents both announcements without adding them into a fabricated awards count. The actual 2019/2020/2021 selections, final Wits/Rhodes enrolments, individual stipend transfers, internship placements and programme-wide totals remain **UNVERIFIED**.

**Source-quality caveat:** The 2021 postings independently appear on two websites but may reproduce the **same issuer announcement**; they are not independent evidence of disbursement or award recipients. A complete archived issuer-hosted revision of the 2021 call was not recovered in this pass. The stronger conclusion is that *the advertised 2021 intake varied*, not that seven students were enrolled.
