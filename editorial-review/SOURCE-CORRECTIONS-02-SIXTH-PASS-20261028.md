## Specific independent-source comparison
**SABC-to-foreign-media pipeline and tenure chronology.** The original static paragraph already rejected an unjustified claim that seven selected journalists had all transferred to foreign broadcasters; the new evidence makes this more concrete. An SABC report dated **13 December 2025**, corroborated by the United Nations Correspondents Association's own 2025 award page and South African government reporting, identifies **Sherwin Bryce-Pease and Aaron Berbrick as an SABC reporting team** for the Ricardo Ortega Gold Medal. This is direct counterevidence to treating the mere location of the UN bureau in New York as employment by a foreign broadcaster. This does not establish the complete employment records of the other six listed journalists.

**Conflict recorded, not silently resolved:** Rhodes University's **21 September 2016** profile based on an alumni interview says Bryce-Pease returned to the United States to become SABC's US correspondent in **2008**. The UN Correspondents Association's **2016 award biography**, apparently supplied as an award biography, instead says he had been based at UN Headquarters in New York **since 2002**. The two claims refer to overlapping dates, and the precise start of any earlier New York assignment remains **UNVERIFIED** pending appointment/employment documents. The original 3D tooltip continues to display 2008; it was not edited or treated as independently certified.
