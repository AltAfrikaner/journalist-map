## What changed
One substantive paragraph added alongside the 2017 farm-attack murder-rate methodology discussion. Africa Check's 27 September 2018 spot-check *challenged* President Ramaphosa's sweeping denial that farmers/white farmers were being killed. The public police records for 2017/18 gave **62 total murders on farms and agricultural smallholdings, 46 white victims, and 52 victims listed as owners/occupiers**. These categories overlap but are not interchangeable: there is no racial breakdown of the owners-only count. This is an essential counterexample when testing alleged editorial selectivity, and corrects potential reader inference that Africa Check always downplayed farm murders. It does **not** settle a population-based rate, bias motive or white-commercial-farmer-specific victim count.
