## Correction and added independent corroboration
- The existing 13 November 2018 Africa Check case described a Port Elizabeth Community and Police Cooperation Centre; its 16 November Chinese diplomatic statement was correctly dated *after* the fact-check. This pass adds AFP's **23 November 2018 independent investigation**, which separately interviewed SAPS spokesperson Colonel Priscilla Naidu and centre chief Jie Lee Zhang. AFP reported **14** community-police cooperation centres including the Port Elizabeth opening. This is neither 14 delegated police stations nor proof that no community centre ever committed an unlawful act.
- Source chronology is important: the 28 October opening prompted the allegation; Africa Check published 13 November; diplomatic statement followed 16 November; AFP published 23 November. The original article should not be described as *only* an acceptance of a Chinese government denial. The additional contemporaneous reporting independently supports the narrower classification as **not formally SAPS police stations**.
