## Adversarial / red-team finding

The two EFF studies document scrutiny of a left-wing party, **not** unbiased selection across *all* parties, topics and languages. Conversely, their mix of favourable and unfavourable EFF verdicts contradicts a categorical claim that Africa Check only published unfavourable EFF reviews. The state land audit does not supply the complete unambiguous national racial-ownership denominator required to resolve broader historical distributional claims. Neither a factual gap nor a rejected politician's number establishes deliberate editorial suppression.
