## Claim examined: Africa Facts Network — 2026 summit chronology and governance

The article correctly reported Africa Check's self-described **58 member organisations, over 35 countries and 25 IFCN signatories**, but its description of reach did not record a consequential 2026 governance development or separate conflicting dated location notices.

1. **Primary original (5 June 2026):** [Africa Check's Johannesburg announcement, now with its 3 August editor's correction](https://www.africacheck.org/fact-checks/blog/africas-premier-information-integrity-summit-returns-johannesburg-october-2026).
2. **Independent older record (22 June):** [Media Rights Agenda's report](https://mediarightsagenda.org/africa-check-to-hold-fifth-edition-of-africa-facts-summit-in-johannesburg/) repeated the original Johannesburg venue and stated that an Africa Facts Network Advisory Committee was being established.
3. **Primary correction (3 August):** [Africa Check's revised Nairobi announcement](https://www.africacheck.org/fact-checks/blog/africas-premier-information-integrity-summit-take-place-nairobi-october-2026) superseded the host city, retained **21–22 October 2026** and publicly named **Eric Mugendi** as committee chair.
4. **Independent event-provider record:** [Quicket Kenya event listing](https://www.quicket.co.ke/events/377880-africa-facts-summit-2026/) identifies Nairobi's Safari Park Hotel & Casino for those dates and now marks the event finished. A ticket listing is not an independently audited attendance, voting or meeting-minutes register.
5. **Primary programme description:** [Africa Facts Network](https://www.africacheck.org/africa-facts-network) provides the 58 / over 35 / 25 self-described figures. These need not have the same date or definitions as the separate summit press release's looser 'over 30 countries' formulation.

**Correction:** Added a dated, source-linked distinction between **earlier Johannesburg plans** and the subsequently documented **Nairobi event**, plus the committee's disclosed existence and chair. Corrected the article's visible research date to **31 October 2026**. The committee description is evidence of a declared governance structure, **not** evidence that it directed fact-check verdicts.

**Red-team interpretation:** A new advisory committee could indicate decentralisation, network collaboration or governance standardisation. Its existence alone neither proves independence from funders nor centralised censorship; choosing either story requires appointment rules, minutes, votes, financial agreements, conflict disclosures and case-specific records. The independent venue listing confirms the announced location but does not independently establish substantive meeting outcomes.

**Still UNVERIFIED:** committee appointment and voting rights; grant-linked editorial conditions; how its advice affects 58 member organisations; any particular Meta rating/appeal or systematic Afrikaner-targeting outcome; 2025 exact income shares not recoverable from the public image-text extraction. This pass is not a full funding or graph audit.
