## Independent contemporaneous cross-check and numerical discrepancy

The case already cited Africa Check's 13 November 2018 inquiry and AFP's 23 November follow-up. An earlier **1 November 2018 on-site Herald interview** with Jie Lee Zhang and SAPS Captain Khaya Tonjeni documents their explanation of community liaison and a Mandarin-language programme said to have begun in 2016. The same basic-service explanation was made before the later global secret-police-station controversy. It is **testimony**, not independent proof of lawful activity at all centres.

A **2 November 2018 Africanews** article, later updated, identified the new Port Elizabeth cooperation centre as **the 13th**. AFP's later report instead said there were **14 including Port Elizabeth** after speaking separately to SAPS Colonel Naidu and the centre head. Neither contemporary report supplied an audited national register. The article now flags the numerical discrepancy as **UNVERIFIED**, not a fact-checking verdict about 13/14 legally empowered Chinese police stations.
