## Correction / funded-recipient example
The old Google/YouTube card treated all recipient-level cash records as unverified. **FactCheck.org's independently published funding ledger** directly reports **$25,598** in **January–March 2025** and **$8,100** in **April–June 2025**, each attributed to Google/YouTube for an **IFCN-administered grant** to grow FactCheck.org's social-media audience. This supplies a specific, dated recipient-level disclosed funding pathway, not merely a diagrammatic association.

Poynter describes a $13.2m Google/YouTube commitment announced in November 2022, including a **$12m** Global Fact Check Fund, and separately announces downstream 2025 grant recipients. **Do not presume** those two individual FactCheck.org payments originated in the $12m component, assign them to Africa Check, or infer a donor-directed verdict without the signed subgrant and accounting identifiers. The revised public card clarifies that a programme-wide paid-versus-awarded register and most recipient contracts remain **UNVERIFIED** rather than making the erroneous all-recipient claim.
