## Corrections actually made
1. **Undated geographic exclusion and a current-name allegation.** A blanket assertion of zero IFCN governing-board representation for a long list of countries was not an audited current roster and is now **UNVERIFIED**. The actual IFCN published roster includes Africa Check deputy director **Noko Makgato**. That is a genuine governance overlap; neither an undated assertion about **Dapo Olorunyomi** holding two seats nor a specific recusal failure is established without contemporaneous board and meeting records.
2. **Accreditation procedure.** IFCN describes external assessments **before** final votes by verified-signatory representatives. Its policy lists 15 verified-signatory board seats and up to four independent members, who do **not** vote on incoming applications. It is inaccurate to portray every tier as only director-and-board self-judgment with no external assessor. HTML *explanatory complaints ladder text*, not the embedded SVG, was corrected and the distinction is documented here.
3. **Complaints and appeals.** IFCN says it handles alleged breaches of its **Code** but is not an appellate body for individual verdicts; those are first the signatory's and any relevant statutory press regulator's responsibility. Applicants may appeal assessor treatment **to IFCN's board**. Absence of a verified case-level external review pathway is an open accountability concern, but does not establish that no complaint mechanism or other remedy exists.
4. **Misleading public homepage copy.** Replaced “certification determines which content Meta suppresses” and the undated “Peter Cunliffe-Jones now sits on IFCN” with documentary accreditation/Meta-action distinctions and a current documented cross-institution role. The HTML concluding editorial critique of private peer oversight is retained but no longer represents unaccountability and exclusive global Meta control as settled facts.
