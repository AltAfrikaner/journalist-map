## Claim-level correction
The IFCN dossier already states that IFCN does not adjudicate every individual published fact-check or reverse platform enforcement. It now documents an explicit remedy distinction: **an IFCN Code-of-Principles complaint is not the same as appealing an individual Meta rating**. Affected publishers should preserve the original post and its Meta notice, contact the responsible fact-checker via the published dispute/correction route and distinguish fact-checker rating review from platform distribution consequences.

Meta's dated October 2023 guidance specified seven days to submit a publisher correction/dispute and automatic rejection of unprocessed appeals after fourteen days. Those terms are **historical descriptions**, not proven October 2026 South African deadlines. A March 2026 independently produced Oversight Board opinion confirms that rated posts can carry distribution or advertising consequences and that appeal mechanisms exist in markets where Meta retains third-party checks. It does not establish successful local reversals.

**UNVERIFIED:** Whether an individual complainant was refused an IFCN Code examination, how many 2026 South African appeals succeeded, contract-level requirements, named Meta account effects, case-level proof of bias and exact contemporary appeal service conditions. The article makes no blanket allegation of suppression from mere procedural overlap.
