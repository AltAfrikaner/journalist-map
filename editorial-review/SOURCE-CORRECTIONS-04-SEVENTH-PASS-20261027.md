## Claim corrections
1. **Contradictory official counts:** IFCN/Poynter's **12 June 2025** Code report said 182 verified organisations in 57 countries; Poynter's **2025–26 impact report**, printed in February 2026, gives “170+” and “80+ countries” for 2025. Both are Poynter institutional figures. Their distinct geographic scope or reporting denominator has not been reconciled; neither is a verified live October 2026 census. Dated the text stat labels, explicitly disclaimed the original SVG number without changing the SVG.
2. **Gates chronology/recipient:** Tim Schwab's **21 August 2020 CJR** investigation records $383,000 to Poynter (2015). PolitiFact's **primary recipient disclosure** records separate Gates entries of **$70,000 (2015)** and **$126,650 (2016)** for global health fact-checks with Africa Check. PolitiFact states Poynter acquired it in **2018**. Previous wording omitted the $70,000 and left $126,650 undated; it also invited inappropriate attribution of multiple grants to IFCN. These figures are **not** a certified IFCN aggregate. CJR's 16-case qualitative criticism remains attributed, not upgraded to evidence of an order or conclusive bias.
3. **NED legal category:** NED's published FAQ/history and underlying 1983 framework distinguish a Congress-funded **private nongovernmental nonprofit** from USAID or State Department as a federal agency. Reclassified card accordingly; left alleged World Learning pass-through and PPP/VOA items **UNVERIFIED** without award IDs.
4. **Complaint filter inference:** Replaced speculative implications of actually excluded valid complainants with an explicit evidence threshold and **UNVERIFIED** designation.
