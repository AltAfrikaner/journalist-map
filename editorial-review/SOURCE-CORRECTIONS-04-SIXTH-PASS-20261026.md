## Exact corrections

1. **US$13.2m grant versus US$12m Fund.** Google and YouTube announced a **US$13.2m overall grant to IFCN on 29 November 2022**, including **US$12m for the Global Fact Check Fund**, launched for grantee applications in 2023. The article's old wording incorrectly equated the entire grant to the onward-grant pool and dated the fund 2022–2025. The headline metric now calls the figure a dated grant. The remaining US$1.2m related to supplementary support for IFCN monitoring, selection and capacity in the announcement, **not an identified Africa Check grant**.
2. **US$8.5m and recipients as of 29 May 2025.** The **174 organisations / 72 countries** and **US$8.5m+** figures are IFCN's **awards reported as of 29 May 2025**, not verified year-end cash payments and not an audited 2026 cumulative. The article's three advertised award tiers remain historically contextual.
3. **Survey denominator.** The 6 April 2026 report covers the **2025 calendar year**, 141 responding organisations from 71 countries; different questions have different response counts. The 34.3% / 45.5% figure is a reported sample statistic, **not Africa Check's own financing**, IFCN central office income or a single global revenue share.
4. **ByteDance/CPC attribution.** The historical 60/138 assertion derives from reporting on alleged leaked 2020 committee documents, **not a verified corporate register** in this investigation. We removed language that promoted reported employee associations into an established chain of CPC commercial direction of IFCN. ByteDance contract, amount, true recipient entity and any alleged political instructions remain **UNVERIFIED**.

## Red-team and protection

A concentration of standards, money and platform access gives legitimate reasons to demand independent recusal records, contracts, grants and case-level appeal data. A funder's grant, a voluntary certification or an alleged corporate political association **does not prove an instruction to censor Afrikaner posts**. The existing historical IFCN SVG still includes unverified funding and causal captions; it was **deliberately not modified** under the preserve-graphics rule and needs diagram-level release review.

All original scripts, SVG, style blocks, images, media, element IDs and **79 original article hyperlinks** were checked unchanged, with five documentary links added. The article's JS, visualisation, URL and material editorial viewpoint were preserved. Full published browser/mobile access checks, grant contracts, financial statements, individual case evidence and public CDN freshness remain outstanding. **Do not certify release.**
