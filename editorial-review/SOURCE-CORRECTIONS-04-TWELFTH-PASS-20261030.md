## Separate outage chronology, first-party explanations and unproven casualties

The page already distinguishes a 21 March concert from a later viral allegation. This pass adds a dated, attributable explanation of the **21 March 2026** nationwide grid collapse: Cuba's state utility Unión Eléctrica (UNE) publicly reported that generation unit **6 at Nuevitas** tripped before a cascading shutdown. A contemporaneous first-party UNE statement is preserved verbatim in reporting, while AP and Reuters independently reported the outage; these are **not a commissioned engineering audit**.
