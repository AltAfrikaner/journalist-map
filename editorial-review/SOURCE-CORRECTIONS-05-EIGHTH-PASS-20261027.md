## Claim-level finding and correction

The Stage One introduction said Prof Herman Wasserman **is** the chair of Stellenbosch University's Department of Journalism. This conflated an appointment effective January 2023 with the university's **current** staff register. The [current Stellenbosch departmental directory](https://www.su.ac.za/en/faculties/arts/departments/journalism/staff) lists **Dr Marenet Jordaan** as chair and Wasserman as **CINIA director and professor of Journalism**. The university's [4 July 2022 appointment announcement](https://www.su.ac.za/en/node/6285) establishes that Wasserman was due to take up the chair in January 2023. An [independent 26 February 2024 interview in AJEN](https://ajenafrica.com/lets-rethink-journalism-education-wasserman/) also describes his tenure as department chair at that time. Older [2024 National Research Foundation profile](https://www.nrf.ac.za/about-us/nrf-awards/2024-2/2024-a-rated-researchers-a-rated-for-the-1st-time/professor-herman-wasserman/) and [2025 CINIA biography](https://cinia.africa/herman-wasserman-phd/) preserve this historical designation; they are not reliable proof of his current administrative post.

**Correction applied:** Stage One retains Wasserman's verified professorship and CINIA directorship, identifies Jordaan as current departmental chair with a source link, and explains the 2023 appointment chronology. A past department chair title cannot establish current departmental control, Africa Check rating authority, or an instruction to Meta. Specific instructions or contracts remain **UNVERIFIED**. The R20m IDRC award remains identified as a research award to CINIA, not a proven disbursement or Meta contract.
