## Primary-versus-independent legal comparison

1. The [IEC's 8 September 2026 original statement](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local) confirms its Disinformation Code under s 88(a) Municipal Electoral Act was effective **4 September 2026 until election results are declared**, for political parties/candidates and people acting on their instruction/authority/for reward. It expressly protects satire, lawful criticism and debate. It imposes separate **36-hour** duties to retract/correct false or misleading information publicised on their behalf and report suspected online electoral disinformation to Real411. It also specifies synthetic-content and political-advertising identification requirements. The [7 August ministerial proclamation](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/minister-velenkosini-hlabisa-gazettes-date-2026-local-government-elections-07) formally gazetted **4 November 2026** as the election date (Gazette 55162). An April date announcement was not itself formal proclamation.
2. The independent [Cape Times reporting, 10 September](https://iol.co.za/capetimes/news/2026-09-10-iec-gives-parties-36-hours-to-fix-election-disinformation--or-face-sanctions2/) corroborates those duties and describes the legal scope. **Correction:** added the missing obligations, date and endpoint inside the existing South Africa-specific explanatory section, rather than implying an unlimited internet takedown power.
3. **Separate, not successive decision paths:** A party's statutory correction/reporting duty is distinct from the published IEC–platform **non-binding** cooperation framework. A public Real411 complaint is not automatically an IFCN rating, a binding platform takedown or a court verdict. **UNVERIFIED:** signed platform annexes, case referrals, moderation notices, actual compliance outcomes, discrimination against Afrikaans commentary or any proposed six-stage command chain.
4. **Red-team caveat:** A real election-law duty is not merely voluntary; only the distinct IEC–platform *cooperation framework* is described as non-binding. The legal authority and practical enforcement must not be conflated.
