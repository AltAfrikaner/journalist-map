## Documented fact and new context
1. The Electoral Commission's [8 September statement](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local) establishes a Code gazetted **4 September 2026**, effective for the November municipal elections. It binds political parties, candidates and agents, and recognises lawful criticism, commentary, satire and reporting. Members of the public can lodge Real411 complaints; their eligibility to complain does **not** mean they are all personally bound as candidates.
2. [SAnews' 20 October primary government report](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/iec-enters-partnership-counter-disinformation) identifies IEC cooperation with Meta, Google, Microsoft, TikTok and Moxii Africa. Independent [eNCA reporting dated 19 October](https://www.enca.com/news-top-stories/iec-partners-tech-giants-fight-disinformation) described an expected signing.
