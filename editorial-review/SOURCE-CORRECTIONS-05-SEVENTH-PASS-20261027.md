## Claim-level correction
The existing Stage Four note accurately reported the [Oversight Board's 20 April 2023 policy advisory opinion, paragraph 25](https://www.oversightboard.com/decision/pao-sabu4p2s/): at that point in time, Facebook profile owners lacked in-product fact-check-label appeals; Page and group routes had device/country restrictions, while emailing fact-checkers was another option. But stopping that chronology in April 2023 risks suggesting those limitations necessarily persisted unchanged.

A [Meta-issued appeal guide dated 30 October 2023, reproduced verbatim by Public](https://www.publicnow.com/view/5F10728927D7FDD3B191217B76A9B75AFFFA8714) later described **in-product appeals as available to everyone on Facebook and Instagram**, but also described **one appeal**, encouraged submission **within seven days** (without guaranteeing handling of late submissions), stated that an appeal not processed after **14 days** would be rejected automatically, and warned that deleting a rated post would not remove the account strike. The page now places these accounts in date order rather than treating a conditional remedy as either universally absent or universally effective.

## Red-team / competing interpretation
- The Board and Meta are institutionally distinct but the Board's procedural description drew in part on Meta's submissions. These records are **not independent field tests** of access and do not establish a clear date when every profile gained appeal access.
- Meta's October article is its own policy statement; its advertised availability is **not proof of a successful South African appeal**. The Board's April restrictions are **not proof of zero appeals in October 2026**.
- A genuine case needs the original post and rating, account type, country/product/date, actual notice, available challenge channel, submission, time to decision, fact-checker response, penalties and final appeal disposition. All case-level outcomes and current South African availability remain **UNVERIFIED**.
- No new allegation of coordinated targeting or donor control is made; the investigation's existing pro-Afrikaner scrutiny of unequal access and enforcement is preserved. No right of reply requested or implied.
