## Original article problem and correction

- The previous election-mechanism paragraph referred to 19–20 October reporting and said the reviewed public reports did not provide the complete signed agreement/annexes. That formulation failed to acknowledge the later operator-hosted **public text** of the 2026 Voluntary Framework of Cooperation.
- The article now links the **actual published framework**, preserving the existing government and independent press references. The substantive additions quote the operative **clause numbers** rather than treating a press announcement as an enforcement contract.
- Clause **2.2.1**: Moxii Africa may forward a reviewed Real411 complaint to the Electoral Commission; where cause for concern exists, the IEC **may notify** a platform; the platform considers investigation/action under its own practices. That is neither an automatic IEC referral nor an automatic removal.
- Clause **3.3**: the framework is **non-legally binding**, does not constitute a contract, and creates no legal/financial obligations under its own terms. Clause **3.5** preserves each platform's independent moderation/enforcement discretion.
- The operator-posted PDF has **blank signature fields**. This does **not** prove no separately signed version exists. Its executed counterpart, annexes, actual referrals, moderation notices and case outcomes remain **UNVERIFIED**.
- The **4 September 2026 legally operative electoral Disinformation Code** is not the same instrument as the non-binding cooperation framework. The Code covers parties, candidates and those acting for them; this does not prove an ordinary Afrikaner account was penalised.
