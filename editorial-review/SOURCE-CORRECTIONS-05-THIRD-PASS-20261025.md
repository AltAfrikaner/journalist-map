## Six substantive, source-linked corrections
1. **Current job and grant recipient.** The older stage 1 conflated Stellenbosch/UCT affiliations and treated a reported **R4.5m IDRC** award as established. [CINIA staff](https://cinia.africa/about-us/) lists Herman Wasserman as Stellenbosch Journalism chair and CINIA director; [Stellenbosch University's 26 January 2026 announcement](https://www.su.ac.za/en/faculties/arts/departments/journalism/news/grant-over-r20-million-cinia) documents a *grant award exceeding R20m* for international research. It does not show funds transferred to Meta, a named fact-checker, or affected farmers. The separate R4.5m figure and quoted farm-page classification are **UNVERIFIED**.
2. **Award chronology.** [CINIA's own 25 August 2025 announcement](https://cinia.africa/major-grant-awarded-to-the-centre-for-information-integrity-in-africa-cinia-to-convene-partnerships-in-global-south/) precedes the university's January 2026 report; the latter is not the first announcement or a date of documented disbursement. Other funder relationships need grant-by-grant verification.
3. **Africa Check legal entity versus university host.** [Africa Check's chronology](https://africacheck.org/fact-checks/blog/ten-years-africa-check-brief-history) establishes the Wits hosting and 2012 launch; [its funding/governance page](https://africacheck.org/who-we-are/how-we-are-funded) identifies the 2012 UK community-interest company and South African trust control since early 2019. Housing the newsroom at Wits did not, by itself, make Wits the controlling legal entity.
4. **Gates percentage.** Removed the unattributed “26% in 2021”. Africa Check itself [reported 11% of *2019* income](https://africacheck.org/infofinder/explore-facts/bill-melinda-gates-foundation-sponsoring-africa-check) from Gates. Other historic 12%/8% funder figures cannot be projected to 2026 or combined without a common denominator. **UNVERIFIED** for current attribution and alleged editorial control.
5. **Wrong institution for $57,000.** Stage 3 placed an uncertain $57k start-up next to IFCN governance, suggesting IFCN's initial budget. [Cunliffe-Jones states](https://www.pcunliffejones.com/) that **Africa Check** began with about $57,000 in 2012; [Africa Check's dated chronology](https://africacheck.org/fact-checks/blog/ten-years-africa-check-brief-history) reports a $50,000 Google-sponsored IPI seed award. Different denominations: launch *budget* versus *prize funding*. No evidence assigns either to IFCN start-up.
6. **Measured versus imagined scale.** [Africa Check's impact page](https://africacheck.org/who-we-are/our-impact) claims over **6.8 million social-media posts labelled using its fact-checks in 2025**. This is an *organisation self-report*, not independently audited, not a count of unique users, not an Afrikaner-specific sample and not proof that Meta blocked 3 billion people. Added as a denominator-focused countercheck beside the unverified planetary figures.
