## Four substantive passage corrections

1. **Stage 4 policy error corrected.** The line claiming that all account restrictions are separate Community Standards decisions was too categorical. Meta's [September 2026 EU/EEA Code report, Commitment 31](https://disinfocode.eu/reports/meta/9/text) identifies restrictions from **repeat** False/Altered ratings: reduced recommendations and distribution, and time-limited monetisation/advertising limits. A third-party checker does not itself delete an account; Community Standards removal remains a distinct mechanism. Europe's report cannot be projected wholesale onto South Africa.
2. **Stage 4 cascading penalty assertion qualified.** Meta does not thereby say that one check disables events/groups or monetisation instantly. [Oversight Board, 20 April 2023, paragraph 29](https://www.oversightboard.com/decision/pao-sabu4p2s/) independently reports a **threshold and strikes** under the narrower historical COVID-19 rules. All named-account effects and SA thresholds remain **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **Stage 5 documented EEA scale, not an Afrikaner impact estimate.** Meta's September 2026 report gives **country-summed** 1 January–30 June 2026 figures of **14,420,053 Facebook** and **655,556 Instagram** content items viewed and treated with fact-check labels. These are NOT 14.4 million affected people, not necessarily cross-border-deduplicated, and not numbers of removals or measured reach losses. Meta warns **53 days missing data**, primarily 21 January–6 March; treat counts as lower-bound/underreported and first-party, not independently validated. Nor can one equate the EEA half-year quantities to [Africa Check's organisation-reported 6.8m labels during 2025](https://africacheck.org/who-we-are/our-impact). Up-to-95%-reach-loss remains **UNVERIFIED**.
4. **Appeal access clarified.** Added the [Oversight Board's April 2023 paragraph 25](https://www.oversightboard.com/decision/pao-sabu4p2s/) distinction: some Page and group in-product appeals, ordinary profiles lacked that pathway then, alternative email appeals; implementation of recommended independent appeals in South Africa as of 2026 is **UNVERIFIED**.

## Independent / adversarial assessment

Meta (operating-policy signatory and data originator), the separately constituted though Meta-funded Oversight Board (adversarial policy advisory), and [PolitiFact's independent partner account](https://www.politifact.com/article/2025/jan/07/meta-ending-third-party-fact-checking-partnership/) supply different institutional perspectives. [AFP reporting on the 9 September 2026 Latin American contributor pilot](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/09/10/meta-tests-fact-check-replacement-in-latin-america) corroborates the earlier geographical caution. Neither the Board nor AFP independently audits Meta's 2026 EEA numbers: only their provenance is confirmed. **Strongest criticism:** Meta itself admits consequential, repeat-offender content sanctions and geographically unequal remedies. **Strongest limitation:** a comparative policy record and aggregate totals cannot establish a specific wrongful moderation decision or ethnic discrimination.
