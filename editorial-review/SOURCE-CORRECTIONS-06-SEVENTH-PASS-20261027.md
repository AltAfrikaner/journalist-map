These are separate policies and dates, **not a single 2026 South African universal appeal rule**.

The material point is procedural fairness: even where a challenge nominally exists, practical access, timing, review independence and corrective outcome matter. Existing documented Meta downranking and repeat-offender sanctions warrant scrutiny; they do not prove that a named Afrikaner user was denied an appeal.
