# Investigation 06 — metasuppression.html: tenth-pass framework-versus-platform-power audit

**Reviewed:** 29 October 2026. **Status:** **OPEN — NOT RELEASE-CERTIFIED.** This is a source-led refinement of a mechanism hypothesis, **not** a finding of coordinated targeting.

## Correction and audit trail

- Reconciled the earlier article's "executed agreement unknown" passage with the now-public **operator-posted 2026 IEC/platform voluntary framework**. The *text* is available even though a signed counterpart, annexes, referrals and outcomes remain **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Clause 2.2.1** supplies a conditional chain: Moxii reviews a complaint, IEC may notify a platform if warranted, and the platform investigates and/or acts under its own procedures. It is not automatic enforcement.
- **Clause 3.3** expressly describes non-binding cooperation without contractual or financial obligations. **Clause 3.5** leaves moderation and enforcement with each platform, not with the IEC or Moxii. The signature fields in the published PDF are blank; this does not settle whether executed copies exist elsewhere.
- Reaffirmed that election code obligations (for parties/candidates/their agents), platform content policy, third-party fact-check ratings, Real411 reports and Electoral Court sanctions are **different legal/operational actions**. This matters when making claims about an alleged six-stage pipeline.
- The article's Afrikaner-specific targeting implication remains **UNVERIFIED** absent individual posts, notices, reach data, appeals and comparator cases; no unsupported media, graph or statistic was changed.
