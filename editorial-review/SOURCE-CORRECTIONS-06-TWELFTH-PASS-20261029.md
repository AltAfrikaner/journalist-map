# Investigation #06 — Meta suppression: twelfth-pass WhatsApp surface audit (29 October 2026)

**OPEN — NOT RELEASE-CERTIFIED.** Evidence update, not a finding of intentional Afrikaner-targeted suppression.

## Documented correction

The pre-existing six-stage diagram already notes that an automatic four-product enforcement pipeline is **UNVERIFIED**, but did not distinguish the encrypted content of WhatsApp personal/group chats from Facebook or Instagram public feed distribution. WhatsApp's own privacy explanation states that personal/group messages are end-to-end encrypted, and the service does not read their ordinary content in transit. This does not exclude user reports, metadata-based spam controls, business/AI conversations or other account restrictions. On **16 June 2025**, Meta introduced discovery and paid promotions for **Channels/Status in WhatsApp's Updates tab**, separate from private chats; the Associated Press independently reported that product distinction. It is inaccurate to treat a Facebook fact-check rating as documented access to, or direct demotion of, encrypted WhatsApp chat content.

**Finding:** A cross-platform suppression pathway, a particular South African enforcement notice, the same user's material in two services and controlled reach differences remain **UNVERIFIED**. Conversely, encryption is not proof that no account-level action ever happens. Obtain the original notice, exact product surface and applicable moderation policy rather than counting four Meta products as one tested workflow.
