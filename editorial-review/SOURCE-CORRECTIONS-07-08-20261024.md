# Editorial source corrections — investigations #07 IOL and #08 USAID
Date: 24 October 2026. Branch: `editorial/claim-audit-iol-usaid-20261024-0642`.

**Disposition:** targeted evidence-led correction only. Both investigations remain **OPEN** for full claim-level release audit. Pro-Afrikaner scrutiny of foreign funding and media power is preserved; no unsupported coordinated-control accusation is asserted as established fact.

## Investigation #07 — `iol.html`

1. **Two-stage 2013 acquisition:** The [Competition Commission's 29 July 2013 primary notice](https://www.compcom.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Commission-approves-Sekunjalo-and-INMSA-merger-subject-to-conditions2.pdf) records a first-stage Sekunjalo Media 75% / PIC 25% proposal and says a separate Chinese investors' 20% filing was still pending. The subsequent reported 55% Sekunjalo / 25% PIC / 20% China allocation appears in [amaBhungane's 2019 investigation](https://amabhungane.org/pic-wants-to-investigate-sekunjalo-directors-after-liquidation/) and [Financial Mail's August 2026 analysis](https://fm.co.za/opinion/2026-08-13-tim-cohen-the-mystery-of-the-meandering-mastheads/). The pre-existing article treated these as simultaneous, and risked treating a historical INMSA register as current IOL beneficial ownership. Corrected ownership heading, explanatory deck, route label, introduction and timeline. **UNVERIFIED:** current post-restructuring IOL register and share transfers.
2. **Share capital versus editorial decisions:** The claimed R150m contribution / R2bn purchase price (7.5%) does not substantiate “100% of editorial direction”. Replaced the universal assertion with a historical-stake qualification. Named editorial interference allegations still need unredacted instructions, contemporaneous responses and case evidence.
3. **PIC pension exposure:** R850m of historical investments and lending is not a reliable current outstanding balance. A categorical assertion of no recovery was removed; pension member interests remain pertinent, but individuals do not normally approve each portfolio holding. **UNVERIFIED:** audited current loan, equity and recovery balances.
4. **Continental exclusivity and regulatory silence:** No comprehensive African survey was supplied to support “only major media investment” by a Chinese state entity; no complete record supports “no regulator has said a word”. The South African Competition Commission actually recorded conditions for the 2013 PIC transaction, and noted a pending separate China-related filing. The United Kingdom's Ofcom action on CGTN concerns a different broadcaster and jurisdiction.
5. **Political contacts:** A South African Communist Party meeting with the CPC illustrates a political relationship, not an instruction to a particular IOL editor. The meeting remains described without claiming it demonstrates an editorial command chain.
6. **Threat/plot allegation:** Retained the reported court-filing allegation of a R250,000 murder-for-hire plot, but removed its unconditional presentation as an arranged criminal fact. A criminal judgment, prosecution and rebuttal record are **UNVERIFIED**.
7. **Evidence weighting:** Editorial panel now distinguishes judgments, inquiries, reporting and unresolved allegations. Many cards, financial figures and individual allegations still await source-level verification.
8. **Adversarial / response check:** The [Press Council's 23 October 2024 expulsion decision](https://presscouncilsa.org.za/2024/10/23/press-council-expels-independent-media-group/) and independent [Mail & Guardian reporting](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2024-10-23-press-council-expels-independent-media-for-not-complying-with-rulings/) substantiate a real accountability controversy. That decision does not prove all other allegations. The [Sekunjalo group disputes conflation of IMC and operating publishers and reports a June 2026 rectification](https://www.sekunjalo.com/sekunjalo-welcomes-rectification-of-sca-judgement-error-in-sactwu-matter-and-reaffirms-distinction-between-imc-independent-media-and-sekunjalo/); until the underlying signed corrected order is obtained, its claimed amount and the contrary original March judgment must remain distinguished.

## Investigation #08 — `usaid.html`

1. **Fact-checking and platform authority:** The old graphic said IFCN accreditation gave Africa Check a Meta moderation API and universal suppression authority. [Meta's official January 2025 policy record](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/01/meta-more-speech-fewer-mistakes/) says its historical programme applied labels and reduced distribution. [Independent fact-checking partner FactCheck.org](https://www.factcheck.org/2025/01/our-partnership-with-meta-is-ending/) says partners supplied ratings and could not remove posts themselves. Revised the six-stage explanatory text, separating accreditation, platform contract and enforcement. The 7 April 2025 change was initially **US-only**, per [Meta's dated update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/03/testing-begins-community-notes-facebook-instagram-threads/). **UNVERIFIED:** case-level South African moderation, claimed blanket WhatsApp enforcement.
2. **Donor “closed loop” versus governance:** Overlapping grants merit scrutiny but do not show that a funder unilaterally certifies its own recipient, controls Meta or prevents all independent review. Changed the absolute assertions into explicit hypotheses awaiting dated grant/board documents, ratings and platform logs.
3. **MDIF versus OSF control:** Historical MDIF majority ownership of Mail & Guardian (2017–October 2024), already reconciled in investigation #09, does not establish direct OSF majority ownership. The latter is **UNVERIFIED**. Left the historical financing question open.
4. **NED grants are not newsroom orders:** A reported Adamela Trust workplan with “monthly disinformation column” and “quarterly investigations” shows claimed deliverables, not independently established personal instructions to editors. The signed award, disbursements, newsroom correspondence and recipients' response remain outstanding. [22 U.S.C. §4411](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/22/4411) calls NED a private nonprofit, not a federal agency; government appropriations do not make NED identical to USAID or the CIA.
5. **ICA 1958 cover versus perpetual CIA chain:** Even if an individual used ICA cover before USAID's 1961 creation, the same covert method is not thereby proven to have continued or dictated journalism decades later. Replaced the categorical assertion of uninterrupted intelligence control.
6. **2025 terminations denominator:** The [22 April 2026 USAID OIG Southern Africa audit](https://oig.usaid.gov/node/8106) concerns 51 *terminated* awards, $28.7m in related assets and 30 unapproved disposal plans as of May 2025, not a census proving zero surviving/transferred awards. [Reuters' 27 July 2026 independent account](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/watchdog-finds-staffing-guidance-gaps-transfer-usaid-programs-2026-07-27/) separately reports programmes moved to State. Revised “zero active awards” to **UNVERIFIED**.
7. **3D network provenance:** The map's source paragraph incorrectly asserted *every* edge derived exclusively from primary records. Reclassified edges as investigation leads pending edge-by-edge validation. No 3D datasets or rendering scripts changed.
8. **Adversarial interpretation:** Shared donors, journalism grants and policy similarities can reflect disclosed collaboration or legitimate development assistance without establishing top-down control. This alternative does not dismiss financial dependence or moderation power; it defines the documentary threshold to prove coordination.
