## Corrections made

1. **Historical equity versus reported deal financing.** A prominent section equated **20% INMSA equity** with an unqualified **R1bn from China**. The 2013 Competition Commission announcement (reported contemporaneously by [News24](https://www.news24.com/business/independent-deal-gets-the-nod-20130815), [Mail & Guardian](https://mg.co.za/article/2013-08-16-chinese-companies-scoop-shares-in-independent-news/) and [Business Day](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/national/media/2013-08-16-second-part-of-sekunjalo-deal-approved/)) establishes the 55% Sekunjalo / 25% PIC / 20% Interacom structure. [2019 reporting of Surv&eacute;'s Mpati Commission testimony](https://www.news24.com/business/economy/independent-media-has-only-paid-interest-to-chinese-investors-not-pic-surve-20190403) attributes about **R1bn financing** to the Chinese consortium, but does not independently audit repayment or equity/loan detail. These figures measure different things. Current 2026 direct IOL ownership and individual editorial instructions remain **UNVERIFIED**.
2. **Wrong financial-institution category and timeline.** The fund was called a generic **US$10bn sovereign wealth fund** formed in 2007, misleading about size and vehicle. [China Development Bank's own history](https://www.cdb.com.cn/English/ywgl/zhjryw/zffzjjyxgs/) describes CADFund as a state-backed market-oriented investment vehicle operational in **June 2007**, initially framed at **US$5bn**, expanded to **US$10bn** in **December 2015**. [Africa-China Reporting Project](https://africachinareporting.com/chinese-media-in-south-africa-an-overview/) independently confirms the historical Interacom ownership route. US$10bn was **fund scale**, not funds paid to INMSA or IOL. No inference of newsroom instruction or exclusive Chinese media influence.
3. **Public-facing chain label.** A graphical caption read “20% of IOL” without present-day company/register support. Its explanatory label now names the historical **2013 INMSA** holding and explicitly marks present ownership/editorship as **UNVERIFIED**. The underlying graph, scripts and SVG were not edited.
