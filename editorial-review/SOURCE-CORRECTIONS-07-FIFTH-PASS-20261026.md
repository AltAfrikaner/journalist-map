## Documented corrections and provenance

1. **Sagarmatha debt-to-shares conversion was wrongly described as completed.** A PIC executive signed a *proposed* share-and-claims sale agreement in December 2017; Sagarmatha's April 2018 listing failed. In 2019 Matjila argued the intended exchange lapsed, while 2021 court papers showed the separately signed subordination agreement could still affect the PIC's legal priority. **It is not established that the PIC actually received listed shares or that the debt was discharged by a completed swap.** Corrected the PIC information panel, case card and timeline. Primary-evidence channel: [2021 investigation quoting the underlying PIC court pleadings](https://amabhungane.org/210830-dr-dan-matjilas-secret-deal-to-make-surves-r700m-debt-to-pensioners-disappear/); independently [Matjila's evidence reported by Moneyweb in 2019](https://www.moneyweb.co.za/news/south-africa/surves-debt-for-shares-plan-to-clear-independent-medias-pic-debt/) and [2019 contemporaneous analysis](https://www.news24.com/business/analysis-how-ex-pic-boss-swallowed-iqbal-surves-indy-lemon-20191129-2). **Legal enforceability and debt discharge UNVERIFIED** pending signed adjudication and audited ledgers. Note litigation of the subordination agreement is distinct from share issuance.
2. **Acquisition financing arithmetic/denominator.** [Official Parliamentary Monitoring Group transcript of the 17 March 2021 Standing Committee on Finance](https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32598/) attributes an initial **R850m** PIC exposure to the Independent Media COO, but the four itemised components (R167m equity + R133m shareholder-loan acquisition + R285m bridging + R215m consortium support) total **R800m**; the gap is **R50m**. This is **the company's testimony**, not audited PIC data. The 25% historical equity holding is separate from loans or current recovery exposure. Preserved historical bar lengths, labels and numerical data, but corrected the **text heading and accompanying explanation**: bars cannot be cited as an audited cash-flow ledger. [Independent 2026 account of the financing dispute](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/independent-medias-future-uncertain-as-sekunjalo-pulls-funding-plug/) provides the competing record. Reconciliation of the R50m, timing, borrowers and balances **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **China-Africa Development Fund misdated as part of Xi's BRI.** The original fund originated in Hu Jintao's **2006 FOCAC** policy and launched in **2007**; Belt and Road launched in **2013**. The latter cannot explain the former's establishment. Sources: [China Development Bank institutional account](https://www.cdb.com.cn/English/ywgl/zhjryw/zffzjjyxgs/) and [London School of Economics comparative historical review](https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/africaatlse/2022/02/03/what-is-focac-three-stages-the-new-china-africa-relationship-trade-economics/). This does not exclude later strategic overlap or prove editorial instructions from China.

## Red-team dissent and hold points

Sekunjalo may argue the 2017 agreements affected debts regardless of a failed listing; the PIC disputed authority and validity. **Neither position can be treated as an adjudicated final outcome on this source set.** Critics may plausibly ask about subsidy, political influence and historic foreign capital, but financing, shareholding and individual editorial instructions are different evidence classes.
