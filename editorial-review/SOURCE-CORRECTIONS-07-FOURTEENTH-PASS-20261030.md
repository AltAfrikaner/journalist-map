The masthead statistic now states **Chinese-linked 2013 INMSA stake (historic)** instead of an undated claim of Chinese ownership.
2. **The "0% Independent capital" statistic lacked any operational definition or audited calculation.** It has been replaced with **? / Current IOL beneficial ownership**, which is not determined by the 2013 transaction. This is not a denial that the historic entity had the reported investors.
3. **Separate financing denominators:** Independent Financial Mail/Currency on **25 June 2026** reported a **R400m Chinese investment for the historic equity stake**, and quoted the PIC's confirmation of a **25% interest in Independent News Media** and refusal to invest further. Separately, the existing article cites Surv&eacute;'s **2019 testimony alleging approximately R1bn total Chinese acquisition financing**. R400m equity is not necessarily contradictory with R1bn combined financing; transaction statements and the loan/equity splits are **UNVERIFIED**. The current IOL company register and any instruction to named editors are **UNVERIFIED**.
