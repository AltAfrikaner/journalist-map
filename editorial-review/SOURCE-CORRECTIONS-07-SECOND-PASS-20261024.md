## Correction: Alide Dasnois and the Cape Times
**Previous displayed claim:** The investigation stated as established fact that Alide Dasnois was dismissed *for running* the Public Protector's Sekunjalo marine-tender story, with the dismissal described as occurring the day after Mandela died.

**Evidence and change:** The Cape Times published the tender coverage alongside its 6 December 2013 Mandela wraparound. Her employment ended during 2014, not the day after Mandela died. Dasnois argued that the tender coverage was the actual reason; Independent Newspapers disputed the account and criticised her editorial handling of Mandela's death. The parties reached a **9 May 2016 Labour Court settlement**. Independent withdrew racism allegations and acknowledged her editorial prerogative, without a court finding establishing the exclusive dismissal motive. On **1 August 2016**, Press Ombud Johan Retief held that subsequent newspaper reporting omitted material settlement terms and ordered apologies. The homepage URL, article title and original visual and dataset elements are untouched.

- **Primary:** [Press Council of South Africa, *Alide Dasnois vs Independent Newspapers*, 1 August 2016](https://presscouncilsa.org.za/2016/08/01/alide-dasnois-vs-independent-newspapers/) (quotes settlement wording; ombudsman ruling).
- **Independent:** [News24, *I would not do things differently*, 9 May 2016](https://www.news24.com/SouthAfrica/News/i-would-not-do-things-differently-former-cape-times-editor-20160509); [Daily Maverick, 11 May 2016](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2016-05-11-cape-times-dasnois-settlement-no-place-for-truly-independent-editors/).
- **Counterposition:** [Independent's contemporaneous public statement](https://iol.co.za/capetimes/news/2016-05-10-independent-vindicated-in-dasnois-settlement/) advanced a different personnel/management rationale; the Ombud later found the "vindicated" headline misleading. The settlement is not a definitive judgment in either party's favour on the causal question.
- **Editorial classification:** Date, existence of settlement, withdrawn racism allegations and Press Ombud ruling are verified records. The **ultimate cause of dismissal remains contested / UNVERIFIED**. Claim removed from scandal card, not the underlying controversy.
