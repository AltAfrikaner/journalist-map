## Source-led corrections

**1. Separate the US and UK CGTN regimes.** The original [Ofcom decision of 4 February 2021](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/tv-radio-and-on-demand/broadcast-standards/ofcom-revokes-cgtn-licence-to-broadcast-in-uk) says the existing UK licence-holder, **Star China Media Limited**, lacked editorial control over the service. Ofcom *separately* refused the proposed transfer to **CGTN Corporation**, an entity it considered ultimately controlled by the Chinese Communist Party and hence legally disqualified. [Independent Euronews coverage](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2021/02/04/uk-regulator-ofcam-revokes-cgtn-s-licence-to-broadcast-in-the-uk) reports both limbs. The former page collapsed two different legal findings into one conclusion. None proves who instructed South African IOL editors or IOL's current shareholder position; **UNVERIFIED**.

**2. Identify the actual Press Council members and procedural causes.** The [original Council announcement of 23 October 2024](https://presscouncilsa.org.za/2024/10/23/press-council-expels-independent-media-group/) records re-entry effective 1 January 2024 and the group's noncompliance with Karyn Maughan and Gillian Moodie remedies. The Council rejected attempted withdrawal because its constitution required three years' notice and expressly expelled **Independent Media, Independent Online (IOL) and African Community Media**. Independent [Mail & Guardian reporting on the same date](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2024-10-23-press-council-expels-independent-media-for-not-complying-with-rulings/) corroborates these facts. The Council advises submitting new complaints about expelled titles to Independent Media, while the final treatment of pending complaints is **UNVERIFIED**. This was a failure of voluntary press self-regulation, **not** abolition of all judicial or public accountability.
