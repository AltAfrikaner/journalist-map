## Documentary correction

The previously published card asserted that the October 2022 Operation Hlanza document ordered **all IOL editors** to oppose Ramaphosa and support Dlamini-Zuma, and treated a provocative reported phrase as spoken by journalists collectively. The complete authenticated plan, all recipients, instructions and individual compliance have **not** been reproduced here.

**13 October 2022 first-party response:** Iqbal Surv&eacute; explicitly acknowledged a circulated ANC conference coverage plan attributed to The Star editor Sifiso Mahlangu while disputing that it was secret or called for overthrowing/toppling the president. This is evidence of a participant's stated position, not proof of neutrality. Independent Media's other public 13 October response also denied the contested characterisation. **16 October 2022 independent commentary:** Daily Maverick described anti-Ramaphosa language and a favourable Dlamini-Zuma reference, but acknowledged that the sensational "topple" framing stretched the evidence. The resulting card attributes these distinct records and marks a universal instruction, individual actions and the reported insult's authorship **UNVERIFIED**. Reconstruct editorial output and distribution before alleging a systematic mandatory operation.
