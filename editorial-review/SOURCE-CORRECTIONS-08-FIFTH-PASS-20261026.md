# Investigation #08 — USAID / NED and South African media: fifth source audit (26 October 2026)

**Status: OPEN. Not release-certified.** Follows fourth-pass corrections; source-led changes only to published narrative and timeline, without editing network/3D data or scripts.

## Documented corrections and provenance

1. **NED was incorrectly described as wholly FOIA-exempt.** The 1983 timeline claimed "FOIA-exempt from inception" as a continuing institutional fact, inconsistent with **Public Law 99-93 (1985)**. **22 CFR §66.1 explicitly obliges NED to comply fully with FOIA notwithstanding its private/non-agency status**; specific lawful FOIA exemptions and the dates of rules must still be distinguished. Primary record: [22 CFR §66.1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/66.1), with independent republication of government promulgated text in [2025 CFR compilation, Government Publishing Office](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title22-vol1/pdf/CFR-2025-title22-vol1-chapI-subchapG.pdf). This correction does not imply that every contract/grant must be publicly released without exemptions or administrative review.
2. **February 2021 USAID primer contradicted elsewhere on the same page.** The page's sourced context accurately described "malinformation" as genuine information misused to cause harm, but the dated 2021 timeline asserted that the published primer targeted *any* "factually correct but narratively inconvenient speech" and was "the government's manual for suppressing speech it dislikes." Replaced with the document's stated definition, acknowledgment of *advertiser outreach* and the primer's warnings on overbroad false-news prohibitions. Read the [official February 2021 USAID PDF](https://pdf.usaid.gov/pdf_docs/PA00XFKF.pdf) against an [independent 2024 policy-evidence review by Carnegie](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2024/01/countering-disinformation-effectively-an-evidence-based-policy-guide). The risk of indirect advertiser suppression **is a legitimate question**, but a government directive to suppress a specific truthful Afrikaner publication is **UNVERIFIED**; document production via a later FOIA request likewise remains UNVERIFIED pending request and response.
