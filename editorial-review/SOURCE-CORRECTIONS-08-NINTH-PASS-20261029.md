## Corrections made

1. **2023 solicitation versus confirmed payment.** The timeline's “$8M SA journalism cooperative scrubbed” asserted a completed award and shutdown based on an opportunity notice. USAID/SA funding opportunity **72067424APS00001** was a [Grants.gov-listed 13 November 2023 competition](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/350970) for **one prospective cooperative award up to US$8m over five years**, described contemporaneously by the [Global Forum for Media Development](https://gfmd.info/fundings/usaid-southern-africa-regional-investigative-journalism-activity/) and an [independent grant-notice archive](https://www.highergov.com/grant-opportunity/350970/). Solicitation ceiling is not a disbursement; winner, signed award, transfers and whether any particular award was cancelled remain **UNVERIFIED** until USAspending/agency award records or signed notices are obtained.
2. **2025 legal event.** [Executive Order 14169 of 20 January 2025](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500136/html/DCPD-202500136.htm) imposed a 90-day foreign-development-assistance pause and review, not a line-item finding that the advertised journalism programme had already paid or terminated US$8m. The separate South Africa-specific policy order and later [USAID OIG Southern Africa audit (22 April 2026)](https://oig.usaid.gov/node/8106) concern distinct regulatory actions and asset/award populations.
3. **US$28m denominator.** AIJC convenor Beauregard Tromp's reported **US$28m in regional investigative-journalism grants in process** across southern/eastern/western Africa appears in [GIJN's original 17 February 2025 report](https://gijn.org/?p=2072010) and [Stiftung Wissenschaft und Politik's independent 22 May analysis](https://www.swp-berlin.org/en/publication/mta-spotlight-54-debating-trump-20-rethinking-donor-funded-journalism). This regional report is **not** an audited account of money received by named South African journalists. It must not be confused with USAID OIG's unrelated **US$28.7m high-value assets**. [ICIJ's February 12 report](https://www.icij.org/news/2025/02/foreign-aid-freeze-decimates-investigative-news-outlets-internationally/) corroborates the >70-organisation anonymous-letter context but not individual receipt amounts.

**Red team:** The U.S. aid freeze and consequences for journalists are real. Neither a notice ceiling nor a region-wide estimate proves paid instructions, a recipient contract, stolen funds, an unbroken coordination network, or an exact US$8m termination. A dated federal award, payment/disbursement ID, stop-work letter and direct editorial evidence would materially strengthen claims.
