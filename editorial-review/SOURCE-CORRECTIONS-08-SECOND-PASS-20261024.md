# #08 USAID South Africa — second-pass correction register (24 October 2026)

**Release status: OPEN.** Corrects a misleading characterization of a US government audit and a later-programme inference. This is not evidence certification for the USAID article or its full research network.

## Correction: 1991 GAO nine-country audit
**Previous displayed claim:** The 1991 GAO report supposedly found NED programmes in nine countries (including South Africa) were a pattern of operations previously conducted covertly by the CIA.

**Primary evidence:** [US General Accounting Office (now Government Accountability Office), *Promoting Democracy: National Endowment for Democracy's Management of Grants Needs Improvement*, NSIAD-91-162, 14 March 1991](https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad-91-162). The report examined management, subrecipient compliance, audit trails, board oversight and effectiveness, using reviews in **nine** countries including South Africa; it identified control weaknesses and questionable spending. This is **not** a government finding that the grantees performed CIA covert operations.

**Independent corroboration and specificity:** [The Washington Post, *'Democracy' Grants Often Misused*, 28 March 1991](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/sports/1991/03/28/democracy-grants-often-misused/373829cf-6320-4fa1-b992-e7c8582c34a9/) reported examples of personal loans and spending contrary to grant purposes involving South African recipients and described the grant-control findings. That supports scrutiny of public accountability, not a covert-operation designation.
