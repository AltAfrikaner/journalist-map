## Corrections and provenance

1. **NED's founding mechanism and dates were conflated.** The earlier page stated that NED's 18 November 1983 incorporation "followed" the 14 January 1983 NSC directive, implying that NSDD 77 legally created NED. The [Ronald Reagan Presidential Library's NSDD index (primary presidential archive)](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/reagans/reagan-administration/nsdd-digitized-reference-copies) identifies **NSDD 77, 14 January 1983**, as *Management of Public Diplomacy Relative to National Security*. It does **not** substitute for an incorporation filing or the NED Act. [NED's institutional history (primary organisational record)](https://www.ned.org/about/history/) describes the bipartisan Democracy Program and **18 November 1983** incorporation; Congress enacted the **NED Act on 22 November 1983**. The [US General Accounting Office decision B-214585, 22 March 1985 (independent federal legal audit)](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-214585) separately confirms the incorporation and four-day-later Act. The page now distinguishes concurrent presidential public diplomacy from the private corporation's actual legal charter and congressional funding.
2. **US$18 million was misleadingly undated as initial congressional funding.** GAO B-214585 establishes an **FY1984 authorisation of US$31.3 million**, an actual **US$18 million appropriation**, and an **USIA–NED grant agreement signed 19 March 1984**. Authorisation, appropriation and delivery are different events. Updated the historical paragraph, retaining the critical discussion of the 1991 GAO review and the contemporaneous critical reporting.
3. **Founding personnel title misidentified.** Allen Weinstein was described as NED's founding director. NED's own history names **Carl Gershman as founding president**; Weinstein was a co-founder associated with the democracy-promotion initiative. Revised the attribution to "co-founder", without removing the existing 1991 Washington Post quotation.
4. **Modern newsroom control inferred from continuity and career overlap.** The legacy account presented NED's 1980s–90s programme as proven "institutional DNA" and personal moves as a documented donor-directed pattern. The NED retrospective is evidence of historical grants; it does **not** establish that a named editor's current salary or published article was under donor direction. Qualified these broad prose assertions as inference, **UNVERIFIED** pending individual appointment records, legally identified grantees, grant agreements, dates and editorial instructions. The original career cards, JavaScript graph and source references remain visible for later line-by-line audit.

This is an important distinction, not a conclusion that foreign money cannot influence topic selection.
