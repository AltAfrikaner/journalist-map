## Findings and corrections

1. **Regional mission is not the bilateral South African mission.** The [original USAID OIG audit 4-674-26-001-P, 22 April 2026, Introduction p.2 and footnote 3](https://oig.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/USAID%20Southern%20Africa%20Asset%20Disposition%20Audit%20Final%20Report%20No.%20%204-674-26-001-P.pdf) identifies **87 terminated awards** at USAID's Pretoria-based **Regional Mission for Southern Africa**, among which **51 contained high-value assets**. The regional mission administered awards across multiple countries and was *distinct* from the bilateral USAID Mission in South Africa. The previous page's 51-award summary was numerically incomplete and geographically ambiguous. Recast it to prevent treating 51 regional projects as 51 South Africa-only grants or South African newsroom receipts.
2. **Sample versus universe, approvals versus theft.** The OIG reviewed **16 purposively selected awards** and visited **seven implementation sites**; implementers reported **614 high-value assets costing US$15.1m** in that sample, with approval to dispose of **94** at the audit cutoff. The **US$28.7m** refers to assets in all **51** awards with high-value assets. The existing **30/51** disposal-plan deficiency is preserved, but the page now states explicitly that lack of approvals and asset-accounting discrepancies do **not** establish theft of the other 520 assets, theft by any recipient, or editorial manipulation. [Federal audit summary](https://oig.usaid.gov/node/8106).
3. **Independent South African country comparator is separate evidence.** [Bhekisisa's reporting carried by News24, 27 February 2025](https://www.news24.com/southafrica/news/trump-orders-immediate-end-to-usaid-funding-for-hiv-organisations-in-sa-20250227) independently reports termination notices to specific South African HIV implementers. It corroborates real country-level programme disruption but does not reclassify the regional OIG's 51 projects or prove support to any named newsroom. [Reuters, 27 July 2026](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/watchdog-finds-staffing-guidance-gaps-transfer-usaid-programs-2026-07-27/) separately documents administrative gaps in State's transfer of surviving awards; the 1,504-award population must not be added to the OIG's 51.
