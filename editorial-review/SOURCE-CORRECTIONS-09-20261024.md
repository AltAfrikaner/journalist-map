## Adversarial check and right of reply

The relevant counterinterpretation is that donor-funded journalism or training can be editorially independent. The opposing concern is that even legal, voluntarily disclosed grants can introduce agenda-setting pressure. The revised passage permits that criticism but requires direct grant text, sub-award ledgers, newsroom communications or identified production agreements before attributing particular reporting to a donor's instruction. No accusation of criminal or intelligence misconduct was newly introduced.
