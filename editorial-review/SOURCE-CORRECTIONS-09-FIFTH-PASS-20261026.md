## Red-team tests and contrary hypotheses

The same proposal could support legitimate public-interest anti-corruption reporting without donor editorial instructions. Conversely, contract deliverables or dependencies could create bias or covert pressure if documented. **Neither inference is proved** by a solicitation alone. Deadline variations between archived listings are more parsimoniously investigated as version amendments before any concealment allegation. The critical perspective is retained: ask for award IDs, contract clauses, disbursements and newsroom approval rights rather than assume them.
