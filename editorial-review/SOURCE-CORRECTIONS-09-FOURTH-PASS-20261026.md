## Claim-specific corrections

1. **1986 programme amount and Act wording.** The dossier's source card asserted an initial **$25m rising to $50m** and placed the phrase **“build a foundation for non-racial democratic majority rule”** inside quotation marks as the Act's purpose. The primary [Comprehensive Anti-Apartheid Act of 1986, Pub. L. 99-440, section 4](https://uscode.house.gov/statutes/pl/99/440.pdf) expresses a US foreign-policy purpose of ending apartheid and supporting nonracial democratic government. It does not itself establish a completed media grant or the cited growth sequence. [Jennifer Davis, Foreign Policy in Focus, 1 January 1997](https://fpif.org/south_africa/) independently analyses the historical assistance programme and describes an early approximate **$40m annual** programme. **Red team:** Davis's retrospective interpretation and totals are not audited US government outlays; the legislation is authority and policy, not a payee ledger. The precise award/disbursement series, alleged $25m/$50m transitions, recipients and programme definitions remain **UNVERIFIED**. Existing $1.2bn archival assertion remains flagged; no invented replacement amount was inserted.
2. **Patrick Gaspard career succession versus control.** [Open Society Foundations, 13 December 2017](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/newsroom/patrick-gaspard-named-president-open-society-foundations) says Gaspard was US ambassador 2013–2016 and assumed OSF presidency from January 2018; [Alliance, 21 December 2017](https://www.alliancemagazine.org/blog/patrick-gaspard-appointed-president-open-society-foundations/) independently reports the appointment. The [US State Department's archived PEPFAR organisational account](https://2017-2021.state.gov/about-us-pepfar/) names the Global AIDS Coordinator and implementing departments/agencies. A chief of mission may coordinate interagency work; that is not the same as unilaterally administering every USAID or PEPFAR contract. A Gaspard→OSF→USAID→named newsroom instruction chain is **UNVERIFIED**. The article now states the succession without insinuating an established instruction chain.
3. **Simon Allison person-level funders.** The infographic counted “three major funders” for one individual's four listed institutional positions. A funder of one institutional activity is not demonstrated to have funded a named person across jobs. Exact employment agreements, personal remuneration, commissioning instructions and four dated affiliation records are missing. The assertion is now explicitly a **research lead / UNVERIFIED**, not a numerical fact about his income.
