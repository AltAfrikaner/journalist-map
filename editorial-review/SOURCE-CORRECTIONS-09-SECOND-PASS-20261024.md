## Corrections and reasoning
1. **MDIF loan-versus-equity timeline.** The earlier ownership paragraph began at 2017. Sources: [MDIF 12 Dec 2017](https://www.mdif.org/news/mdif-majority-stake-mail-guardian/), [MDIF 30 Oct 2024](https://www.mdif.org/news/mdif-exits-south-africas-mail-guardian/), [M&G about](https://mg.co.za/about-us/); [independent contemporaneous coverage](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/us-based-mdif-exits-mail-guardian-sells-to-local-shareholders-811123a).
2. **NED/Adamela attribution.** The diagram's `NED to M&G Adamela Trust` title implied Adamela Trust was the M&G legal recipient. Corrected label to **NED to Adamela Trust / The Continent** and specified the **publisher/editorial-partner distinction**. M&G published an explicit publisher notice on 13 June 2022; The Continent identifies Adamela as its registered nonprofit publisher; Wits Journalism's 2025 State of the Newsroom describes support to Adamela. This is evidence for Adamela/The Continent support, *not* direct corporate grant payment to M&G Media Ltd or an individual journalist. The claimed **US$355,200/four 2020–21 grants** remains **UNVERIFIED** until original NED grant IDs, terms, grantee and disbursements are reconciled. Sources: [M&G 2022 notice](https://mg.co.za/2022-06-13-the-continent-issue-87-june-4-2022/), [publisher](https://www.thecontinent.org/fr/about), [Wits 2025 study source](https://journalism.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/State-of-the-Newsroom-2025.pdf).
3. **NED/CIA causal leap.** Retained the historical question and the Allen Weinstein quotation, but labelled its precise provenance **UNVERIFIED** pending the original interview. The 1983 establishment of a private nonprofit funded by Congress is not in itself evidence of CIA instructions to a South African newsroom decades later. Historical planning and a named command chain are distinct propositions.
4. **Gershman date and aggregate grant provenance.** Updated the diagram to recognise his **1984–2021 leadership** and marked its **US$4.7 million** South Africa-linked total, CIA-to-NED operational edge and denominators **UNVERIFIED** until archival records are linked. Sources: [NED biography](https://www.ned.org/experts/carl-gershman/), [NED retrospective](https://www.ned.org/carl-gershman/).
5. **No unsupported absolution.** The replacement does not assert that grant-supported reporting is inherently independent; disclosure, project deliverables, recurring relationships, reporting overlap and editorial correspondence are appropriate subjects for continued investigation.

## Red-team checklist / competing explanations
- **Foreign funding can cause incentives without written commands.** Investigate award schedules, conditions, donor reporting, disclosures, named editorial output and any conflict policies; do not infer suppression merely from money or board overlap.
- **M&G/Adamela relationship is real; publication control is a separate question.** Demand the trust deed, recipient bank/award identifiers, material governance terms and editorial signoff records before attaching an institutional payment to M&G as a company or to a particular journalist.
- **NED congressional funding does not make NED the CIA.** Establish direct instruction via dated original documentation, not quotes detached from the 1980s.
- **Open/UNVERIFIED:** grant IDs and amounts, spending/outlays, 2017–24 newsroom governance effects, NED-CIA directed publication chain, Internews worldwide/SA disaggregation, all embedded graph edges and original archival sources.
