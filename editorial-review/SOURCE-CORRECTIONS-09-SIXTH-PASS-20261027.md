## Claim-level correction — historical publisher versus current legal publisher

1. **Historical publication record:** The [Mail & Guardian issue notice, June 2022](https://mg.co.za/2022-06-13-the-continent-issue-87-june-4-2022/) described **Adamela Trust** as the publisher of The Continent, in an editorial partnership with M&G. It is evidence of a relationship *in 2022*, not proof of present ownership.
2. **Primary current account:** [The Continent's English About page](https://www.thecontinent.org/about), checked 27 October 2026, names **All Protocol Observed**, a separate nonprofit, as publisher.
3. **Independent source/contrary evidence:** [Adamela Trust's own site](https://www.adamela.org/) describes itself as The Continent's incubator and **former publisher**. The [Wits Centre for Journalism State of the Newsroom 2025, p. 32](https://journalism.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/State-of-the-Newsroom-2025.pdf) independently reports earlier NED support through Adamela to the M&G Africa desk and early Continent experiments, but it does not certify a current grant paid to All Protocol Observed.
4. **Conflict retained, not silently resolved:** [The Continent's French About page](https://www.thecontinent.org/fr/about) still calls Adamela the publisher. It appears to preserve an older attribution inconsistent with the current English site. The *effective date* of publisher transfer, any inter-entity contracts, beneficial-control relationships, current grants, disbursements and individual editorial instructions are **UNVERIFIED**; do not invent a changeover date.
