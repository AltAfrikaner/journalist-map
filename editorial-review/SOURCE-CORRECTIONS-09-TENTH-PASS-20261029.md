## Primary record, independent checks and correction

1. **USAID's programme-level influence is demonstrable without presuming control of editorials.** A [27 March 2023 announcement from Internews](https://internews.org/internews-microsoft-usaid-to-develop-media-viability-accelerator/) states the three-party MVA memorandum provided for USAID to **set and monitor high-level programme objectives**, Microsoft to provide in-kind technology and Internews to aim for **up to 500 media outlet users in six months**. [Microsoft's separate contemporary programme description](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2023/03/27/media-viability-accelerator-internews-usaid/) specifies market-data and business tools rather than story approval. [MediaPost's independent 27 March 2023 reporting](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/383770/None) confirms the programme's independently described news-business purpose.
2. **A real award announcement is not equivalent to the separate prospective South Africa award.** [Internews' 13 November 2023 update](https://internews.org/blog/mva-enters-new-phase/) describes a **three-year US$15m MVA Forward project awarded in October 2023**. [Its 26 September 2024 launch announcement](https://internews.org/internews-launches-the-media-viability-accelerator/) says **260 outlets signed up during the soft launch**. An [independent Communication Initiative programme chronology](https://www.health.comminit.com/content/media-viability-accelerator-mva) confirms the development versus public-launch dates. The **500** was a target, **260** a dated soft-launch registration total, and **US$15m** an announced programme amount, not cash already paid to 500 outlets. All are **global MVA** information, separate from USAID/Southern Africa's 2023 solicitation **72067424APS00001** for up to **US$8m**. Neither is evidence of direct payments to named Afrikaner-related publishers or covert article instructions.

**Red team / UNVERIFIED:** Executed MVA memorandum and USAID grant agreement, final obligations/outlays, signed subawards, geographic distribution, actual funds received by any particular South African publisher and any instructions governing individual articles. The described high-level objectives support legitimate donor influence scrutiny, not a finding of an enforced political line.
