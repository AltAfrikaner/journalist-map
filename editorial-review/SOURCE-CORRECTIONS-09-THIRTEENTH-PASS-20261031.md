# Investigation #09 — USAID/Internews MVA Forward: prior approval and subgrant ceilings (31 October 2026)

**OPEN — NOT RELEASE-CERTIFIED.** This is distinct from the previously reviewed USAID **Southern Africa Regional Investigative Journalism Activity** opportunity (72067424APS00001), which is not identified as an executed award.

## New direct contractual-programme evidence
- The *MVA Forward Annual Program Statement*, `MVA-APS-001`, issued **4 June 2024** and cover-updated **20 December 2024**, provides direct documentary detail beyond the high-level 2023 announcement. Page 12 planned first-round **up to 50 independent-media grants at up to $25,000 each**, **up to four media-support grants at up to $100,000 each**, and **up to five solution-provider grants at up to $50,000 each**. These are prospective ceilings/counts, not actual outlays, award recipients or South Africa-only totals.
- Its page 18 expressly says **both Internews and USAID must approve selected subgrant applicants before award**. Technical and financial reporting was expected quarterly. This substantiates recipient-selection oversight by USAID and a more concrete conditional funding mechanism than a generic donor association.
- Category A technical evaluation and the overall prior approval requirement must both be interpreted from the actual APS rather than implying that the agency approved individual stories. The statement explicitly does not promise to make awards. Its amended 2024 version preceded the 2025 US foreign-aid cancellations; **post-2025 implementation/outlays remain UNVERIFIED**.

## Adversarial test
**Blue team:** An actual subgrant solicitation expressly requiring USAID co-approval of recipients is stronger evidence of programme-level influence than a simple list of donor names. It warrants examination of final award instruments and applicant screening criteria. **Red team:** An invitation, steering role, grant approval power or prospective grant ceiling is not proof of a particular payment to a South African journalist, control of article wording or a coordinated media campaign. Final recipients, selection decisions, disbursements, geographic breakdown and editor communications remain **UNVERIFIED**.
