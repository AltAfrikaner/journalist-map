## Evidence-led correction
- The original **13 November 2023** Southern Africa Regional Investigative Journalism Activity solicitation, opportunity **72067424APS00001**, described an anticipated **one cooperative award, up to US$8 million over five years**, and priority SADC countries **Angola, Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia and South Africa**. The announced country-selection process was to be negotiated through *co-creation* between a successful applicant and USAID. These details describe **planned programme design and intended federal involvement**, not an actual grant already made or a South African newsroom's article instructions.
- The [Global Forum for Media Development reproduction of the USAID notice](https://gfmd.info/fundings/usaid-southern-africa-regional-investigative-journalism-activity/) and a separate [Grants.gov-origin opportunity listing](https://www.highergov.com/grant-opportunity/350970/) support the solicitation chronology, with earlier differing deadline versions already distinguished in the article. The primary **[2 CFR 200.1](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2026-title2-vol01/xhtml/CFR-2026-title2-vol01-part200-subpartA-subjectgroup-id314.html)** establishes that a cooperative agreement anticipates substantial agency involvement **in the activity**, not necessarily line-editing stories; the [US Department of Energy's instrument explanation](https://science.osti.gov/grants/About/Grants-Contracts-Differences) independently clarifies programme versus procurement oversight.
- **UNVERIFIED:** whether that particular opportunity became a signed award, the recipient and all subrecipients, any country-specific workplans, obligation versus outlay, and a donor's instruction to a named journalist. Actual award and payment records, agreed substantial-involvement clauses, newsroom contracts and original editorial communications are required.

## Red / blue test
**Blue:** A donor's published plan to co-select priority countries for investigative-journalism capacity-building is a legitimate subject of editorial independence and foreign influence scrutiny. **Red:** Priority-country programme participation and a government-driven grant category cannot substitute for a signed award, media ownership or journalist payment evidence; publication topic similarity is not proof of instructions.
