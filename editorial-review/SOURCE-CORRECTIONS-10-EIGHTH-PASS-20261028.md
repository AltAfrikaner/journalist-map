## Core factual discrepancy: current 51% Irancell partner is not consistently named

The original lead and founder cards stated without qualification that **Iran Electronic Development Company (IEDC)** presently held the Iranian 51% majority in MTN-Irancell, with Bonyad and Sairan as indirect owners. Yet an independent June 2025 report gives **Kowsar Sign Paniz (KSP)** as the 51% joint-venture holder. These cannot be assumed to be identical names. They may designate separate dates, registered entities or ownership tiers. A current official register extract, dated transfer records and ultimate-beneficial-ownership chain have not been inspected.

Source comparison:

1. **Primary corporate account:** [MTN Group, 18 July 2025](https://www.mtn.com/mtn-takes-note-of-media-reporting-on-groups-interest-in-iran/) confirms its **49% stake**, no operational control, and its own position on sanctions/exiting, **without naming** the 51% legal partner in that release.
2. **Independent contemporaneous reporting:** [The Citizen, 17 June 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/mtn-group-downplays-iran-strife/) identifies the partner as **Kowsar Sign Paniz**.
3. **A conflicting independent later account:** [Daily Maverick, 4 March 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-03-04-analysis-mtns-iran-headache-just-got-worse-as-sanctions-strikes-tighten-noose/) identifies **IEDC** as the 51% holding vehicle and names Bonyad and Sairan as its purported underlying owners.
4. **Further cross-check:** [Iran International, 6 September 2025](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202509068304) uses a third name, **Gostaresh Electronic Sina**, for the majority owner. This reinforces why a single unqualified definitive company-name assertion is inappropriate.
