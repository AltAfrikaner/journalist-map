## Claim-level findings, denials and revisions

1. **Critical 2004 / 2005 conflation.** Previous article said MTN signed a memorandum promising “heaven, earth and fish” during Defence Minister Lekota's **August 2004** Iran visit. Former executive Chris Kilowan's witness account, examined in Reuters's **18 June 2012** investigation, dates the ministerial visit to **August 2004** but describes a **separate alleged September 2005 Letter Agreement** with Iran-side shareholders. Reuters quotes reported wording about “defensive, security and political” cooperation. In the 2023 US federal *Zobay* opinion, the letter is an exhibit incorporated in plaintiffs' allegations, with competing legal readings, **not adjudicated proof that arms were promised, procured or delivered**. We corrected the dated introduction and evidentiary heading. Lekota denied a licence role; a contemporaneous bilateral *government* agreement cannot be silently substituted for an MTN arms-sales contract.
2. **“The Fish” arms list.** The list is **alleged by Turkcell/Kilowan**, not a proven delivery manifest. Reuters reports Kilowan distinguished introductions to suppliers from promises to sell, and states South Africa did not supply the weapons. Denel's former manager described independent exploratory aviation work and official blocking of proposed export. Whether supplier payments, export licences, a procurement contract or subsequent prohibited deliveries existed remains **UNVERIFIED**. Original diagram and item entries preserved; an evidence-status note was added alongside them.
3. **Phuthuma Nhleko biography.** Replaced an established-fact claim that he personally signed a *military* pact or directed arms transfers with attributed Kilowan testimony, court-recorded disputed document and Nhleko's explicit denial of influencing government decisions. The JSE's official **9 October 2025** release documents Nhleko's chairmanship; a past MTN executive's present exchange governance role alone is not proof of unlawful conduct.
4. **Irene Charnley purported fax.** Previous article incorrectly said she faxed **Denel's CEO**. Reuters examined the contested **November 2004 fax addressed to Sairan's head**, allegedly on Charnley's behalf and said to be signed by Nkateko Nyoka. **Both Charnley and Nyoka disputed their involvement/knowledge**. Authenticity, actual Denel contact and the article's unsourced personal wealth figure are **UNVERIFIED**.
5. **Thabo Mbeki / Rouhani date and inferred quid pro quo.** Reuters reports Kilowan's allegation MTN helped pay for Hassan Rouhani to meet Mbeki in **late 2004**, not a confirmed 2005 meeting. Mbeki's office declined comment. No authenticated meeting record establishes an arms/nuclear request or a quid pro quo on an IAEA vote. **UNVERIFIED**, not a proven government action.
6. **Afghanistan and Turkcell case summaries.** Explicitly describe protection payments, nocturnal tower shutdowns and bribery as **plaintiffs' allegations**. The existence of the pleaded cases and procedural decisions is factual; payments, operational consequences, corrupt causation and civil liability remain **UNVERIFIED** at merits level.
7. **Homepage #10.** Removed unqualified description of the “heaven, earth and fish” memorandum as authenticated, already proven parliamentary evidence; replaced with a sourced/contested summary.

## Red-team and unresolved contrary evidence

Reuters' interviews create **substantial questions** about how MTN representatives facilitated access to defence officials, the alleged 2005 clause, executive conduct and disclosure. The reported denials and Denel account mean the strongest allegations cannot be stated as findings; nor do denials by themselves establish innocence. The US court held certain *pleaded* inferences sufficient to survive dismissal; that is not a trial verdict. The 2004 government visit and the 2005 alleged company letter should be independently authenticated and compared with the litigants' exhibits. Documented company ownership does not establish military-intelligence transfer.
