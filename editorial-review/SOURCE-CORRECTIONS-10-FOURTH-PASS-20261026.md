## Claim-specific corrections

1. **Shanduka's Mauritius vehicle is not proof of an illicit shell.** [BusinessTech, 28 November 2012](https://businesstech.co.za/news/mobile/27444/ramaphosas-shanduka-claims-stake-in-mtn/) records that Shanduka paid **US$335m** for a **minority MTN Nigeria stake** through the wholly owned Shanduka Telecommunications (Mauritius), purchasing from three outside investors. [Reuters reproduced by Moneyweb, 28 November 2012](https://www.moneyweb.co.za/archive/shanduka-buys-mtn-nigeria-stake/) independently describes the transaction. A Mauritian incorporated subsidiary and its commercial purchase do not establish a personal Ramaphosa beneficial holding at that subsidiary, Iranian sanctions evasion, or an unlawful channel. The card and matching Mauritius-shell caption have been qualified.
2. **Governance concerns had an on-record response.** [Bruce Whitfield, News24, 7 December 2012](https://www.news24.com/business/cloud-over-mtnshanduka-deal-20121207) raised director-conflict questions and quotes MTN rejecting speculative conflict commentary. [TimesLIVE, 14 December 2012](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2012-12-14-shanduka-deal-was-above-board---md/) records Shanduka's position that Ramaphosa disclosed his interests and recused himself from relevant discussions. These are rebuttals, **not independently authenticated board minutes**. The dossier now includes opposing accounts and says director minutes and actual beneficial ownership remain **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **Party to ICJ filing.** The [ICJ's own 29 December 2023 announcement](https://www.icj-cij.org/index.php/node/203395) identifies the **Republic of South Africa** as the applicant, not Ramaphosa filing personally. The card has been corrected; political leadership is distinguishable from legal party and agent. Separately, allegations connected to a later US DOJ inquiry are not proof Ramaphosa personally knew of a crime.
4. **Irish trademark plaintiff's €4bn is a demand, not a judgment.** [Digital Business Africa, 24 January 2026](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/en/the-irish-company-ac-shining-stars-initiates-legal-proceedings-against-mtn-group-and-seeks-cfa-2600-billion-in-damages-for-the-unauthorized-use-of-the-mobile-money-trademark/) reports AC Shining Stars' **21 January announcement** and examination of a WIPO international mark registration. [OSIRIS, 24 January 2026](https://osiris.sn/la-societe-irlandaise-ac-shining-stars-engage-une-action-judiciaire-contre-mtn.html) separately reports the claimant's demand. Importantly the listed **14 jurisdictions include France**, so the former “14 African countries” count was false. Registration alone cannot prove each national claim, infringement, MTN's knowledge, financial concealment or damages. Exact pleadings, competent court, MTN response and outcome **UNVERIFIED**. The unsupported CEO-concealment sentence was removed.
5. **Iranian middlemen / sanctions-evasion graph.** A static box enumerated five parties as “documented” and described an established sanctions-evasion procurement mechanism. Their precise corporate positions, procurement orders, export licences and sanctions designations were not substantiated at individual-edge level. Re-labelled **five named entities, functions UNVERIFIED**, and specified the records needed to establish an unlawful transaction. This does not erase the allegations or any public record that may later substantiate them.
6. **Historical conflict-market footprint is not five active MTN operators.** [MTN's 4 March 2026 Syria agreement statement](https://www.mtn.com/mtn-group-syria-formalise-agreement-to-regularise-mtns-exit/) distinguishes Syria's pending handover, Afghanistan exit in **2024** and Yemen exit in **2021**; [Syria's national news agency, 4 March 2026](https://sana.sy/en/syria/2300443/) independently records the transitional operation. Corrected the “five operating companies” heading without erasing the five jurisdictions from the historical map.
