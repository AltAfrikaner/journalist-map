## Correcting an internationally misleading legal category

The Nasirzadeh card's hover text described a single 'Foreign Terrorist Organisation' designation by the US, EU, Canada and Australia. Legal status and dates are materially different. The EU [formally designated the IRGC on 19 February 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/02/19/eu-terrorist-list-council-designates-the-islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps-as-a-terrorist-organisation/), after the **29 January 2026 political agreement**; [Canada listed it as a terrorist entity under its Criminal Code on 19 June 2024](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-safety-canada/news/2024/06/government-of-canada-lists-the-irgc-as-a-terrorist-entity.html); Australia implemented a *distinct state sponsor of terrorism* designation effective [28 November 2025](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Joint/Intelligence_and_Security/IRGC2025/Report/Chapter_1_-_Listing_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps). Its ministers publicly announced the move on 27 November. [Reuters 19 June 2024](https://www.reuters.com/world/canada-preparing-list-irans-irgc-terrorist-group-says-cbc-2024-06-19/) independently contextualises Canada's action. The relevant tooltip and prominent sourced paragraph now use each actual legal category.

The previous article asserted identical US/EU/UN sanctions without establishing a UN listing matching the exact legal entity. Its specific UN-list claim is now **UNVERIFIED**, not asserted as fact. Likewise original 2025 AI partnership instruments, Nasirzadeh's purported direction of SPND and specified weapon/surveillance supply to named IRGC units remain **UNVERIFIED** without primary contracts, appointments and exports.

The user's investigative point, that Iranian state-linked telecom, defence and financial interests warrant scrutiny, remains clear. However, an IRGC legal designation is not proof that MTN executives arranged a military transaction, that IEI directly controls Irancell or that subscriber data fed a specific attack.
