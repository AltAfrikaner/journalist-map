## Legal and chronology corrections
1. **September 2005 Letter Agreement:** The original prose presented a signed military-security commitment as established. It is identified instead as **Turkcell's 2012 litigant allegation**. The [South African Supreme Court of Appeal judgment, 29 April 2025, *East Asian Consortium v MTN*, [2025] ZASCA 50](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZASCA/2025/50.html) describes defendants' explanation of a letter agreement about Irancell share acquisition/funding. The court did not authenticate the separately alleged military undertaking or determine MTN's liability for bribery. These are different evidentiary uses of the word "letter agreement".
2. **Quotation provenance:** Retained the alleged security/political-cooperation wording but qualified the caption: filings are allegations, and 2012 reporting about Kilowan's documents did **not** amount to adjudication. Source: [Mail & Guardian, 30 March 2012](https://mg.co.za/business/2012-03-30-mtn-s-cash-weapons-and-diplomatic-ties-in-iran/); [2012 reporting of testimony](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/ict/mtns-iran-accusations-20120618). **UNVERIFIED:** original signed exhibit, authenticity and whether terms were implemented.
3. **Litigant euphemism and shareholder knowledge:** Removed unsupported assertion that 'security' *necessarily* meant attacks on Americans and that management concealed the document from all shareholders until 2012. The complaint's interpretation, corporate disclosure record, and state of knowledge are separate **UNVERIFIED** propositions. [MTN's July 2025 formal statement](https://www.mtn.com/mtn-takes-note-of-media-reporting-on-groups-interest-in-iran/) denies operational control of Irancell; that denial is a corporate position, not a merits judgment.
4. **Military cooperation versus telecoms letter:** Recast categorical "this was not commercial" conclusion as a serious disputed question requiring the actual instrument, decision chain, performance evidence and proceedings.

Both are **UNVERIFIED** without agreements and logs. Verify 2026 registered holding chain, weapon provenance and operational access separately. Sources: [US Treasury OFAC, 18 Oct 2023](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20231018_33); [independent TechCentral Iran analysis, 2026](https://techcentral.co.za/iran-war-hits-home-for-mtn/284724/).
8. **Alleged unified IRGC command:** The named commander's exact position, claimed January 2026 authority over all Irancell facilities and data, the ~4% connectivity measurement and matching of outage to specific strikes remain **UNVERIFIED**. Demand appointment, operating authority and timestamped independent network telemetry.
9. **Huawei 'kill switch':** No primary contract here substantiates a Huawei-built $700m–$1bn project at a named facility. Distinguish Iranian censorship technology reporting from a verified system and procurement chain; number and operational attribution **UNVERIFIED**.
10. **2013 offshore corporate structure:** Disclosure of subsidiaries in MTN's Note 47 is not by itself proof of purposeful secrecy, criminality or illicit procurement. This motive remains **UNVERIFIED** without transaction-level evidence.
