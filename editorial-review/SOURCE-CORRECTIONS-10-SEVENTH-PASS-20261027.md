The [Chand primary pleading, printed pp. 130–131 (PDF pp. 140–141)](https://terrorismcase.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/0097.-08-12-2025-AMENDED-COMPLAINT-against-All-Defendants-with-Jury-Demand-filed-by-ALL-PLAINTIFFS-Chand.pdf) alleges more than US$1bn was funnelled to IRGC/Hezbollah-linked interests between 2006 and 2012. These are **different categories and currencies**; the complaint also uses venture revenue and inferred onward flow. It has *not* independently established that those sums were all direct, paid transfers to designated armed groups. Named bank outflows, onward recipients, timing, intent and alleged use for violence are **UNVERIFIED**. MTN disputes the allegations.
