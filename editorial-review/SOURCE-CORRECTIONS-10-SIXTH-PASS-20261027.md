1. **Primary MTN report:** MTN's [H1 2026 interim financial results](https://www.mtn-investor.com/reporting/interims-2026/results-overview.php) report **317.7 million total subscribers at 30 June 2026**, up 6.7%. Its [financial statements' reporting notes](https://www.mtn-investor.com/reporting/interims-2026/index.php) distinguish geographically clustered African operating businesses from equity-accounted joint ventures, including Iran.
2. **Independent report with historical comparator:** [Reuters, 17 November 2025](https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/south-africa-telco-mtns-service-revenue-rises-26-nigeria-ghana-growth-2025-11-17/), reports **301 million customers across 16 African markets** during the 9 months ended September 2025. A count of African markets cannot include Iran, and a 2025 count cannot substitute for the latest reported H1 2026 total.
3. **Editorial action:** Replaced the inaccurate, undated assertion “16 countries across Africa and the Middle East, over 300m users” with dated figures; explicitly marked Iran as a **separately reported 49% equity-accounted joint venture**. The apparent global “sixth-largest” rank was not supported by a common comparator denominator and is now **UNVERIFIED**.
4. **Red team:** An equity-accounted interest remains financially and politically relevant; the correction does not deny the joint venture or settle allegations over sanctions, telecom surveillance or historical licences. Subscriber counts do not prove the number of people monitored. Do not attribute military use of customer data from mere corporate affiliation.
