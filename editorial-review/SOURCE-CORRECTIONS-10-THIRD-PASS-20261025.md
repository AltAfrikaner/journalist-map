## Individual corrections and provenance

1. **Rooivalk / Apache false platform claim:** The article called the South African Rooivalk 'based on' the **US Apache**. This is technically false. It derives from South African helicopter development around **Puma/Oryx** dynamics and local programmes. Corrected the record against the [South African Air Force's aircraft history](https://www.saairforce.co.za/the-airforce/aircraft/57/rooivalk) and [independent Times Aerospace engineering chronology](https://www.timesaerospace.aero/sandbox/features/defence/alliance-plots-the-new-route-for-rooivalk). That correction preserves the significance of a possible illicit arms procurement while excluding a false US-origin platform assertion.
2. **Charnley / Denel fax:** [News24's 2012 testimony and interview report](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/ict/mtns-iran-accusations-20120618) describes a purported fax as a **litigant-produced, contested document**; Irene Charnley denied sending/authorising it, and another named executive disputed knowledge. The article previously asserted she faxed Denel as confirmed fact. Receipt, authenticity, instructions and any shipment remain **UNVERIFIED**. A complaint is not a judgment.
3. **G5 / US components:** No specific original export/licensing provenance here establishes American parts or a completed transfer. Kept historic South African artillery background; identified alleged procurement separately from supply. **UNVERIFIED** component/transfers.
4. **Casspir attribution:** The broad 'Reutech-developed carriers' wording conflated independent South African defence-company histories. The **Casspir** is rooted in **CSIR/Mechem** development and different manufacturers. Sources: [Mechem design authority's interview](https://defenceweb.co.za/land/land-land/mechem-returns-to-its-roots/) and [Engineering News 2016 production overview](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/casspir-vehicle-shedding-its-apartheid-links-as-it-takes-on-peacekeeping-role-2016-04-08). Any Iran procurement chain remains **UNVERIFIED**.
5. **Pilot display/chips:** Recast unsupported identification of a Western Cape supplier and personal instructions by Iran's First Vice President as **litigant allegations needing the original instrument**; explanatory avionics background is not evidence of a shipment.
6. **Press coverage != independent confirmation:** Replaced the equipment-list footnote's claim that Reuters, News24 and others 'confirmed' the entirety of pleaded military supply claims. Reporting confirms **that allegations and denials were made**; it does not authenticate every exhibit. [News24 testimony/denials](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/ict/mtns-iran-accusations-20120618).
7. **New South African Constitutional Court appellate chronology:** [Primary court listing](https://collections.concourt.org.za/items/fddc72ce-da2b-4990-bee1-8a541cd69633/full) dates proceedings **31 August–1 September 2026** in CCT 119/25, 121/25 and 124/25, after the [29 April 2025 SCA decision](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZASCA/2025/50.html).
