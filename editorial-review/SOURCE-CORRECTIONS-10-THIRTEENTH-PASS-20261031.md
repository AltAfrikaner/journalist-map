## Claim-level correction
- **Primary audited document:** [MTN Group 2025 annual financial statements, note 9.2, printed page 114](https://www.mtn.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/MTN-Group-FY-25-Annual-Financial-Statements.pdf) describes an obligation to offer 21% of Irancell to members of the Iranian public and says that a proportionate issue **could** reduce MTN's 49% holding by 10.3 percentage points to 38.7%. It describes continued regulatory engagement; **it does not record completion of that offering**. The disclosure is repeated in [MTN's earlier 2023 financial statements](https://www.mtn.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/MTN-FY-23-Annual-Financial-Statements.pdf), which makes it an enduring unresolved stated contingency, not proof of a new 2026 transfer.
- **Contemporary cross-check:** [MTN's 30 June 2026 interim financial statement notes](https://www.mtn-investor.com/reporting/interims-2026/notes-to-the-condensed-consolidated-interim-financial-statements.php) still discuss the 49% Irancell stake. Independently, [Reuters on 24 August 2026](https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/south-africa-telco-mtns-half-year-adjusted-profit-rises-213-2026-08-24/) reports a 49% stake and the R3.9bn impairment. A reported stake as of a date is **not** a dated Iranian register of the other holder or proof of how the public-offer obligation will be executed.
- **Corrected inference:** 21% is a **potential public float**, not 21 additional percentage points to add to an already fully allocated 49%/51% capitalisation. The resulting 38.7% is a **conditional mathematical scenario, not MTN's current actual shareholding**. Direct Iranian holder(s), regulatory waivers/deferrals, any offer dates, purchasers, pre-emption rights and ultimate beneficial owners remain **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Source independence:** MTN's 2023/2025/2026 filings are three dates but **one company's records**. Reuters supplies independent coverage of the 2026 percentage, not independent authentication of the 21% contractual clause. Obtain Iranian regulator/licence and current share-register records before certifying execution status.
