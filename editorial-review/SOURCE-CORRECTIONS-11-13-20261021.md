### #13 — `communists.html`: primary ideology versus global diffusion

## Red-team challenge and unresolved evidence
A close chronology and a real contact network can support a serious influence hypothesis, but not automatically the stronger proposition that all later actors obeyed one command chain. The most damaging alternative explanation is **convergence or independent adoption** driven by widely shared social-policy ideas and known pre-1994 precedents. Confirming any claimed directed export requires originals identifying the transmitter, recipient, date, adopted policy wording and subsequent action; test opposing statements and missing links. For named people, distinguish affiliation from conduct and allegations from judgments.
