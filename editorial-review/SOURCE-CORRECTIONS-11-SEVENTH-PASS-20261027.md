## Exact-file corrections
1. **1913 Land Act denominator and Smuts attribution:** Replaced the misleading statement that the Act "stripped Black South Africans of 93% of the land" as if it were an individual ownership measurement. It restricted acquisition/leases outside designated areas encompassing roughly 7% of national territory, subsequently expanded to about 13% under 1936 legislation. Many people were dispossessed or affected, but 93% is not itself a recipient-by-recipient confiscation inventory. Smuts served in the Union government; his specific personal authorship of the 1913 statute remains **UNVERIFIED**. His influential draft of the 1945 UN Charter preamble is distinct from the negotiated final text.
2. **25 March 2026 UN declaration:** Original 123–3–52 vote confirmed, with an additional **15 states not voting**. Clarified that A/RES/80/250 is a non-binding General Assembly declaration, not a criminal judgment, enforceable reparations order or legal ruling on every other slavery system. Reported objections and supporters' positions separately.
