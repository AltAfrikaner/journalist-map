## Adversarial interpretation
A revised WDI index does **not** demonstrate that post-1994 redistribution achieved its stated goals, just as the old unsourced 0.63→0.67 comparison did not demonstrate it failed. Other income-based/consumption-based Gini estimates can differ; reconcile country-year, household unit, survey series and methodology. The 1986 Act's broader assistance and sanctions provisions are compatible with funding to some civil-society actors, but do not by themselves prove a **US$25m direct COSATU grant** or an ideological command chain. The article's political thesis is editorial analysis, not an authenticated record of donor instructions.
