## Claim-level corrections

1. **Australia / false TRC attribution.** The country card asserted that National Sorry Day was a South African TRC template, and that the Voice referendum and Welcome to Country had identifiable South African/ubuntu origins. The Australian Human Rights Commission's own chronology records the first National Sorry Day on **26 May 1998**, following the country's **1997 *Bringing Them Home*** inquiry. The National Museum of Australia's institutional history independently traces the Stolen Generations inquiry. This is evidence for an Australian historical chronology, **not** proof that South Africa was irrelevant to every later Australian reconciliation debate. The claimed direct transfers are **UNVERIFIED**, not check-marked exports; an invented 10–15-year diffusion lag was removed.
2. **UN/ICJ/IFCN conflation.** The UN card formerly called IFCN a South-African-origin UN-related global standard and asserted an ANC-pioneered ICJ mechanism beginning in 1994. Poynter's **21 October 2015 launch notice**, corroborated by independent TechCrunch coverage of its 2017 Google partnership, places IFCN at **Poynter in the United States**; IFCN is not a UN agency. The ICJ's **29 December 2023 original filing notice** establishes the date of South Africa's identified Gaza genocide-convention application. No evidence cited demonstrates a continuous directed 1994–2026 UN/IFCN command chain; this remains **UNVERIFIED**. An opinion about the politicisation of litigation is a separate thesis, not a proved chronological origin.
3. **Comparative constitutional claim.** A matrix cell said “Kenya 2010 cites Klare” as though the constitution itself made a citation. It now distinguishes a 2010 constitutional text from later academic/judicial interpretation, with each claimed recipient borrowing **UNVERIFIED** pending a dated judgment or policy source.
4. **Funded-media table.** The comparison table presented an unproven OSF 2012 PIJ strategy, an “identical model globally” and a USAID **US$8m** opportunity as completed command architecture. It now states that the OSF primary text and directed editorial instructions are **UNVERIFIED**; Africa Check launched in 2012; the 2023 USAID figure is an **up-to** opportunity ceiling rather than a documented disbursement.
