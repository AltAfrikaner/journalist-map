# Motsepe — eighth-pass seller chronology and source-conflict review

**27 October 2026 | Homepage local investigation #12 | OPEN, not release-certified**

## Exact article corrections
1. The 1997 mine-shaft purchase had repeatedly been titled an **AngloGold** transaction. AngloGold's own corporate timeline and public issuer filing date its formation to **June 1998** through consolidation of Anglo American Corporation gold holdings. Corrected the timeline, financial-flow source attribution and Bobby Godsell biography to identify the pre-1998 **Anglo American gold interests**. Calling the successor company AngloGold retrospectively can be convenient, but treating it as the contemporaneous legal seller is anachronistic.
2. Reconciled incompatible reported dollar prices. **Forbes, March 2008**, based on interviews including Motsepe/Godsell, reports **US$8.2 million** and future-profit repayment. **Die Welt, June 2015** independently reports **US$7.7 million**. Both are now transparently attributed. Without the 1997 contracts, lender/guarantee and FX records, the precise contractual price and personal risk remain **UNVERIFIED**.
3. Removed any implication that the **2002 Mining Charter** was already adopted in 1997. The chronology is relevant to an Afrikaner-focused assessment of corporate/empowerment policy; a later regulatory charter is not proof of contemporaneous coordination.
