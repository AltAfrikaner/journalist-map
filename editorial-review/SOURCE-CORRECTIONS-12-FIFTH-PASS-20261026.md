## Editorial corrections made to `motsepe.html`
1. **1994 mining charter anachronism.** The 1994 career timeline claimed that black-ownership **26% mining legislation** had begun and the Mining Charter was already in design. The sectoral Mining Charter with **15% in five years and 26% in ten years** was agreed in **October 2002**, not a licence rule in 1994. The new mineral-rights framework became effective in **May 2004**. Replaced with dated corporate disclosure and contemporary independent reporting. Updated the BEE glossary and 1997 deal entry, explicitly **UNVERIFIED** as to alleged advance knowledge of the later charter or a personally coordinated financing scheme.
2. **Sundowns ownership 2003 versus 2004.** The 2003 entry compressed the takeover into a single purchase. Official club history records **51% acquired in 2003** and ownership rising to **100% in 2004**. Contemporary **News24 of 11 May 2004** independently describes Motsepe buying the Tsichlas family's remaining **49%**. The overlap of corporate and sports appointments does not alone prove a preplanned route to CAF leadership.
3. **CAF governance allegations.** The timeline's categorical “Infantino pressured rivals” and personal installation language was qualified as **reported allegations**, subject to testimony, interested-party responses and voting records. The page still records the contested election, subsequent appointment and Motsepe's supportive statements. **UNVERIFIED:** personal direction of 54 association votes and any finding of fraud by the named officials.
4. **Poll denominator.** The 33.1% number remains displayed as a **reported poll** with its sample/question and methodology explicitly **UNVERIFIED**, rather than an official leadership vote.

## Red-team challenge
Financial access, political family relationships, public board roles and football influence are legitimate investigative topics, particularly if publicly sponsored transactions warrant scrutiny. They do **not** establish foreknowledge of future legislative text, corrupt regulatory capture, bribery in a CAF election or a predetermined ANC succession outcome. The timeline must not substitute suggestive chronology for documented terms, payments and instructions. Sources include company records and contemporary reporting but do not supply original 1997 vendor-financing contracts, 2026 PM27 invoices, survey microdata or sworn FIFA/CAF records. Opposing and interested-party responses must be recorded.
