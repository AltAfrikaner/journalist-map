## Verified chronology and correction

The personnel-network card had asserted **Jeff Radebe personally drafted the Integrated Resource Plan** while signing IPP contracts. Original records distinguish separate events:

- **4 April 2018:** Department of Energy announced completion of the signing of **27 REIPPPP agreements** (bid windows 3.5/4), with **R55.92 billion** in *proposed investment*, not a measured investment disbursement. Reuters independently reported the same transaction and its previous delays.
- **22 / 27 August 2018:** Cabinet authorised publication of the **draft 2018 IRP** for public comment on 22 August; Radebe released/briefed it on 27 August. A minister presenting a departmental draft is not evidence of his personal authorship of the model or individual clauses.
- **18 October 2019:** **Gwede Mantashe**, not Radebe, announced Cabinet approval and gazetting of the *final 2019 IRP*. Parliamentary records establish consultation and departmental authorship.

**Primary records:** [4 April 2018 energy ministry signing notice](https://www.gov.za/news/media-advisories/government-activities/minister-jeff-radebe-signs-27-renewable-energy-0), [24 August 2018 ministry consultation briefing notice](https://www.gov.za/news/media-advisories/media-briefings/minister-jeff-radebe-briefs-media-integrated-resources-plan), [18 October 2019 final IRP ministerial address](https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minister-gwede-mantashe-approval-integrated-resource-plan-2019-18-oct-2019).

**Independent corroboration:** [Reuters, 4 April 2018, on signed power contracts](https://www.moneyweb.co.za/news/south-africa/south-africa-signs-first-of-r55-92bn-renewable-energy-deals/); [Mining Weekly, 27 August 2018, draft IRP release](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/no-new-nuclear-in-irp-update-as-time-horizon-is-trimmed-to-2030-2018-08-27).
