**Release status: OPEN, not evidence-certified.** The original page's assertion that default judgment had been granted was contradicted by a published court judgment. The claim was corrected in the visible legal case card and the card status, not buried in a footnote.

## Correction record

1. **Default judgment:** Before: `Default judgment granted` after December 2023 nonappearance. After: the published Johannesburg High Court decision of 14 April 2026 reports a Tanzanian **application** for default judgment against ARM, Motsepe and ARCH *pending*, while ARC defended; this is not an award or finding of wrongdoing.
2. **South African ruling:** The 14 April 2026 judgment gave ARC declaratory relief because ARC was **not a signatory** of the 25 October 2019 Pula Group–ARM confidentiality agreement. It neither decided the merits against ARM nor found Motsepe committed a breach. Pula sought leave to appeal (granted 22 May), then reportedly abandoned the SA appeal in July.
3. **October Tanzanian disposition:** Bloomberg/Moneyweb 14 October 2026 reports a **9 October** procedural strike-out of the $195m claim after an earlier August ruling about absent necessary parties. The court awarded **no damages** and did not find a breach. Pula filed notice of appeal and may refile, so total case closure and future appellate outcome remain **UNVERIFIED**. ARM publicly welcomed the outcome, disputing Pula's allegations.
4. **Card badge / introduction:** Replaced misleading 'ACTIVE' with 'STRUCK OUT | $195M CLAIM' and clarified that other matters listed are allegations, not findings.
