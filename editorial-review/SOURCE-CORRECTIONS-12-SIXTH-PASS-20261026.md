## Adversarial interpretation
A favourable public poll does not make an ANC leadership candidacy inevitable or prove political lobbying financed by the WEF, FIFA or mining capital. Conversely the existence of a public denial does not prove that all independent campaigners were uninvolved. A bloc may often vote similarly without being under one individual's formal control. Documentary scrutiny should ask who financed actual campaign material, who set poll questions, what federation delegates voted, and what records support each claim.
