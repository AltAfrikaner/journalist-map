## Claim-level corrections

1. **Biography versus independently verified authorship.** The old family-network card stated as settled fact that Bridgette Motsepe-Radebe personally designed the charter and related laws. IWF South Africa's published biography says she was “instrumental in the lobbying and design” in her SAMDA role. That is **an attributed institutional/biographical claim**, not signed records establishing which provisions she drafted, who approved them, or that any particular Motsepe transaction was arranged by her. The on-page biography and Chain A IF clause now explicitly distinguish these levels. **UNVERIFIED:** exact role in individual clauses, conflicts, authorisations and special benefits.
2. **The 1997 acquisition cannot have been caused by the October 2002 charter.** The on-page Chain A AND clause formerly said Patrice Motsepe's gold-shaft purchase was *specifically enabled* by the later **26%** Mining Charter target. Motsepe's reported initial six-shaft acquisition was in **1997**, as described in Forbes' retrospective reporting. Anglo American's **10 October 2002** original statement and independent *The Independent* account set out negotiated **15% within five years /26% within ten** sector targets. An original Gold Fields annual filing at the SEC describes **1 May 2004** as the charter's effective date for calculating those targets. This cannot retroactively cause the 1997 purchase. Later contractual incentives, any preferential treatment and detailed beneficial ownership remain separate **UNVERIFIED** questions.
