## Documentary finding

The 1969 ANC Morogoro *Strategy and Tactics* discusses the contemporary national-liberation struggle in an explicitly international socialist setting, economic emancipation and the political leadership of armed struggle. It does **not** supply a dated post-1994 two-stage socialist-conversion timetable. A previous timeline panel compressed those distinct propositions into a claim that Morogoro itself prescribed 'national liberation first, socialist transformation second.' The relevant **SACP's 1989** *Path to Power* programme more expressly describes the national democratic revolution as the route towards socialism and ultimately communism **while stating that this further transition was not guaranteed**. Independent scholarship places the Morogoro text in the ANC's strategic turn to armed and political struggle.

Changed the **visible 1969 timeline explanation**, with direct links to the original texts and scholarly interpretation. This preserves evidence of actual ANC–SACP ideological overlap without pretending an SACP 1989 programme was an enacted 1969 ANC timetable. Alleged later foreign policy-export instructions remain **UNVERIFIED**.
