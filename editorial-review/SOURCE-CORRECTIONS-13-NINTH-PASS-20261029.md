## Verified correction: South Africa's 1994 flag

- Earlier headline metric **“3 ANC-associated colours”** gave a count without the essential attribution caveat. It now identifies **six national-flag colours**, with no single official meaning assigned to each. Black, green and gold are associated with the ANC, but this association is not exclusive authorship.
- **Primary eyewitness record:** F. G. Brownell, *The National Flag of South Africa: Evolution of the Final Design*, [Federation Internationale des Associations Vexillologiques, pp. 3–4](https://fiav.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/ICV16-Brownell.pdf), recounts the technical committee's March 1994 process and traces green to the **Transvaal Vierkleur (1857)**, black to **Land of Goshen** and gold to **Stellaland** designs in the 1880s. These precede the ANC. His account acknowledges party influences in the design process, not unilateral party authorship.
- **Independent official record:** [South African government's flag explanation](https://www.gov.za/about-sa/national-flag-0) confirms designer, first use **27 April 1994**, and that individual colours/sets have no universally assigned symbolism; convergence is the stated interpretation of the central shape.
- The assertion that a slogan or symbolic resemblance alone demonstrates overseas DEI-policy transmission is classified **UNVERIFIED** until country-specific proposals, decision-makers, correspondence and policy-adoption dates are traced. Existing comparative criticism remains.
