## Changes to published investigation

1. **1928 Comintern chronology (direct documentary fact).** Replaced the timeline's implication of a combined US–South Africa experimental instruction with its distinct country documents. The 1928 *South African Question* expressly calls for the Communist Party of South Africa to participate in the ANC **while retaining party independence**. The resolution also recognised the CPSA's opposition to the native-republic slogan. A 1928 South African delegate publicly disputed that formulation. Both the existence of the instruction and evidence of internal dissent belong in the same history.
2. **Mechanism paragraph (editorial inference).** Previously stated that SACP had "perfected" a transferable, freestanding Western policy mechanism. Recast as a theory requiring a dated chain of organisation-to-organisation transmission; not a finding based only on ideological similarities.
3. **ANC leadership denominator and unattributed quotation (UNVERIFIED).** Previously asserted that about half the ANC NEC were dual SACP members *by 1990* and attributed "should lead, but not be seen to lead" directly to the party. Without a dated NEC roster, named SACP membership records and an identifiable first-hand original for the quotation, neither the exact ratio nor verbatim attribution is certified. Documented overlap is distinct from quantified, covert control.
4. **Global replication claim (UNVERIFIED).** Preserved the critical hypothesis while specifying the missing evidentiary bridge to BLM, Western institutions and recent moderation decisions.

## Sources and source independence

- **1928 primary policy record:** Executive Committee of the Communist International, [Resolution on 'The South African Question'](https://omalley.nelsonmandela.org/index.php/site/q/03lv01538/04lv01600/05lv01603/06lv01605.htm), hosted by the Nelson Mandela Foundation / O'Malley archive. It calls for participation in the ANC but continued party independence; it discusses **internal CPSA opposition** to Moscow's native-republic instruction.
- **1928 counter-voice / primary:** [S. P. Bunting's statement at the Sixth Comintern Congress, 20 August 1928](https://sahistory.org.za/archive/document-26-s-p-bunting-statement-kuusinen-thesis1-presented-sixth-comintern-congress-20), sourced from Allison Drew's documentary history. Shows the policy's contested character rather than unanimous South African acceptance.
- **Independent scholarly analysis:** Sheridan Johns, [*The Comintern, South Africa and the Black Diaspora*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/review-of-politics/article/abs/comintern-south-africa-and-the-black-diaspora/23AE1795FC4FEEE14219B36D3ACED6BE), *Review of Politics* (published 5 August 2009). The contemporary party was surprised by Moscow's late-1927 policy shift; its adoption was disputed.
- **Alternate published transcription:** [1928 South African Communist Party document](https://www.marxists.org/history/international/comintern/sections/sacp/1928/comintern.htm), not an additional independent witness because it reproduces substantially the same primary resolution.

## Red-team challenge and gaps

The Comintern's preference for organisational influence is a real historical fact; it does **not** supply proof of an uninterrupted, centrally directed international command chain through 2013–2026. Competing evidence would include ANC independent policy development, local electoral/constitutional drivers, social movement convergence and earlier international antecedents. Obtain NEC rosters and membership records for the asserted year; identify the original "lead but not be seen" quotation; trace each purported export edge to dated documents and reciprocal source confirmations.
