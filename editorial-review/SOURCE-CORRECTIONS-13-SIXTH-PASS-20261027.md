## Claim reviewed and corrections

1. **BRICS/New Development Bank purpose (primary treaty).** The article twice stated that South Africa established a BRICS bank *to circumvent* Western financial institutions, and linked this conclusively to a 1969 revolutionary blueprint. Article 2 of the [15 July 2014 NDB Articles of Agreement published by Brazil's Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br/canais_atendimento/imprensa/notas-a-imprensa/atos-assinados-por-ocasiao-da-vi-cupula-do-brics-fortaleza-15-de-julho-de-2014) states a mandate to finance infrastructure and sustainable development **complementing** existing multilateral and regional institutions. The five founding states included South Africa. This is an explicit treaty-purpose statement, **not** evidence that all founders had no competing geopolitical objectives.
2. **Independent geopolitical characterisation.** [Reuters, 8 October 2024](https://www.reuters.com/world/uk/uk-brings-brics-banks-former-cfo-head-development-bank-2024-10-08/), described the NDB as an *alternative* to Washington-based lenders. This interpretation can be reported, but it is not the same as a treaty clause demanding circumvention or evidence of SACP instructions. The 2026 chronology and the BRICS 'Counter-Architecture' research paragraph now distinguish charter, interpretation and alleged transnational direction.
3. **Causation:** Direct documented fact = founding NDB treaty and membership; attributed interpretation = alternative lender; editorial hypothesis = a continuous 1969 ANC/SACP policy-export or covert banking instruction chain. **UNVERIFIED:** internal SACP/CPC/ANC banking directives, BRICS negotiation records showing such a mandate, decision-makers and traceable downstream transactions. No allegations of individual misconduct are inferred from treaty participation.

## Adversarial test

One could criticise BRICS as geopolitical competition without rewriting a financing treaty as a secret instruction manual. Conversely, the treaty's complementarity language does not rule out a separate ambition to reduce dollar dependence. To prove the more specific thesis requires dated party and bank governance documents linking the 1969 programme to named 2014–26 decisions. A policy interpretation is not an operational command-chain finding.
