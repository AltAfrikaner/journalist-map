## Documentary correction

The prominent 39% 'wastewater plants critical' figure was from the **2022 Green Drop report**, not the latest full audit. The Department of Water and Sanitation's **2025 Green Drop report**, released **31 March 2026** and auditing the **2023/24 municipal financial year**, found **396 of 848 wastewater treatment systems (47%)** critical, compared with **334 of 850 systems (39%) in 2022**. A system count must not be presented as the number of municipalities or water service authorities. The report's different audited totals (848 versus 850) matter for denominator comparison. The declining good/excellent share (118/850 or 14% to 66/848 or 8%) independently corroborates substantial deterioration but was not added as a new dashboard datapoint.

The visible **dashboard**, **2025–26 infrastructure timeline**, **source-wall explanatory card** and **bibliography** were corrected to label period and system denominator and cite current official and independent records. Existing **2023 47.4% non-revenue water** is kept separate from wastewater-system condition; it is not all physical leakage. The previous statements that **90/144 authorities discharge raw sewage**, **31 million litres/day enter Vaal Dam**, and **42/56 criminal enforcement cases** are now expressly **UNVERIFIED** where their evidentiary basis is missing, rather than asserted in the most visible dashboard.
