## Verified correction: 2025 city homicide ranking

- The page stated **four** South African cities in the global top fifty, undated and ambiguously as “most dangerous”. The original [Consejo Ciudadano para la Seguridad Publica y la Justicia Penal's **11 February 2026** report, pp. 4–5](https://mexico.quadratin.com.mx/www/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Boletin-ranking-2025-de-las-50-ciudades-mas-violentas-del-mundo-v3.pdf) actually lists **six South African cities in its 2025 edition**, ranked by **homicides per 100,000 people**: Mandela Bay #3 (119.81), Cape Town #15 (68.77), Durban #19 (57.35), Buffalo #22 (51.99), Johannesburg #31 (39.55), Pietermaritzburg #45 (30.77).
- [UnoTV's independent Spanish-language 11 February 2026 report](https://www.unotv.com/nacional/ranking-2025-coloca-a-17-ciudades-de-mexico-entre-las-mas-violentas-del-mundo/) reproduces the **six** city count and category. The report's ranking is an NGO sample subject to city size, reporting and boundary definitions, **not** an official world ranking of all crime or a comparison of total city safety.
- Corrected both the main dashboard and the evidence-card statement, rather than leaving contradictory totals.
