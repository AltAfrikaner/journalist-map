## Five specific corrections

1. **Dommisse 1986 (attribution).** The 1986 peer-reviewed article covers social and psychological effects of apartheid including malnutrition, migrant-labour family separation, mental health provision and detainee torture/suicide. Its abstract does not establish the website's separate "world's highest murder rate" superlative; that claim was removed from the attributed summary and flagged **UNVERIFIED**.
2. **Stunting 2008 (wrong denominator).** The 2024 longitudinal article estimates a **34% modelled population-attributable proportion** of stunting associated with selected maternal/socioeconomic factors in 2008. It does not report a 34% prevalence. The observed prevalence in this study was **25%** in 2008.
3. **Stunting 2017 (reversed trend).** The same paper estimates a **65% attributable fraction** in 2017, but its measured stunting prevalence actually **declined from 25% in 2008 to 23% in 2017**. The unqualified "biological damage is accelerating" claim and misleading timeline heading were corrected; a modelled risk contribution is not a count of stunted children.
4. **Marikana forensic specificity.** Wording "most shot in the back" generalised evidence from the second scene to all 34 killed. Independent forensic reporting attributed back wounds to **11 of 17 people killed at scene two**, not 18+ of all 34 victims. The corrected text keeps both the scale of the killings and precise limits of the evidence.
5. **Farlam Commission versus Ramaphosa's rhetoric.** The article grammatically attributed a call to treat the wage dispute as a criminal matter to the Commission. The record instead distinguishes Ramaphosa's communications from the Commission's findings; the Commission criticised Lonmin and police, and did not find Ramaphosa caused the shooting. Any unqualified present-day conviction/status assertion requires docket checks.

- **Original epidemiological analysis:** [*Preventing Stunting in South African Children Under 5*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10935-024-00766-2), *Journal of Prevention*, published 28 February 2024. Explicitly distinguishes modelled population-attributable risk 34%→65% from actual study prevalence 25%→23% during 2008–2017.
- **Independent methodological cross-check:** [Sartorius et al., Oxford-hosted BMJ Open study](https://www.tropicalmedicine.ox.ac.uk/publication/1160715) finds a separate 11.0%→7.6% series for 2008–2017, **not directly interchangeable** with the 2024 paper's prevalence estimates because sampling and operational definitions require reconciliation. No synthetic pooled trend has been asserted.
- **Official Marikana inquiry summary (primary):** [25 June 2015 government release of the Farlam Commission findings](https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/president-jacob-zuma-release-marikana-report-25-jun-2015). Explicitly distinguishes the Commission's findings concerning Lonmin, policing, Ramaphosa and other parties.
- **Independent contemporaneous chronology:** [Mail & Guardian, 12 August 2015](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2015-08-12-remembering-marikana-three-years-on/), confirms 34 police-shooting deaths, prior strike violence and R12,500 wage demand.
- **Independent scene-two forensic analysis:** [Niren Tolsi and Paul Botes, reproduced by South African History Online](https://sahistory.org.za/archive/marikana-blame-game-special-report-niren-tolsi-and-paul-botes), 11 of 17 scene-two victims shot in the back, reported from inquiry evidence.

## Red-team method and remaining claims

Serious documented governance harm merits scrutiny, but a retrospective trend, modelled attributable fraction or editorial analogy cannot isolate the causal effect of a government without research design and matched controls. The 2024 paper's modelling is not itself a trend in observed physical stunting. Likewise, assigning criminal responsibility demands specific findings and court outcomes rather than a list of political associations. The published embedded figures, Marikana case-by-case injury/death tallies, causation and control groups, social welfare measures, water and crime denominators and alleged cross-platform moderation network remain **UNVERIFIED** or incompletely reconciled.
