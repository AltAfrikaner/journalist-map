## Correction: Cuba-related expenditure was not a harmonised R3.5–4bn 'transfer' finding

The resource-allocation card previously displayed **R3.5–4 billion Cuba Transfers**, described **seven channels** as documented and characterised **R1.7 billion** as the cost of Project Thusano. It conflated **reported programme cost** with a subset classified as **irregular expenditure** and combined loans, technical services, medical salary arrangements and aid with incompatible period, recipient and valuation denominators.

- **Official parliamentary source (31 October 2025):** [National Assembly Announcements, Tablings and Committee Reports, pp. 67 and 70](https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Docs/atc/01ls62wgh52kroy7j4czdzxpitjqd7y7vo.pdf), a defence committee report dated 30 October: **more than R1.5bn** unresolved irregular Thusano spending, and specific 7 October 2025 findings against the successor Project Kgala (missing market and cost-benefit analysis, insufficient delegation and procurement justification). It recommended possible termination and further status reporting; an **audit irregularity is not, without a criminal finding, proof of theft**.
- **Separately attributed 2026 figure:** [DA statement, 15 October 2026](https://www-origin.da.org.za/2026/10/da-action-finally-ends-anc-cuban-project-kgala-obsession) attributes to March 2025 Defence testimony about **R3.04bn decade-long Thusano cost**, **R1.699bn irregular Thusano spending** and **R278.6m irregular successor Kgala**. [Independent specialist defenceWeb reporting, 16 October 2026](https://defenceweb.co.za/sa-defence-sa-defence/ag-orders-cuban-sa-military-co-operation-project-to-end/) relays the parliamentary statement and an instruction to stop Kgala, with current courses to finish. These outlets discuss the same disclosed evidence, **not two independent audits or proof of completed closure**.
- **Displayed correction:** Changed the headline card to **R3.04B / Project Thusano: reported decade cost**, separated the irregular subset, identified parliamentary primary findings and distinct successor programme, removed the unsupported combined 'seven channels' certainty, and clearly labelled the prior R3.5–4bn aggregate plus IDC loans/salary estimates **UNVERIFIED as one non-overlapping disbursement total**. Original traceable claims remain described as allegations for further checking, not silently erased.
