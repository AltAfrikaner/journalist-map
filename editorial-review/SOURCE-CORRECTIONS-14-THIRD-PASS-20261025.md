# #14 — ANC liberation: third-pass source-correction register (25 October 2026)

**File:** `ancliberation.html`. Targeted factual and red-team corrections; **full evidence certification OPEN**.

1. **Financial-year child homicide:** The website attributed **1,100 children murdered in 2024/25** to SAPS in two prominent locations while a timeline marked the count unresolved. The original [SAPS 2024/25 Annual Report, page 165](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202511/south-african-police-service-annual-report-2024-2025.pdf) gives **1,065**, versus **1,164 in 2023/24**. Independent [Mail & Guardian contemporaneous report](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2025-06-11-reported-cases-of-child-abuse-and-neglect-rose-to-26-000-in-2024-25/) described "about 1,100" for 2023/24, which should not become a precise 2024/25 number. Preserved distinct calendar-2024 overall murder total and made reporting periods explicit.
2. **Non-revenue water ≠ physical leakage:** Corrected statements that **47% of drinking water was lost before reaching households**. DWS 2023 national **47.4% non-revenue water** combines real/physical losses, apparent commercial losses and authorised but unbilled consumption. Original [DWS No Drop definitions](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/minister-senzo-mchunu-release-blue-drop-watch-report-no-drop-watch-report-and); independent [Daily Maverick water-accounting coverage](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-04-06-billions-down-the-sa-big-city-revenue-drain-as-40-of-purified-water-is-lost-to-pipe-leaks/). The interpretation is material, even though the underlying state water crisis remains important.
3. **R400bn water backlog:** Recast this as DWS's proposed capital rehabilitation/upgrade need for **105** worst-rated authorities in late-2025 reporting, not one year's maintenance spending. Independent [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/failing-water-systems-will-cost-r400-billion-to-fix-says-minister/) and [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-02-01-r400bn-price-tag-to-fix-failing-municipal-water-services-in-sa-says-government/) cover the answer to Parliament. A later October 2026 figure of **107/144** with "critical or recurrent water failures" [reported by EWN](https://www.ewn.co.za/water-department-estimates-r400-billion-needed-to-upgrade-infrastructure-in-sa-s-worst-affected-municipalities/) uses a different definition; neither should be treated as proof of a two-unit trend.
4. **Enforcement/budget totals:** The legacy **R90bn required/R17bn spent annually**, **56 municipalities charged**, **42 water authorities facing criminal charges**, and other water-facility percentages lacked common-year source denominators or criminal dockets. Narrowed assertions and explicitly marked **UNVERIFIED** for the exact values pending records.
5. **Life Esidimeni attribution:** Preserved the documented **144 deaths after state-directed transfers**, but replaced categorical "killed by policy" with more careful wording distinguishing deaths, systemic findings and individual criminal culpability.
