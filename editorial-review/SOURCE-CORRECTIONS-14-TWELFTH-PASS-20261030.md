## Substantive new evidence

South Africa's **22 May 2026** official presentation of the 2025/26 financial-year fourth-quarter SAPS figures reports **5,181 recorded murders during 1 January–31 March 2026** against **5,727 in January–March 2025**: **546 fewer, -9.5%**. Independent *The Citizen* reporting corroborates the same dated quarter and counts. This is a genuine measured improvement in a still-serious homicide burden, distinct from the site's historical **26,232 / roughly 72 per day in calendar 2024** comparison.
