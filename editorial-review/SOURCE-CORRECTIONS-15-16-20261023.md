## 16: `un.html` — The Selective Conscience

**Material corrections and reasons (14 targeted changes):**
1. Hero/title, dashboard and timeline described **31 years of UN silence**, **zero objections**, and **no monitoring body**. This was false: CERD reviewed South Africa during its 111th session in **November 2023**, and the concluding observations `CERD/C/ZAF/CO/9-11` are dated **27 Dec 2023**. Revised to distinguish *absence of apartheid-era sanctions machinery* from active modern treaty review.
2. Corrected the 1973 Apartheid Convention's legal predicate: criminal apartheid refers to **specified inhuman acts and intent to establish/maintain racial-group domination**; mere recording of race categories is not the complete offence. Distinguish it from the **ICERD special-measures exception** and the South African Constitution's **s 9(2)** redress permission. Neither text settles whether a specific modern measure is appropriate.
3. Original headline statistic **16.3 million “total unemployed”** contradicted the Q4 2024 Stats SA count. Changed to **8.0 million officially unemployed, Q4 2024**. Official statistics count approximately **16.5 million not economically active**; conflating these groups is a category error, not an alternative unemployment methodology.
4. Replaced an unsupported 2003-to-2024 change in total unemployed and proof-of-BEE-causation claim with a transparent methodological requirement: same rate definition, quarter, population denominator, labour participation and causal identification.
5. IRR `324 / 122+ / 145` race-legislation counts are **attributed to the IRR's own methodology**, not presented as UN counts. Article still needs full source roster, amendment/repeal handling and overlapping-definition reconciliation.
6. Added an accessible primary-source note preserving the critique of UN institutional selectivity while presenting relevant monitoring and law.
