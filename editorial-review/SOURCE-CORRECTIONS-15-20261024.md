Legal authority/2021 CSIR status remains **UNVERIFIED**. **Primary:** <https://www.up.ac.za/node/64175> and <https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/deputy-minister-nomalungelo-gina-launch-south-african-centre-industry-and>. **Independent direct statement:** <https://www.dsti.gov.za/media/news/4816-sa-world-economic-forum-launch-centre-for-industry-and-technology>.
