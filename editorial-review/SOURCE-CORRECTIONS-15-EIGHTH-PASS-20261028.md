## Exact material corrections

1. The published heading, infographic number **“.5T”** and explanatory card lacked both the leading digit and the currency. Corrected to the independently reported **estimated US$2.5 trillion** ore reserves, referencing **Wits University (30 March 2026)** and **CSIS (2026)**, both of which identify 16 globally high-ranking mineral commodities. This is a broad resource estimate, **not a cash deposit, grant, WEF payment or audited recoverable balance**. Geological and commodity-price assumptions remain **UNVERIFIED**.
2. The August 2025 WEF report was mis-titled and presented as creating South African legal rules. The report is **Securing Minerals for the Energy Transition: Finance for Southern Africa**, published **29 August 2025**, developed by the WEF with **DBSA**, with **McKinsey as knowledge partner**. Corrected the visible funding card and “What WEF Designs” text to distinguish the report's investment barriers, infrastructure, value-chain and financing topics from binding rules. The original exact six-item “regulatory framework” taxonomy remains **UNVERIFIED** as a direct report quotation.
3. A graphic declared Patrice Motsepe a primary beneficiary of a WEF-controlled transition without a public procurement or directive chain. Revised the commercial conclusion: AREP publicly announced a **40% SOLA investment in 2020**; **Business Day, 12 May 2026**, independently reports ARE raising its stake **from 41% to 83%**. This is an important ownership interest requiring conflict scrutiny, **not proof of preferential award, illicit benefit or WEF command**. Those causal allegations remain **UNVERIFIED**.
