## Claim-level corrections
1. **1992 Davos is not a demonstrated instant ANC nationalisation reversal.** The timeline asserted that Mandela abandoned the ANC's 30-year nationalisation programme in Davos. The ANC's own May 1992 *Ready to Govern* text, adopted months after the meeting, still allowed nationalisation among possible ways to expand the public sector while envisaging a significant private sector. The event and the subsequent policy debate are documented; single-meeting finality and WEF instruction are **UNVERIFIED**. Replaced the timeline's categorical title and narrative, without deleting the original historical account attributed to Tito Mboweni in the expanded timeline.
2. **Provenance and chronology.** A WEF-published **2015 retrospective by banker Neal Chapman** recounts Li Peng's encounter; an organisational retrospective is evidence of its author's interpretation, not a contemporaneous transcript proving the ANC enacted a private deal. The biography and source register no longer upgrade the anecdote to a demonstrated completed reversal. The 2013 Schwab retrospective and contemporary Mandela speech already in this dossier remain separate from the 2015 Chapman account.
3. **Unverified individual control.** The Schwab biography no longer treats asserted continuous annual private access or broad adoption of his books in binding South African statutes as established facts. The books and dates remain; directives, draft provenance and the precise statutory links still require primary records. The political question of privileged access is preserved.
