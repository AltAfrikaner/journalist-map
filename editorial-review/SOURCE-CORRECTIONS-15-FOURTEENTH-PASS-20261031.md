- **WEF earlier first-forty-years book, Africa milestones p.100:** **more than 200** African and multinational companies signed a NEPAD-support declaration at the June 2002 Durban meeting. The book has a 2009 copyright and covers 1971–2010; it is WEF self-reporting, not an independent audit. https://www3.weforum.org/docs/WEF_First40Years_Book_2010.pdf
