## Corrections, reason and uncertainty

1. **JETP commitment lost its leading digits.** The timeline rendered the 2021 launch as ".5B" and ".5 billion". Restored **US$8.5B** as the *initial international financing pledge*, not a payment. The Presidency's 2021 COP26 declaration identifies the partner governments and the justice/worker component. The later US$12.8B pledge figure in March 2025 is a separate dated denominator; neither is a WEF disbursement.
2. **Coal employment was converted into a claimed layoff schedule.** A 2024 independent policy brief reports approximately **90,977 coal workers in 2022**. The 90,000+ context card now identifies this as an employment baseline, not 90,000 confirmed losses. Bloomberg's 2023 account of Krutham's model separately reported **48,500 projected direct jobs by 2030**, potentially **120,000 including suppliers** under scenario assumptions. World Bank's Komati plan provides transfers/retraining options. Actual community harm deserves strong scrutiny, but modelled risk is not observed dismissal.
3. **Unsourced nine-by-2035 closure and universal financing conditions.** Removed certainty that precisely nine coal power plants must close by 2035 or that all financing automatically depends on WEF-shaped NDC targets. These remain **UNVERIFIED** pending dated plant plans and signed agreements. The individual 26%/29%/45% survey claims remain **UNVERIFIED** pending sample, questionnaire and dates.
4. **Wrong trustee office.** The old text called Christine Lagarde IMF Managing Director in the WEF's 2024 roster. ECB has placed her in its presidency since **1 November 2019**. WEF's 2024–25 annual report lists **Lagarde as ECB President** and **Kristalina Georgieva as IMF Managing Director**. Corrected the distinction and limited any conclusion about legislative direction.
5. **Broken funding-graphic string.** The Netherlands row showed literal `/bin/sh.9B`. No reliable grant amount can be reconstructed from that string; its displayed label now reads **UNVERIFIED**. Other original bilateral bars and figures remain preserved as archival graphics and are **not** certified.
