## Corrected inherited numerical assertions

1. **Platinum group metals:** The dossier stated **88% of the world's platinum reserves**. USGS *Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026* instead lists **63,000 tonnes of South African PGM reserves** and more than **76,000 tonnes worldwide**. Summing the listed five country entries gives **76,200 tonnes**, South Africa **~82.7%** (display rounded to **~83%**). The USGS indicator is for **platinum-group metals**, not exclusively platinum metal nor annual production. Both the top KPI, glossary and mineral card are corrected. The detailed tabulation is independently reproduced by Ore Daily; this is **source reproduction, not an independent geological estimate**.
2. **Manganese:** An inherited mineral card reported **80% of known global manganese reserves**. USGS 2026 reports **550 million tonnes of South African manganese reserves / 1.8 billion tonnes worldwide** (about **30.6%, rounded to ~31%**). The USGS separately estimates around **70% of world manganese resources** in South Africa. Reuters reported an **80% resource** share in its 22 June 2026 Exxaro/rail story. The differing **resource** claims require their own methodological reconciliation; neither may be substituted for the narrower **reserve** statistic. The visible percentage and explanatory text are now corrected.
3. The pro-Afrikaner argument that mineral concentration and politically connected commercial interests deserve scrutiny remains. Neither reserve share, family tie nor international climate-programme vocabulary by itself proves WEF control of mineral licences, financing terms or procurement. Those edges remain **UNVERIFIED**.
