## Material corrections (before → after)

1. **Jan Smuts' role in the UN Charter Preamble:** The former heading and claim treated him as exclusive author and uniquely the only person to sign two treaties. UN archives say Smuts was *largely responsible* for the drafting, while San Francisco delegates revised the final wording. Kept his documented role and both-signature fact; removed unjustified exclusivity. **Primary:** <https://archives.un.org/en/content/united-nations-70-aspirations-actions> and official <https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text>.

2. **Universal absence of UN resolutions:** The page asserted 'UN issues 0 resolutions' on post-1994 laws without defining resolution type, search method or time window. That categorical absence is **UNVERIFIED**. It is analytically distinct from **CERD's completed 2016 and December 2023 treaty scrutiny**, which need not be equated with Security Council sanctions. **Primary:** 2023 concluding observations CERD/C/ZAF/CO/9-11 <https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4031173>; UN Geneva session summary <https://www.ungeneva.org/en/news-media/meeting-summary/2023/12/committee-elimination-racial-discrimination-closes-one-hundred>; independent report mirror <https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2102671.html>.

3. **Employment Equity Act classification and sanctions:** Distinguished race-classified designated-employer reporting (generally 50+ employees) from direct revival of the Population Registration Act's individual classification scheme. Clarified up-to-10%-turnover fines apply to **specified contraventions**, while 2025 regulations permit **reasonable grounds** for missing sector targets. These legal distinctions do not make criticism of the effects of group-based requirements illegitimate. **Primary:** 15 April 2025 SA Department of Employment and Labour regulations <https://www.labour.gov.za/Media-Desk/Media-Statements/Pages/Publication-of-the-two-sets-of-Employment-Equity-Regulations-following-the-commencement-of-the-EE-Amendment-Act%2C-no--4-of-2.aspx>, and statutory Gazette regulations. **Independent challenge/report:** Reuters 6 May 2025 <https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/south-africas-da-party-challenges-new-racial-equity-law-court-2025-05-06/>.

4. **2023 CERD findings:** Corrected the assertion that there was no formal finding or concluding observation; CERD published concluding observations **27 December 2023**. The narrower question of whether the recommendations adequately addressed the SAHRC's concerns is still under investigation. CERD observations are neither a domestic court judgment nor proof all relevant criticism was answered.
