## Verified corrections
1. **1974 General Assembly mechanism.** The original headline implied formal UN-membership suspension and declared unquestioned Charter compliance of a credentials workaround. In 1974 South African credentials were rejected, preventing its delegation from participating in General Assembly work, **not** expelling it or suspending membership under Charter Article 5. The UN General Assembly called on the Security Council to review the relationship (A/RES/3207). The legality was contested: the UK Government told the Commons on 27 November 1974 that the procedure violated the Charter. This is evidence of a historical constitutional dispute, not proof either legal side prevailed.
2. **Post-1994 monitoring.** Blanket references to a void of UN scrutiny and a proven collapse of institutional incentives contradict published CERD reviews in 2016 and 2023. Institutional capture remains **UNVERIFIED** absent named decisions, recusal records or excluded submissions. The more precise question is whether concrete racial-classification, employment and procurement complaints were adequately examined.
3. **UNDP category error.** UNDP reports beginning South African operations in 1994; UNDP is a programme, and “South Africa gained UNDP membership in 1994” is not a substitute for country-office history. The claimed January 1998 Executive Board seat is **UNVERIFIED** here pending an official contemporary roster. A coincident 1998 Employment Equity Act is not evidence of UNDP direction.
4. **Pandor:** CERD considers the state's measures rather than prosecuting ministers as individuals; the implication that race laws were never internationally reviewed is false. Contemporary 22 Nov 2025 reporting quotes Pandor stating an **existing US visa was revoked** after leaving ministerial office. The US disclosed no individual rationale; no motive is attributed.
5. **IRR race-law count:** Its classification methodology remains a question for statutory reconciliation. An undated statement that three media companies “fact-checked” the whole index is **UNVERIFIED** without named checks; the president's general endorsement of redress laws is not an independent validation of totals.
