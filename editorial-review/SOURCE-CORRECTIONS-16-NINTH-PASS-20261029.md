## Evidence and exact corrections

1. **UNICEF Pretoria office now independently documented by the agency.** The 1994 timeline had correctly cited UNDP's start of South African operations but left its parallel UNICEF office date uncertain. UNICEF's own historical page confirms its **Pretoria office opened in 1994**, and records that UNICEF had already worked through NGOs during apartheid. This now appears in the timeline with the crucial qualification that opening an office is not proof of assisting with subsequent South African employment-equity statutes. A 1998 UNDP Executive Board seat remains **UNVERIFIED** without a contemporaneous roster.
2. **Dumisani Kumalo: co-facilitator, not controller of treaty monitoring.** Independent journalist Maureen Isaacson's **24 January 2019** historical profile describes Kumalo's role as **co-facilitator** of the creation of the Human Rights Council; UN General Assembly **Resolution 60/251 dated 15 March 2006** establishes the Council and defines its mandate. The article previously described Kumalo as having co-chaired the creation of the entire "body responsible for monitoring human rights compliance" and conflated it with oversight of all rights treaties. Now distinguishes the **intergovernmental Human Rights Council** from **CERD's independent expert review** under ICERD. No evidence that Kumalo influenced a particular CERD conclusion or suppressed minority complaints. The critical question about institutional independence remains open.
