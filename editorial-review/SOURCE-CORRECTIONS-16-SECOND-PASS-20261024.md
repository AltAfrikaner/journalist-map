## Red-team assessment
The strongest pro-Afrikaner criticism concerns the constitutionality, proportionality and labour-market effects of sector demographic targets, including potential unequal treatment of specific applicants. The opposing factual/legal account is that the EE law provides proportional mechanisms and documented grounds to justify non-attainment, while the judicial merits review remains pending. Neither account warrants claiming the international system was silent or that the interim refusal finally validated the policies. A funding relationship is also not evidence the donor ordered or approved each domestic rule.
