## Documentary corrections

1. **Two separate treaties had been conflated in the headline argument.** The **1965 International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination (ICERD)** includes conditional special measures under Articles 1(4) and 2(2). The **1973 International Convention on the Suppression and Punishment of the Crime of Apartheid** addresses enumerated inhuman acts and racial-domination purpose. Shared words about race do not turn every remedial classification into an apartheid offence. [OHCHR state-party register](https://tbinternet.ohchr.org/_layouts/15/TreatyBodyExternal/Treaty.aspx?CountryID=162) records South Africa's ICERD ratification **10 December 1998** and entry into force **9 January 1999**. [OHCHR ICERD text](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/international-convention-elimination-all-forms-racial-discrimination) sets out its exception and limits.
2. **Critical national accession date was absent.** The Apartheid Convention entered into force internationally **18 July 1976**, but South Africa did **not** accede until **14 May 2024**, effective for South Africa **13 June 2024**. This is documented by [the UN treaty depositary's country action](https://treaties.un.org/Pages/showActionDetails.aspx?clang=_en&objid=08000002806459c4) and [convention participant table](https://treaties.un.org/pages/viewdetails.aspx?chapter=4&clang=_en&mtdsg_no=iv-7&src=treaty), with independent [Business Day, 17 June 2024](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/national/2024-06-17-sa-accedes-to-apartheid-convention-decades-after-regime-opposed-it/) reporting. The historical comparison panel and the 1973 timeline expansion now explicitly separate adoption, international force and South African accession.
3. **Treaty supervision distinguished from coercive sanctions.** The [UN's 2023 concluding observations](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4031173), `CERD/C/ZAF/CO/9-11`, were adopted at the committee's 111th session, **20 November–8 December 2023**, and published **27 December 2023**. The existence of this review contradicts any claim of complete international silence; how it considered each current measure remains open to close reading. The panel now states the session and record rather than treating it as a Security Council enforcement instrument.
