## Corrections, reason and uncertainty
1. **“Every ANC cabinet since 1994” was false.** Dr Nkosazana Dlamini-Zuma served as a South African minister in 1994–September 2012, left to chair the **African Union Commission in October 2012–January 2017**, returned to cabinet in 2018, and retired from the national executive in 2024. Her absence from the 30 June 2024 official cabinet list and independent retirement reporting reconcile the dates. The dossier now gives discrete terms, not a continuous 1994–2026 service claim.
2. **Institutional conflation.** The 2001 Durban World Conference Against Racism, the African Union Commission and ministerial offices are not the treaty body's **independently elected CERD expert committee**. The section headline/lead were corrected to distinguish the institutions and their geographic settings. Parallel professional roles are not evidence that officials controlled CERD decisions; case-specific action or interference remains **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **UNAIDS officer claim.** A claimed **1995 deputy-chair appointment** remains **UNVERIFIED** pending an official contemporaneous roster. No personal culpability or illicit direction is asserted. CERD's documented 2016 and 2023 South Africa reviews remain acknowledged.
