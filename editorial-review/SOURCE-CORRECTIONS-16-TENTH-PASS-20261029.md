## Correction

The prior card said **more than 120 post-1994 Acts** without distinguishing standalone Acts from amended older statutes or defining **operative**. The IRR's official Index, last updated **11 June 2025**, says **324** Acts in the index since 1910, of which **122** are standalone Acts enacted since 1994; **145** remain formally operative, but **nine** have been deracialised. The index's editor Martin van Staden told a Washington audience on **25 September 2026** that **136** remain racially operative, according to independent Newsday coverage of **26 October 2026**. This is an explicit **IRR classification**, not a UN or judicial determination that every entry has presently enforceable discriminatory effects. The older 2022 IRR index counted 116 post-1994 Acts; avoid comparing the series without version dates. The homepage/visual counters `122+` remain historically inherited, unchanged and require separate authorised visual reconciliation.

The new prose separates (a) Acts still in force, (b) unrepealed but possibly constitutionally unenforceable pre-1994 provisions, (c) Acts whose racial terms have been removed, and (d) new post-1994 standalone Acts versus prior laws later racialised through amendment. No claim that the 2025 index was independently audited by Newsday. UN CERD treaty scrutiny in 2016 and 2023 remains visible, and its adequacy is still contestable.
