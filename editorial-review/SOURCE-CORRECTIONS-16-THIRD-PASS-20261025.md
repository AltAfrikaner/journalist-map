## Corrections and evidence thresholds
1. **B-BBEE legal mechanism:** The high-visibility 2003 timeline wrongly said white ownership and management earned zero B-BBEE points across the board and that all noncompliant firms were barred automatically from all government contracts. The **2013 amended generic codes specify five separate elements**; particular ownership/management targets reward qualifying black participation, but other score elements exist. Tender disqualification is not automatic across all public procurement. This correction *does not* defend race-based preferences; it makes the legal criticism precise.
2. **Procurement precedent:** The 2022 Constitutional Court decision invalidated **2017 pre-qualification regulations**, demonstrating that the legal rules and their validity matter. No finding that every use of B-BBEE in procurement is prohibited or that every exclusion is lawful.
3. **ILO chronology/target:** Solidarity lodged an earlier ILO complaint **in 2021** against the South African government, with a **28 June 2023 mediated settlement** entered as a Labour Court order **31 October 2023**. The article's 'first post-1994 2023 challenge' and 'against the UN system' were inaccurate/misleading. SABC reported a renewed complaint on **18 May 2026**; distinguish union allegation from adjudicated infringement.
4. **1987 oil embargo:** UN General Assembly adopted **A/RES/42/23 F on 20 Nov 1987**; the resolution urged cooperation but was **not** a mandatory Security Council oil embargo (contrast mandatory SC arms embargo in 1977).
5. **1973 treaty chronology:** Replaced shorthand 'declared crime in 1973' in display list with adoption of the 1973 Apartheid Convention, in force in **1976**; avoids implying that 1973 was the first UN condemnation or that all affirmative action automatically satisfies the treaty definition.
6. **1994 institutional transition:** UNDP/UNICEF cooperation developed over time, not literally instantly; contemporary CERD scrutiny exists and may be critiqued on adequacy.
7. **CERD and personnel influence:** The 2016 review's recommendation to accelerate transformation does not alone certify approval of particular race quotas. Named-official career overlap is not enough to prove a UN conflict of interest or categorical silence; **UNVERIFIED** without conflict/recusal records and decisions.
