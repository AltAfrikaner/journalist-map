## Documentary finding and correction

1. **Omitted post-1994 scrutiny:** The existing article correctly cited the **2016 and 2023 CERD reviews**, but its 'No Equivalent Apartheid-Era Sanctions Mechanism' chapter did not mention an additional, substantive **21 August 2026 decision** taken using CERD's Early Warning and Urgent Action Procedure, publicly reported **27 August**. The UN Geneva session summary confirms the decision concerned attacks on migrants, refugees and asylum seekers, while **28 August Mail & Guardian** independently reports the dates and recommendations.
2. **Victim-count provenance:** The committee cited reports of **at least four migrant killings and ~50,000 displaced** in 2026. These are **counts recited in the committee's concern**, not an independent census of verified incidents and not numbers of Afrikaner farm-attack victims; the article now labels them accordingly.
3. **Scope distinction:** Evidence of this UN intervention disproves a categorical post-1994 absence of scrutiny, but its **anti-migrant** focus neither disproves unequal attention to Afrikaner minority claims nor proves deliberate institutional suppression of such complaints. An Afrikaner-specific inquiry requires actual submissions, response letters, agenda decisions and legal grounds, still **UNVERIFIED**.
