## Material corrections

1. **Lejaka/Boxing SA:** A visible connection previously described a proved "quid pro quo" and "nine years of rewards". Mbalula's original 20 May 2016 ministerial speech says he *concurred with the Boxing South Africa board* to appoint Tsholofelo Lejaka, rather than making an independent unilateral appointment. Lejaka's original term was June 2016–July 2020. After September 2024 secondment under minister Gayton McKenzie, a different BSA board began recruitment in January 2025 and appointed him CEO effective 17 June 2025. The 2025 board release is quoted in contemporaneous reporting and independently confirmed by TimesLIVE. There is no evidence in those records of a continuous nine-year CEO tenure, or a 2025 Mbalula-directed reappointment. The reported R150,000 cash contribution and alleged absence of traceable accounts require primary receipts and banking verification. **Quid pro quo remains UNVERIFIED**, not dismissed as an issue for investigation.
2. **AYO versus Dubai payer:** A visible card treated AYO independent director Sello Rasethaba as the man proven to have paid the R300,000 supplier channel. AYO's company profile verifies his directorship, and independent News24 confirms AYO's Sekunjalo group relationship. But the Public Protector's original December 2018 investigation, contemporaneously reported by News24, identified the supplier-linked R300,000 as Sedgars/Reimon Uniforms; the later reported Bubesi R220,000 + R80,000 flows are separate allegations requiring primary bank records and repayment reconciliation. Neither the company's board association nor media ownership proves Rasethaba personally paid the supplier channel or controlled related editorial coverage. Both propositions **UNVERIFIED**.
