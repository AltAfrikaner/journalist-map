2. **Different functions: AFU ≠ Pretoria criminal prosecutors.** Replaced the statement that every Mbalula allegation was within an alleged spouse's AFU control. The AFU generally seeks preservation/forfeiture of proceeds; a DPP decides whether to prosecute particular criminal cases. The [NPA's own 3 August 2023 explanation](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/npa-wont-prosecute-mbalula) attributes the Dubai prosecution decline to police investigations and the Pretoria Specialised Commercial Crime Unit/DPP processes. No authenticated assignment memo, recusal decision or proof Rabaji-Rasethaba intervened was found. **UNVERIFIED:** personal conflict, misconduct, control or direction of that decision. Such a relationship should be examined, not presumed.
3. **Siweya link was already published.** Removed the "unpublished" label attached to the alleged Siweya/Dubai/Bryanston overlap. The 29 November 2021 [GroundUp investigation by Raymond Joseph and Masego Mafata](https://groundup.org.za/article/minister-lottery-boss-and-luxury-r56-million-home/) explicitly cross-referenced Siweya's involvement in the house purchase and his role in arranging the Dubai trip, citing the ethics report. It is therefore not an original discovery in a 2026 article.

[Public Protector Report No. 24 of 2018/19](https://www.pprotect.org/sites/default/files/legislation_report/REPORT%20OF%20THE%20PP%20MR%20FIKILE%20MBALULA_0.pdf) addresses bookings, not the asserted signature on a Dubai transaction. [News24's 19 December 2018 account](https://www.news24.com/southafrica/news/mbalula-violated-constitution-by-taking-dubai-trip-but-escapes-sanction-20181219) independently reports that the Public Protector traced Siweya's involvement in booking travel.
