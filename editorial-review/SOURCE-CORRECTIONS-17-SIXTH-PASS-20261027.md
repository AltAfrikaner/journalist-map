## Specific corrections and reasoning

1. **Undated public ministerial salary treated as current earnings.** The archived R2.47 million/year dashboard had no identified pay year and implicitly described today's ANC secretary-general remuneration. Mbalula ceased being transport minister and resigned from Parliament in March 2023. The 2025/26 gazetted public ministerial package applies to people holding ministerial office; it is not proof of Mbalula's later party salary. Qualify the displayed figure as an **undated former-minister assumption** and the 12 × R2.47m = R29.64m calculation as illustrative arithmetic, not audited income. **UNVERIFIED:** historic salary year, current ANC remuneration, lifetime income, household assets and purported R85–94m net worth.
2. **Wrong R8m house figure, forum and ownership.** The existing lifestyle card said "~R8M Bryanston Mansion — Frozen by Special Tribunal". The documented 2016 purchase offer and Upbrand Properties acquisition was **R5.6m**, with a **4 November 2022 Gauteng High Court** NLC-linked preservation order. The SIU issued a statement and GroundUp traced the document-based history. The named registered owner was **Upbrand**, not Mbalula. Restored accurate price, title and court; added a clearly worded source note. Preservation of a property is not a conviction of the former occupant or proof of his beneficial ownership. **UNVERIFIED:** claims about the contested loan and payment pathways, current beneficial ownership and eventual forfeiture.
3. **Scope and right of reply.** Mbalula's rebuttal and the disputed affidavits are recognised; a court preservation order does not adjudicate personal guilt. The pro-Afrikaner investigation of political accountability is retained, including unresolved concerns over NLC grant funds.

## Red-team controls / what this does not prove

The SIU order may be justified on proceeds-of-crime grounds without proving the individual named in an earlier offer personally owned the house. Conversely, the documented purchase sequence and competing loan/tenancy statements justify continuing investigation, not silence. Another property on a broader NLC list could have a different valuation; R5.6m is explicitly the **2016 identified transaction**, not a certified 2026 appraisal. A 2025/26 salary regulation for public ministers must not be back-cast or applied to ANC party officers.
