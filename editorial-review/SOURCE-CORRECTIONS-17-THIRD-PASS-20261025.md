## Source findings and eight scoped published corrections
1. **Housing denominator and criminal attribution.** The source article's "Over 14,000 RDP houses were never built" was a categorically stronger statement than the underlying 2017 reports support. Contemporary press summaries of **Nthimose Mokhesi's filed departmental affidavit** describe about **R631m paid in 2010–11** without required agreements or evidence of housing delivery; **more than 14,000 houses** was the intended programme scale, not a verified census of all never built. Do not assign the R631m global total to the Mbalula trusts or imply an individual conviction. The filed affidavit has been cited indirectly through reporting; **original certified pleadings still required**.
2. **Rebuttal and identified dispute.** 22 Jan 2017, Nozuko Mbalula gave a first-hand published denial: disputed the reported R38m and MMD trusteeship, and said Imbuma received no government money. This is her account, **not** an exonerating judicial finding. The dossier now includes it alongside the allegations, rather than presenting disputed personal participation as proved.
3. **Recovery chronology.** 15–29 Jan 2017 press reports described recovery litigation and a subsequent narrowing/withdrawal as to certain contractors, rather than a documented final adjudication. Claims that Magashule personally ordered withdrawal or that a final court judgment proved corruption remain **UNVERIFIED** absent dockets.
4. **Connection #12.** The narrative card no longer claims a single proven Nozuko–Zwane–Gupta family scheme merely from association and contemporaneous conduct; the proposed combined money-flow inference is marked **UNVERIFIED**.
5. **Court disclosure appeal.** The 3 Apr 2024 High Court **civil contempt** finding over ANC cadre-deployment records is distinct from the separate case on the legality of cadre-deployment policy. Independent 5 and 7 Oct 2024 reporting establishes that **leave to appeal the contempt decision was dismissed 4 Oct 2024**; the previous dossier incorrectly left *that specific step* wholly UNVERIFIED. Later superior-court proceedings/compliance still require fresh court records.
6. **Timeline and sources.** 2024 timeline now describes missing/redacted records and the DA's deletion allegation without independently asserting proof of each destroyed item. Court-appeal/source chronology expanded.
7. **Preserved interactive graph.** The original JS tooltip still claims all 14,000 homes were unbuilt and alleges Nozuko personal fraud. We did **not silently modify** JS; the adjacent visible graph disclaimer explicitly rejects those interpretations pending source-grade evidence. Correcting each data tooltip requires a separately approved graph-data audit.
8. All existing images, SVGs, scripts, IDs, original links and media are byte-identical; 8 new hyperlinks were added to the HTML source trail.
