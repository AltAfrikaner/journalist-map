## Correction A — NPA draft authorship is NOT established by the prosecutor's office

**Previously visible:** “A senior NPA commercial crimes prosecutor reportedly drafted seven counts of corruption and one count of fraud”. **Correction:** AfriForum's own signed **24 March 2026** submission to NDPP Andy Mothibi, paragraphs **12–13.1**, says that its own **2 October 2025** representations contained a **potential charge sheet**. The verified first-party text does not authenticate an NPA-authored draft or prove the previously asserted counts. The underlying charge-by-charge document and its actual drafter are **UNVERIFIED**. Such draft representations are not a court indictment. The original NPA decision of **2 August 2023** said investigators found no evidence supporting criminal activity; the Public Protector's earlier executive-ethics finding is a separate administrative outcome. AfriForum disputed the NPA's conclusion in 2026; Mbalula denied its allegations. None of these positions is an adjudicated corruption conviction.

## Correction B — exact invoice denominator versus rounded allegations

The same primary AfriForum letter reports invoice **R684,620.39** and alleged payment legs of **R300,000**, **R150,000**, two **R75,000** payments and **R85,000**. Their nominal sum is **R685,000**, a difference of **R379.61**, which could reflect rounded presentation. That arithmetic cannot validate underlying bank transfers or prove money laundering. The separate alleged **R220,000 + R80,000** Bubesi movements relate to subsequent financing/repayment narratives; adding them to an original invoice would mislead. New article paragraph expressly labels original statements and funds tracing **UNVERIFIED**, while retaining the Public Protector's confirmed **R275,000 subsequent payment** and independent 19 December 2018 reporting.
