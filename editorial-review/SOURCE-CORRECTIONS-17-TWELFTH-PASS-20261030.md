## Correction 1 — private boat is not an adjudicated procurement kickback

- **Prior wording:** categorical yacht ownership plus “Failed to deliver 2 of 4 units (R61M),” identifying MST as solely responsible, and no contemporaneous party response.
- **Checked:** ANC’s 13 January 2025 statement, directly reported by [Eyewitness News](https://www.ewn.co.za/2025/01/13/anc-defends-mbalulas-use-of-a-private-boat-to-get-to-robben-island-for-party-event), said the boat was a logistical arrangement and denied unethical benefit. ANC NEC member Donald Selamolela defended the arrangement in direct interview, reported independently by [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/politics/2025-01-13-mbalula-doesnt-arrange-his-transport-anc-nec-member-defends-sgs-luxury-yacht-entrance/). ANC chair Gwede Mantashe publicly said there was no NEC authorisation for that specific mode of transport ([Cape Times report of interview](https://capetimes.co.za/news/politics/2025-01-13-anc-chairperson-distances-party-from-mbalulas-luxury-yacht-arrival-at-anniversary-event/)). Reporting about the historic KZN clinics concerned **two companies**, one being MST; the company-level allocation, R61m expenditure and delivery dates remain unconfirmed against original tender/acceptance files ([IOL contemporaneous account](https://iol.co.za/capeargus/news/2025-01-13-mbalulas-grand-entrance-at-recent-event-on-robben-island-raises-eyebrows/)). The ANC’s denial is an on-record primary response, **not an independently established absence of a benefit**.
- **Correction:** The page now distinguishes reported transport, the ANC's account, Mantashe's NEC distinction and the **two-company** procurement allegation. It no longer implies that MST alone failed both deliveries or that any boat arrangement was a proven corrupt favour.
- **UNVERIFIED:** boat title, actual provider/invoice/donation, costs, original tender allocations/acceptances, forensic findings and quid pro quo.
