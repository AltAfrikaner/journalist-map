## #20 — Fikile Mbalula network investigation

| Original assertion / risk | Action and basis |
|---|---|
| “Sello Rasethaba paid Mbalula R300,000 via Reimon-Bubesi.” | **Corrected attribution:** Public Protector report No. 24 of 2018/19 addresses **Sedgars director Yusuf Dockrat's R300,000 via Reimon Uniforms to the travel account**. Do not merge it with separately alleged Bubesi transfers. These require validated bank pages and recipient reconciliation. |
| NPA Asset Forfeiture Unit automatically controls every corruption allegation and Rabaji-Rasethaba's family relationship legally required recusal, which “never” occurred. | **Qualified:** official appointment and AFU remit are demonstrable; individual case allocation, family relationship, exact personal interest, particular recusal application and any resolution are **UNVERIFIED**. Do not attribute misconduct to an NPA official based on common surname or an untested graph connection. |
| Seven overlapping Rasethaba positions establish an unprecedented single control structure; universal absence of investigation. | **Qualified as hypothesis:** role and dated entity filings remain necessary. Universal negatives and comparative superlatives cannot be deduced from the documents reviewed. |
| R85–94m alleged net worth minus R30m career earnings proves R55–64m suspect gap and a prosecutable offence. | **Corrected methodology:** net worth is a stock, income a flow; both are unverified estimates with missing assets, debts, tax, valuation dates, returns and ownership details. The arithmetic is **not** prima facie corruption, an FIC determination or a SARS audit finding. |
| AFU categorically took no action; Public Protector report “2017”; criminal sanction inferred from the ethics finding. | **Corrected:** report was issued **19 December 2018**, concerns December 2016–January 2017 trip. NPA had declined to prosecute and AfriForum challenged in March 2026. Public records reviewed did not show a particular AFU order; that does not prove absence of internal action. Distinguish ethics, prosecution discretion and a criminal verdict. |
| Separate September 2026 prosecution of Mbalula. | **Clarified in right-of-reply:** reported **crimen injuria** proceedings related to remarks about a school principal, not a reversal of the Dubai-holiday decision. |
| “No independent capital” and recurring 23-year common command/funder template. | **Qualified as UNVERIFIED inference** requiring dated shareholding records and transaction-specific links; charts and diagrams left intact. |
