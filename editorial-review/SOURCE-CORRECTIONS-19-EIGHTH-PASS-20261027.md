### Red-team finding

There is legitimate public interest in whether a government employee worked on a contested letter during official duty or through departmental resources, especially given its international political audience. Evidence of canvassing, however, cannot be silently promoted into proof of state payment or intelligence direction. Equally, one signatory's contradictory account cannot exclude all involvement by another person. Outstanding: original dated draft and metadata, Burger's official mandate and employment history, correspondence with signatories, departmental expense approvals, independently inspected PAIA request/reply records, and a verified 43-person affiliation matrix.
