## Source triangulation

1. **Original primary statement:** [News24, 25 October 2025](https://www.news24.com/opinions/columnists/guestcolumn/not-in-our-name-afrikaners-respond-to-the-misuse-of-their-story-in-us-politics-20251025-0450) publishes the declaration, the explicit opposition to any single body's mandate to speak for every Afrikaner, and the signatory list. It is evidence of the signatories' stated position, not proof of representativeness.
2. **Independent contemporaneous roster:** [Smile 90.4FM, 27 October 2025](https://smilefm.co.za/anc-welcomes-not-in-our-name-stance-by-group-of-afrikaners/) reproduces the list with Louise du Plessis duplicated, supporting **44 printed entries / 43 unique names**, without proving intention.

That places the reported discussion in early November, rather than before or during the October 25 publication. This is self-reported outreach, **not** independent Senate confirmation of exactly which text the office received.
4. **Electoral context independent of the letter:** [International Foundation for Electoral Systems election guide](https://www.electionguide.org/elections/id/4318/) and [Parliamentary Monitoring Group](https://pmg.org.za/blog/Election%20Results%20and%20Allocation%20of%20Seats%20in%20Parliament%20National%20Assembly%20and%20Provincial%20Legislatures%202024) confirm the FF+ 2024 election baseline; ballots do not measure community opinion on this declaration.

## Adversarial/release hold

The critical pro-Afrikaner question—whether a socially concentrated professional group was presented as representative of wider communities—is valid to investigate. The letter never claims to be a random-sample survey, however; a recorded conversation with a US senator does not itself show state orchestration. Verify congressional correspondence, full roster, occupations at signature date, sector overlap, the 3D edge provenance, PAIA submissions/responses and any claimed recipient-level grant before certification.
