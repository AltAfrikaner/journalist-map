## Competing records and provenance

1. **Original letter / participant source:** [News24 full 25 October 2025 open letter](https://www.news24.com/opinions/columnists/guestcolumn/not-in-our-name-afrikaners-respond-to-the-misuse-of-their-story-in-us-politics-20251025-0450). The text rejects an aggregate persecution framing, acknowledges crime and inequality, and expressly denies that any single organisation speaks for all Afrikaners. It does not establish the composition, financing or authorship of preparatory drafts.
2. **Independent reproduction / party response:** [Smile 90.4FM, 27 October 2025](https://smilefm.co.za/anc-welcomes-not-in-our-name-stance-by-group-of-afrikaners/) publishes the roster with Louise du Plessis duplicated and separates ANC party praise from Presidency spokesperson Vincent Magwenya's public comment. A repeated printed name is not proof of the duplicate's motive.
3. **Critic's first-person attribution *and contrary account*:** [AfriForum, 3 November 2025](https://www.artikels.afriforum.co.za/regering-het-toe-groot-hand-in-nie-in-ons-naam-nie-ope-brief/) alleges, relying on Rapport's 2 November report, that DIRCO official Karen Burger drew up a draft. That same AfriForum release records that **Burger denied writing the final declaration but acknowledged recruiting support**. Whether a draft and the published text are the same work, and whether she acted officially, remain unresolved. The organisation's characterization of the episode as a state project is a **claim**, not an established institutional finding.
4. **Participant's contrary statement, independently reported:** [Joburg ETC, 4 November 2025](https://www.joburgetc.com/news/gareth-cliff-investigation-44-afrikaners-trump-letter/) attributes to signatory and claimed initiator Max du Preez the denial that he knew Burger when the initiative began. This is a material counterinterpretation; it does not by itself disprove that she canvassed *other* signatories.
5. **Public PAIA assertion:** [AfriForum, 14 December 2025](https://www.artikels.afriforum.co.za/joernaliste-dalk-betaal-om-afriforum-te-diskrediteer-burgerregteorganisasie-doen-paia-aansoek-om-antwoorde-te-kry/) states that it filed information requests with the SSA and DIRCO about possible state-financed communications. A press release is evidence of the organisation's **assertion**, not a substitute for filed request copies, proof of receipt, correspondence, subsequent responses or legally calculated deadlines.

**Red-team comparison:** AfriForum has a legitimate interest in probing governmental involvement in a political letter. An official's participation in signature solicitation is potentially newsworthy; it is not proof that her department instructed, paid for or controlled its signatories. Conversely, one signatory's denial does not disprove involvement by a different participant. Both competing accounts remain visible. A rigorous finding would require the draft chain, dated messages, mandate, work-hours/resources and person-level agreement or financing records.
