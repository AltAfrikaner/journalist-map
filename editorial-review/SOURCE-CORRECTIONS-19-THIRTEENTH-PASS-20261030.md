## Correction and independent evidence
- **Primary self-report:** [AfriForum, *What does AfriForum do with your donations?* (2026)](https://afriforum.co.za/en/what-does-afriforum-do-with-your-donations/) gives the May 2026 membership components as **261,973 monthly contributors + 5,639 annual contributors + 32,799 non-contributors**. The three values add to **300,411**, but its printed total is **300,141**, an internal difference of **270**. The page has no independently audited roll attached. This is an arithmetical documentary discrepancy, not evidence of fraud or intentional inflation.
- **Independent comparison:** [Newsday, 16 July 2026](https://newsday.co.za/south-africa/21136/afriforum-gets-r22-6-million-per-month-from-its-300000-supporters-for-safety-community-development-and-court-cases/) reports roughly 300,000 supporters. These are **different as-of dates and self-reported populations**, not independently verified year-over-year membership change.

The revised article dates the newer disclosure and flags the 270-person source discrepancy **UNVERIFIED pending correction**. It keeps the separate Solidarity estimate explicitly provisional.
