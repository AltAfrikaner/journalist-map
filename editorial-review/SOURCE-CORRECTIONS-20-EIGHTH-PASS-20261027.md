### Red-team assessment

A first-hand reporter's account of live proceedings is meaningful evidence that testimony was given; it cannot establish that the witness was correct in every inference. A prepaid handset does **not** imply carrier metadata was untraceable: that claim requires original records. Forbidden Stories performed significant primary investigative work by analysing 76 leaked documents and corroborating aspects of the larger data set, but its account of a *particular meeting or cash transfer* must still be distinguished from a recipient's bank statement or an adjudicated finding. Historical Soviet relationships may provide a research framework; asserting that an identical payment or intelligence command structure continued across fifty years requires much stronger linking proof. The AFU can pursue recovery independently of guilt determinations, and kinship without personal involvement is not proof of procedural impropriety.
