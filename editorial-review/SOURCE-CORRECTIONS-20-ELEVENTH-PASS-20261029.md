## Three material corrections
1. The section wrongly said that **both** 2025 Mbalula-related defamation proceedings were **default judgments**. The original court judgment **Mbalula v Mda (121445/2025) [2025] ZAGPPHC 878, 20 August 2025**, records Mda's answering affidavit and counsel. It was contested. The judge expressly found the specified Bozwana-murder allegations false and defamatory, granting removal, apology and interdict relief. Do not describe an opposed, reasoned judgment as a default.
2. The page dated Mda's apology **November 2025**; independent reports published **5 and 6 May 2026** corroborate a **5 May 2026** public retraction/apology after refusal of leave to appeal. This error materially reversed the timeline.
3. The **25 November 2025** Mogotsi proceeding was **unopposed**, but contemporary reports of Judge Jacobus Strydom's judgment describe explicit findings of false and defamatory statements, retraction and removal orders, and civil damages liability, with quantum reserved. It is wrong to claim those orders were merely automatic silence or made no findings. Neither the civil findings nor the absence of a defended hearing is a criminal verdict or proof about separate procurement/political-finance accusations. The complete Mogotsi signed order and damages outcome are **UNVERIFIED** in the available primary record.
