## Primary and independent sources
- **Original commission record location:** [State Capture Commission, 29 June 2021 transcript (Day 419)](https://www.statecapture.org.za/site/files/transcript/436/Day_419_-_2021-06-29.pdf). Direct retrieval was blocked (HTTP 403), so this pass does **not** claim independent full-transcript certification.
- **Adverse contemporaneous testimony:** [The Citizen, 29 June 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/cwele-denies-claims-he-wanted-to-stop-ssas-gupta-probe/) directly reports Siyabonga Cwele's sworn account: the meeting occurred in **2010**, not 2011; he denied ordering legal investigations halted and said he asked about judicial authorisation of interceptions and potential conflicts.
- **Contrary contemporaneous testimony:** [Daily Maverick, 26 November 2019](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2019-11-26-how-zuma-and-state-security-minister-cwele-shut-down-2011-investigation-into-the-guptas/) reports Mo Shaik's allegations about interference with a proposed **2011** probe. [Corruption Watch, 25 November 2019](https://www.corruptionwatch.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Zondo-Commission-%E2%80%93-intelligence-services-restructured-to-protect-the-state-says-Mo-Shaik.pdf) dates Mbalula's ANC NEC disclosure to **August 2011**. The testimony is inconsistent on sequence; neither account can be promoted to an uncontested finding by omitting the other.

- Alleged explicit ministerial instruction to stop the Gupta inquiry is now **attributed to Shaik and colleagues and disputed by Cwele**; interception-authorisation explanation recorded, without treating Cwele's denial as proof of innocence.
- “Gupta Capture” timeline and “Structural Paradox” finding separately repeated the same date error and asserted inferred coordination/motive as facts. Both corrected with **UNVERIFIED** markers and source/counter-source note.
- Original source, picture and interactive graph evidence preserved; 3D/JS tooltips may still display old claims and **remain uncertified**, requiring a separate coordinated evidence audit.
