Reviewed **26 October 2026** against current `main` at 4f0133f9766c4bd8f96e3b35218ecad95a658eaf. Page: `mbalula_network.html`. **OPEN — not evidence-certified**.

## Primary sources and independent reporting

1. **Government primary record:** [South African Department of Transport, 31 May 2019](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/transport-congratulates-minister-mbalula-and-deputy-minister-magadzi-their) confirms Mbalula was Minister of **Sport and Recreation 2010–2017**, Minister of **Police 2017–2018**. The [government's personnel biography](https://www.gov.za/about-government/contact-directory/fikile-april-mbalula-mr) gives **31 March 2017** as the Police start date, dates ANCYL president to **2004–2008**.
2. **Independent August 2004 contemporaneous reporting:** [Mail & Guardian, 13 August 2004](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2004-08-13-youth-league-chooses-its-young-lion/) places the Youth League presidential election at a Johannesburg congress in August 2004. 'Nasrec' is not verified as its venue.
3. **Investigative-source error identified:** [Global Initiative Against Transnational Organized Crime, Eastern and Southern Africa Risk Bulletin #18 (2021)](https://riskbulletins.globalinitiative.net/esa-obs-018/05-the-case-of-wandile-bozwana.html) recounts a reported 2015 Bozwana–Mbalula–Mathibela meeting, yet calls Mbalula the *then minister of police*. The government record contradicts that portfolio label. The bulletin's meeting account is a report, not an original attendance/video record reproduced by this dossier; it does **not** establish Mbalula controlled a police murder investigation in 2015.
4. **Conflicting testimony:** [The Citizen's contemporaneous report of Cwele's 29 June 2021 denial](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/cwele-denies-claims-he-wanted-to-stop-ssas-gupta-probe/) and [Daily Maverick's November 2019 account of Mo Shaik](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2019-11-26-how-zuma-and-state-security-minister-cwele-shut-down-2011-investigation-into-the-guptas/) disagree on the date and claimed obstruction. Their claims are not substitute for the complete [commission transcript](https://www.statecapture.org.za/site/files/transcript/436/Day_419_-_2021-06-29.pdf), which previously resisted direct retrieval.

Included the surprising error in the GI-TOC source itself and kept the reported pre-murder meeting as a lead, with primary attendance/communications **UNVERIFIED**.
