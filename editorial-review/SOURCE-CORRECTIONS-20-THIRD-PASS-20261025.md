## Evidence register: primary plus independent comparisons

- **Signed primary corporate announcement:** [Randgold & Exploration SENS issuer notice, 28 July 2003](https://www.sharedata.co.za/senss.asp?id=49771) identifies the Phikoloso empowerment transaction, 8.8 million new shares and contemporaneously quoted R268 million value. This is **28 July 2003**, over two years before Kebble's **27 September 2005** killing, not “two months before”.
- **Primary retrospective securities disclosure:** [Randgold & Exploration circular dated 13 June 2006 and filed with the US SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1033217/000111667906001655/ex99-3.htm), §2.4 and the earlier forensic findings. The new board described an investigation of fictitious assets in the Phikoloso transaction. Those corporate forensic allegations concern the transaction and former company officials; they do not on their own prove that Fikile Mbalula received its consideration or that a later transaction was directed by the same participants.
- **Independent contemporaneous corporate reporting:** [News24, 28 July 2003](https://www.news24.com/business/randgold-phikoloso-in-a-deal-20030728) reported the 8.8 million shares/R268 million agreement before the later forensic findings. Contemporaneous optimism does not nullify the documented 2006 dispute; the two records concern distinct points in time.
- **Original 2011 attributed interview / opposing view:** [Mail & Guardian, 8 April 2011](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2011-04-08-kebble-mentored-mbalula/) reports butler Andrew Minnaar's account and Mbalula's rebuttal of claims he was coached. Mbalula acknowledged a direct telephone line and a call on Kebble's day of death. He did **not** establish that he was Kebble's final caller.
- **Independent reconstruction using contemporary cellphone records:** [Mail & Guardian, 26 May 2006](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2006-05-26-we-ll-meet-in-paradise/) reports that Kebble spoke to John Stratton and then family; its account places the end of a **family call at 20:52** on 27 September 2005. The newspaper itself calls the records incomplete. Thus the categorical “Mbalula was the last person” assertion is **unsupported and contradicted by the available reconstruction**, while a fully exhaustive forensic sequence remains **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Later allegations and subject's denial:** [Forbidden Stories investigation republished in Daily Maverick, 16 March 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-03-16-mbalulas-covert-meetings-and-manoeuvres-of-russian-influence-agents-in-sa-namibia-and-madagascar/) describes leaked Russian influence-agent accounts about a purported 2024 meeting. Mbalula and Bongani Mbindwane denied material allegations. A leaked account is evidence that a claim was made, not adjudication that it happened.
