## Source hierarchy and verified corrections
1. **Primary historical record:** The ANC's [12 May 1997 further submission to the Truth and Reconciliation Commission](https://www.anc1912.org.za/submissions-1997-further-submissions-and-responses-by-the-anc-to-questions-raised-by-the-commission-for-truth-and-reconciliation/) acknowledges serious Camp 32 security-prisoner abuse, describes the Stuart inquiry's criticism of unauthorised disciplinary activity, disputes systematic abuse and explicitly rejects training in torture. The published document is the ANC's position and cannot by itself resolve contested victim accounts.
2. **Independent historical evidence:** Stephen Ellis, ["Mbokodo: Security in ANC Camps, 1961–1990"](https://ascleiden.nl/publications/mbokodo-security-anc-camps-1961-1990), *African Affairs* 93 (1994), reviews four ANC-appointed inquiries. Thula Simpson, ["Military Combat Work"](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00020184.2011.557579), *African Studies* 70 (2011), describes Soviet training of MK cadres in the 1970s. Neither publication establishes a chain of command to a named contemporary SVR operation.
3. **Modern investigation, separately labelled allegation:** [Forbidden Stories, 13 March 2026](https://forbiddenstories.org/russian-influence-south-africa-namibia-madagascar/), reports a purported 23 December 2024 meeting and US$3,000 adviser payments from leaked materials; its interviews record Mbalula and Mbindwane denials. [Daily Maverick, 16 March 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-03-16-mbalulas-covert-meetings-and-manoeuvres-of-russian-influence-agents-in-sa-namibia-and-madagascar/) republishes consortium findings and is **not** counted as independent authentication of the leaked meeting. Identity, bank transfers, instructions and links to Camp 32-era security structures remain **UNVERIFIED**.
