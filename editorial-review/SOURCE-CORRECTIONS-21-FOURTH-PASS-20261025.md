## Red team and limits

The filings verify that donation income grew, **not** that monitoring hatred was undertaken as a sham for fundraising; the causal proposal would require contemporaneous donor data, grant restrictions and board/strategy records. Likewise an informant may be used in lawful civil-rights intelligence or in unlawful fraud: those are contested criminal elements for a court, not an editorial conclusion.
