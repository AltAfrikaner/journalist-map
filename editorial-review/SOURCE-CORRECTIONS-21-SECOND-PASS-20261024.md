## Red-team / contrary evidence

Documenting Hate openly listed SPLC as a collaborating civil-rights organisation, but that is **not** proof SPLC supplied all records or controlled each newsroom. The Google News index sourced from news reports, not solely SPLC allegations, and ProPublica disclosed that it attempted to verify tips. A media outlet may purchase or sell advertising without editorial oversight being sold. IRS Schedule F investment balances are not equivalent to tainted cash transfers. Prosecutors' accusations are not findings. Criticism of SPLC labels and funding remains legitimate; additional proof is required for charges of coordination, fraud or South African platform suppression.
