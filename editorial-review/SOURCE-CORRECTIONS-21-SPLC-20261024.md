# Source and correction register — local 21 `splc.html`

Reviewed 24 October 2026 against latest `main`. This is a *scoped correction*, not an end-to-end fact-check certification.

## Findings, source conflict and correction rationale

1. **April indictment was reported as exposing guilt.** The earlier homepage-leading sentence said the indictment “exposed” a settled wrongdoing and that SPLC directly “exported” its method to South Africa. The 21 April indictment is a grand-jury charging document, not a finding; the Department of Justice's **12 August second superseding indictment** alleged a broader 2007–2023 period and over US$4 million. The hero now expressly labels charges as allegations, dates the initial and later proceedings, and retains criticism of private label-making. April dollar figures in the existing timeline remain historical and must not be interpreted as complete present charges. Sources: [DOJ 21 April 2026](https://www.justice.gov/usao-mdal/pr/federal-grand-jury-charges-southern-poverty-law-center-wire-fraud-false-statements-and); [DOJ 12 August 2026](https://www.justice.gov/usao-mdal/pr/second-superseding-indictment-adds-defendant-and-charges-southern-poverty-law-center); [University of Michigan Civil Rights Litigation Clearinghouse case 2:26-cr-00139](https://clearinghouse.net/case/48122/); [CBS June 2026 superseding indictment coverage](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/southern-poverty-law-center-superseding-indictment/).
2. **South African transfer asserted without a traceable transmission record.** The former article categorically stated SPLC interviewed no AfriForum members, read no court cases, and led reporting at named newsrooms. Those negative/universal assertions were not backed by an auditable research log or each named publisher's editorial records. Rewritten to **UNVERIFIED** in the narrative; the underlying illustrative sequence remains untouched and is identified as a hypothesis map, not observed moderation actions. See [Al Jazeera's 15 May 2018 original AfriForum article and 30 May correction](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2018/5/15/south-african-group-under-fire-for-lobbying-us-for-white-rights), which independently documents coverage without proving SPLC direction. Origin of phrase “white supremacists in suits and ties” requires original-source attribution. No graph edges were removed because each needs separately documented source and authorisation.
3. **Contrary evidence and right of reply.** SPLC has denied the charges and stated its informants helped prevent violence by sharing intelligence with law enforcement; its former official also denies wrongdoing. The 7 August ruling against a vindictive-prosecution dismissal motion did not decide the merits of fraud counts. Sources: [SPLC response reported 13 August](https://alabamareflector.com/2026/08/13/u-s-department-of-justice-files-new-charges-against-southern-poverty-law-center/); [Reuters 12 August](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-justice-department-charges-former-southern-poverty-law-center-executive-fraud-2026-08-12/); [docket](https://clearinghouse.net/case/48122/).

## Numerical, jurisdiction and red-team checks

- **11 counts**: accurate for **original April 2026** charging instrument, no longer representative of all superseding counts. **US$3M/2014–2023** in original versus **over US$4M/2007–2023** in August allegations; not to be mixed as confirmed expenditure or proven criminal proceeds. US federal case in Middle District of Alabama does not establish guilt of African publishers or fact-checkers.
- **Adversarial hypothesis**: independently acquired opinions can converge; shared funder/terminology or media syndication can coexist with editorial independence. To demonstrate deliberate coordinated labelling or platform suppression, require dated direct messages, contracts, grant deliverables, explicit citations, platform decisions and opportunity for identified subjects' response. **No such causal chain was proven by this batch.**
- Unreviewed high-risk figures: post-Charlottesville US$82m comparative revenue, tax form reporting periods, US$57m ‘offshore transfers’ versus investments, 6,600% media citation spike and 53-node /105-edge visual assertions. An article asserting ‘verified’ without linked per-claim documents requires future case-level review.
