## Corrections against current main

The 1971 timeline entry wrongly described KKK litigation as SPLC's original focus, and conflated its two incorporators with its first president. The original [SPLC organisational history](https://www.splcenter.org/about/our-history/) dates incorporation by **Morris Dees and Joe Levin** and identifies **Julian Bond as first president**. Its early work involved desegregation, electoral representation, state-trooper integration and prisons; the dedicated **Klanwatch project began in 1981**, not 1971. The independently written [Encyclopedia of Alabama history](https://encyclopediaofalabama.org/article/southern-poverty-law-center-splc/) corroborates the founder/president roles and the early civil-rights caseload. The first entry now reflects these distinctions, without removing scrutiny of later fundraising.

The 1990 entry's former heading “Hate Map Launched” could falsely imply the modern interactive website launched then. An [independent historical retrospective](https://capitalresearch.org/article/featured-interview-the-debate-over-hate/) identifies an annual hate-group census beginning in **1990**, while [SPLC's public methodology](https://www.splcenter.org/resources/reports/methodology-how-hate-groups-are-identified-and-categorized/) explains present group classification. The heading now identifies the annual census, and the exact launch date of the later interactive map is explicitly **UNVERIFIED**. This history does not establish the accuracy of every classification.
