## Before/after corrections and evidence

1. **Who made the 27 August 2025 ruling?** Earlier hero and metadata said “South Africa's Human Rights Commission convicted” Malema. In fact, **the Equality Court** made a **civil finding under section 10 of the Equality Act** after complaints brought by SAHRC and Dante van Wyk. The 2022 remarks were not a criminal conviction, nor was every rendition of “Kill the Boer” adjudicated by this specific case. Updated hero, metadata and two criterion paragraphs. Primary: [SAHRC official statement, 28 August 2025](https://sahrc.org.za/index.php/sahrc-media/news-2/item/4368-media-statement-equality-court-upholds-sahrc-s-complaint-against-mr-julius-malema-and-the-eff); independent: [News24 report on judgment, 27 August 2025](https://www.news24.com/southafrica/crime-and-courts/malema-found-guilty-of-hate-speech-for-2022-rally-remarks-20250827-0770).
2. **April 2026 five-year sentence falsely presented as speech penalty.** It was imposed for separate **2018 firearm offences** and was under appeal; a high court granted leave to challenge the convictions on **29 September 2026**. Corrected the two prose conflations and added a source note. Sources: [Reuters, 16 April 2026](https://www.reuters.com/world/south-african-politician-malema-sentenced-prison-firearm-case-2026-04-16/); [TimesLIVE, 29 September 2026](https://www.timeslive.co.za/politics/2026-09-29-high-court-grants-malema-leave-to-appeal-firearm-convictions/).
3. **447 farm and smallholding murders: numerator and denominator.** Earlier hero and summaries risked equating a count of all victims in a geographic class with white/Afrikaner victims. **AFP collated 447 from police quarterly reports for October 2023–September 2024; the releases did not specify victims' or perpetrators' race.** The 2024 State Department country report *cited* those figures; the United States did not independently enumerate them. Corrected hero, metadata, two repeated paragraphs and metric label. Source: [AFP Fact Check and method, March 2025](https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.36ZD7HY); independent contextual source: [South African government's criticism of US 2024 report](https://iol.co.za/news/politics/2025-08-13-south-africa-slams-us-human-rights-report-labels-it-biased-and-misleading/). Total farm homicides alone cannot establish/rule out racist targeting without incident-level case data, comparative rates and population exposures.
4. **Historical intelligence agency chronology.** The glossary incorrectly said BOSS was abolished in **1969**, when it was in fact **formed in 1969**; it became DONS in **1978** and the NIS in **1980**. Corrected the prose glossary. Source: [South African History Online](https://sahistory.org.za/article/bureau-state-security-boss); [historical agency timeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Intelligence_Service_%28South_Africa%29) as secondary chronology only. References to BOSS in late-1980s graph nodes are historical shorthand and require primary documentation.
5. **ADL's published 2018 position and right of reply.** Its [24 August 2018 article](https://education.adl.org/resources/article/racist-obsession-south-african-white-genocide) disputes government-sponsored genocide claims, **not the existence of all farm murders**. The article predates the 2025 State Department publication by seven years and cannot be a response to that later report. Updated opening context with the original ADL source; disagreement over advocacy priorities remains fair inquiry.

## Red-team and outstanding allegations

A civil Equality Court ruling about distinct 2022 remarks must not be conflated with the legal conclusion about historic protest songs in other cases; the 2018 US advocacy page is not a binding definition for South African courts. The **447** estimate does not supply a race-specific numerator or baseline rate. Shared donors, advisory participation, high income or historical intelligence investigations **do not alone establish** current coordinated censorship or unlawful influence. These sweeping hypotheses, the donation and lobbying totals, the 40/62 visual-map connections and named individual allegations remain **UNVERIFIED pending primary-award, financial and document-by-document testing**.
