[The consolidated statutory text](https://lawlibrary.org.za/akn/za/act/2003/53/eng%402015-10-24) and independent [Werksmans legal analysis of the 2022 Constitutional Court procurement ruling](https://www.werksmans.com/legal-updates-and-opinions/constitutional-court-confirms-invalidity-of-broad-based-black-economic-empowerment-bbbee-pre-qualification-criteria-for-state-tenders/) demonstrate why non-beneficiary status must not be described as a statutory ban on white employment or business ownership. Legislation's distributive effects may still merit robust scrutiny; a complete ADL B-BBEE analysis archive is **UNVERIFIED**.

ADL's [own 2 June 2017 Nevada SB26 release](https://www.adl.org/governor-sandoval-signs-anti-bds-bill-into-law) documents one identifiable supported measure (then describing 19 other states), not 50 ADL-backed statutes.

Israel-related advocacy and attention to Afrikaner rights are not logically mutually exclusive; no primary grant terms or platform case files prove an ADL-directed suppression chain.
