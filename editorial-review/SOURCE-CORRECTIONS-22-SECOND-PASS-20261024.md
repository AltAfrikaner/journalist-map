# Investigation 22 — ADL: second-pass correction register

Reviewed **24 October 2026** on `adl.html` against parent main `737045611bd49d9e97c23d906ca49318c2ed59f7`. Extends [the original legal/measurement correction](SOURCE-CORRECTIONS-22-ADL-20261024.md). **This is not a final evidence-certification.** The article retains its pro-Afrikaner equal-protection and transparency challenge without converting suspicions into proven donor instructions.

## Reader-facing corrections, one by one

1. **Donor profiling** — Replaced unverified categorical assertions that ADL's contributors belong to one race/religion or political allegiance with a concrete disclosure and equal-treatment test. No donor-level record was produced to establish those demographic claims or a motive to disregard Afrikaners.
2. **2018 article's actual scope** — The ADL source [24 Aug 2018](https://education.adl.org/resources/article/racist-obsession-south-african-white-genocide) acknowledges the murder of white farmers. It challenges a broader alleged government-sanctioned `white genocide`. The prior assertion that *any* evidence of anti-white violence was branded fabricated is inaccurate. Replaced with exact scope and recorded **UNVERIFIED** claim of automatic extremist tagging on platforms.
3. **Africa Check numerical provenance** — ADL did cite **74 murders during 2016–17 farm attacks** and approximately **19,000 nationwide murders**. Africa Check's [primary methodology](https://africacheck.org/fact-checks/factsheets/factsheet-statistics-farm-attacks-and-murders-south-africa) distinguishes farm location, race where known, work role, and reporting periods. Replaced an alleged self-sealing command loop with this primary record and a demand for documented instructions.
4. **2018 versus 2024 comparison (two reader-facing panels)** — It was an invalid contradiction to place 2016–17 counts against **447 geographically defined homicides, all victims, October 2023–September 2024**. [AFP's 2025 methodological examination](https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.36ZD7HY) and the [2024 US country report](https://www.ecoi.net/de/dokument/2128549.html) substantiate the difference. The US report does criticise EFF rhetoric, which is different from an estimate of white victims or proof of genocide.
5. **Google funding/control statement** — Separated reported corporate relationship from the unverified assertion that Google paid ADL to *direct* YouTube enforcement, or could compel Meta rating decisions via IFCN.
6. **Funder percentages / denominators** — Marked the asserted OSF-SA 12%, Omidyar/Luminate 23%, Gates 13% percentages **UNVERIFIED** until financial year, recipient entity, grant definition and audited denominator are supplied. Avoids representing them as current fact.
7. **Africa Check → Meta enforcement** — Distinguished source citation from documented enforcement, and IFCN accreditation from Meta's own decision authority, referencing the [PolitiFact partner explanation of 7 Jan 2025](https://politifact.com/article/2025/jan/07/meta-ending-third-party-fact-checking-partnership/).
8. **Closed-circuit accusation** — Maintained visible institutional critique but revised an absolute claim of deliberate, categorical suppression to **UNVERIFIED** pending contracts, content notice, timestamped ratings, appeal records and independent grant files.
9. **SPLC comparison** — April 2026 charges, later superseded, do not prove guilt; ADL is not charged. Removed claim of an identical criminal/fundraising structure absent records.
10. **Right of reply/absence** — The 2018 ADL piece predates the 2024 US report and 27 Aug 2025 Equality Court decision. Lack of an update to that article is verifiable but does not establish that ADL made no statements elsewhere. Claims of deliberate omission are **UNVERIFIED**.
11. **IHRA misquotation / legal leap** — The [IHRA primary working definition](https://holocaustremembrance.com/resources/working-definition-antisemitism) uses a double-standard example concerning **Israel and other democratic states**. It does *not* rule that ADL legally discriminated against Afrikaners. The original universal extrapolation was corrected; normative appeal for consistent protections retained. Independent supporting reading: [World Jewish Congress 4 May 2022 explanation](https://www.worldjewishcongress.org/en/news/defining-antisemitism-double-standards-against-the-state-of-israel).
12. **Revenue/donor-motive inference** — No reliable primary record showed Jewish donors collectively demanded disregard for Afrikaner victimisation. Reframed as a hypothesis to test against organisational policies and evidence; unsupported group motive marked **UNVERIFIED**.
13. **Final `ADL primary source` card** — Contrasted exactly what the **2018** piece and the later **2024** country report say, without inventing an arithmetical contradiction; retained challenge to ADL's breadth and updating of analysis.
14. **Final `SAHRC v Malema` card** — The **27 August 2025** decision is a **civil Equality Court ruling about particular October 2022 remarks**, not a criminal judgment on every protest song. Linked the [SAHRC primary announcement](https://sahrc.org.za/index.php/sahrc-media/news-2/item/4368-media-statement-equality-court-upholds-sahrc-s-complaint-against-mr-julius-malema-and-the-eff) and independent [News24 same-day report](https://www.news24.com/southafrica/crime-and-courts/malema-found-guilty-of-hate-speech-for-2022-rally-remarks-20250827-0770).
15. **Interpretation across conclusion** — The editorial can robustly ask why threatened Afrikaners receive less visible institutional support without presenting an unverified network's motives as scientifically proven. Source dates, denominators and limits now appear within affected paragraphs rather than buried only in a general note.
