## Corrections made

1. **ANC US terrorism treatment, falsely called formal FTO designation.** The article claimed the State Department formally designated the ANC a Foreign Terrorist Organization until 2008. The [8 April 2008 State Department FTO roster](https://2001-2009.state.gov/s/ct/rls/fs/08/103392.htm) has no ANC listing. The original [Public Law 110-257 enacted 1 July 2008](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-110publ257/html/PLAW-110publ257.htm) instead describes relief from *immigration-law* treatment as a terrorist organisation for specified ANC anti-apartheid conduct and admissibility exemptions. These are distinct statutory mechanisms; the law does not say an INA §219 FTO designation was revoked. Independent historical analysis: [TIME, 2018](https://time.com/5338569/nelson-mandela-terror-list/). The corrected article preserves documented violent MK activity but treats the asserted exclusive Israeli/PLO causal motive behind ADL positions as **UNVERIFIED** without contemporaneous ADL records.
2. **Two unrelated Malema proceedings conflated.** On [27 August 2025 the Equality Court issued a civil hate-speech ruling about specified October 2022 remarks](https://sahrc.org.za/index.php/sahrc-media/news-2/item/4368-media-statement-equality-court-upholds-sahrc-s-complaint-against-mr-julius-malema-and-the-eff). The SAHRC clarified a *separate relief stage remained*; this was not a prison sentence or criminal conviction. On [16 April 2026 Malema was sentenced to imprisonment for the distinct 2018 firearms incident](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/16/south-african-politician-julius-malema-sentenced-to-prison-for-firing-gun); [eNCA's same-day explainer](https://www.enca.com/news-top-stories/explainer-malema-granted-leave-appeal-sentence-not-conviction) addresses appeal scope. The comparison-table cell now differentiates the civil ruling from criminal firearms sentence. Its claim that ADL issued no case-specific reply is **UNVERIFIED** absent comprehensive archives.
3. **IHRA misquote and misplaced legal inference — two locations corrected.** Previous copy replaced the word “Israel” with “a group” in an illustrative double-standards example, then asserted IHRA had effectively condemned ADL's alleged Afrikaner discrimination. The actual [IHRA non-legally binding working definition](https://holocaustremembrance.com/resources/working-definition-antisemitism) frames the example in relation to *Israel and other democratic nations*. Its text does not adjudicate ADL conduct or create an Afrikaner legal ruling. Both article paragraphs now accurately identify the original scope and explicitly say that the equal-treatment argument is an **editorial analogy**, to be judged by documented statements. Independent perspective available via [ADL's own 2018 published article](https://education.adl.org/resources/article/racist-obsession-south-african-white-genocide), which does acknowledge some white farm murders.
4. **Funding causation / group profiling.** Removed the unsupported categorical conclusion that ADL's nonrecognition of Afrikaner concerns is an intentional donor-revenue mechanism or that Afrikaners cannot be donors. The reader now sees the institutional incentive question as **UNVERIFIED** pending grants, donor constraints, board notes, case-by-case complaint records and real moderation actions. This protects the serious consistency inquiry from attributing shared motives to all donors in any religion/ethnic group.
