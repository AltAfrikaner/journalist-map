## Primary records and independent checks

- **Original US tax-disclosure rule:** The [IRS's public-return guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/public-disclosure-and-availability-of-exempt-organizations-returns-and-applications-contributors-identities-not-subject-to-disclosure) states that public charities generally need not publicly disclose donors' names and addresses in annual Schedule B; independent [legal review](https://fedlaws.org/form-990-schedule-b-who-files-what-to-report-and-disclosure/) discusses the same public-record limits. Thus no source in this investigation establishes a complete ADL donor demographic census, ethnic membership totals or that donors caused a particular political response.
- **Original JLens website:** Its [institutional overview](https://www.jlensnetwork.org/overview/) describes **40 institutions / $15B represented assets** *as of 31 March 2026*. In tension with that snapshot date, JLens/ADL's [24 June 2026 announcement](https://www.adl.org/resources/press-release/jlens-jewish-investor-network-welcomes-conference-presidents-major-american) welcomes its "40th institutional member." Independent [24 June finance republication](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/jlens-jewish-investor-network-welcomes-120000443.html) matches the June announcement. Neither implies ADL or JLens directly controls $15B of investments, and $400M should not be relabelled a charitable grant to ADL.
- **Specific governance actions:** [JLens/ADL's 28 May 2025 announcement](https://www.adl.org/resources/press-release/jlens-and-adl-announce-proxy-advisors-support-meta-shareholder-proposal) describes Meta Proposal 8 and proxy-adviser support. [ADL/JLens's 30 March 2026 GE Aerospace statement](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/GE/adl-and-j-lens-urge-ge-aerospace-shareholders-to-vote-against-d73b1xpnthbc.html) describes opposition to Proposal 7. Independent [TipRanks summary](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/adl-jlens-urge-ge-aerospace-shareholders-vote-against-proposal-7) corroborates the latter. Statements of advocacy are not proof that Meta implemented a given censorship action or that GE adopted the group's preference.
