### Primary and independent/counter sources

1. **Meta 2022 primary report**: https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2022/07/Meta_Human-Rights-Report-July-2022.pdf — the report explicitly ties “95%” to *viewers not clicking through after seeing a label*, not a 95% measured reach reduction. The report concerns specific historical policy and cannot be generalized to every South African account in 2026.
2. **PolitiFact independent account, 7 January 2025**: https://politifact.com/article/2025/jan/07/meta-ending-third-party-fact-checking-partnership/ — explains Meta's own role and the US scope of January 2025 announced changes. Independent corroboration of the distinction, not proof of neutral enforcement.
3. **Africa Check institutional primary statement**: https://africacheck.org/who-we-are — separate trust; location at Wits Journalism. Its self-description is not an independent audit or proof of absence of influence.
4. **Wits university Council**: https://www.wits.ac.za/about-wits/governance/council/ — formal authority governing the university, not an all-platform command mandate.
5. **Wits Health Consortium governance/ownership**: https://www.witshealth.co.za/About-WHC/Governance and https://www.witshealth.co.za/About-WHC/Who-we-are — university company with separate board; contrary evidence to both “all unrelated” and “all directly controlled.”
6. **Meta announced changes, 7 January 2025**: https://about.fb.com/news/2025/01/meta-more-speech-fewer-mistakes/ — US-specific start; no proof that all non-US third-party checks ended then.
