## Explicit source/claim corrections
1. **R11.76bn “total annual budget” → R13.06bn consolidated Wits Group FY2025 income.** Wits University's original 2025 audited integrated financial report (published June 2026; CFO overview pp. 105–106 of the report, PDF pp. 4–5) identifies **R13.06bn Group income**. It comprises different categories: state grants/subsidies R2.88bn, tuition R3.14bn, other income R2.24bn and research contracts R3.24bn. This is **not** an annual international-grant figure or proof the previous R11.76bn was a university-wide operating budget. Repaired hero/dashboard and funding lead.
2. **US$100m+ allegedly arriving from abroad each year → UNVERIFIED.** Must reconcile grant identifiers, award dates, receivable accounting, audited group/entities and cash-drawdown years before reporting a true annual total.
3. **Gates–WHC “US$80m in 2024–25”** is independently reported as approximately **47 grant commitments over two years**, awarded to Wits Health Consortium, a wholly owned Wits entity. The Gates Foundation primary grants register itself describes **commitments**, not payments. The article no longer presents all awards as 2025 cash paid to Wits, or donor spending as immunity from scrutiny. **Grant-ID list / ranking UNVERIFIED**.
4. **350m affected people / 81,780 citations / universal newsroom training**: applied visible **UNVERIFIED** to unsupported effects and datasets, separating real institutional links from implied population-wide coercion, a reproducible citation export and universal media employment.
5. **EU Digital Services Act / “jurisdictional immunity”**: the DSA applies to regulated intermediary services offered in EU markets **even when providers are elsewhere**; the independent fact-checking partner cannot automatically be assigned Meta's platform reporting/audit obligations merely by moving headquarters. The original geographic-immunity deduction was wrong.
6. **Five-to-eightfold cost ratio and no SA scrutiny**: no comparable research-project cost denominators, absent enforcement records, and no basis for claims that no SA journalist, court, regulator or opposition organisation could investigate. These are now explicitly investigative hypotheses rather than established findings.
