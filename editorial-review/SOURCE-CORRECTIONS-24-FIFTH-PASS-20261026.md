## Corrections

1. Replaced the unreplicated **"75% Replaced. 0% Consent."** declarative headline in the article metadata, hero and homepage with a critical, source-answerable question about the 1993 interim/1996 final texts. The percentage was an unpublished scoring hypothesis, while the 1996 Constitution followed representative adoption and Constitutional Court certification rather than a direct popular vote. The lack of a referendum cannot itself establish zero political or legal consent.
2. Corrected the single-day **27 April 1994** claim to the election period **26–29 April 1994**. The contemporary compiled election results put national turnout at approximately **19,726,610 including 193,112 invalid/blank ballots**, and **19,533,498 valid votes**. The 1994 election chose representatives; it did not present the as-yet unwritten 1996 Constitution for a vote.
3. Qualified the 75% donut and blanket zero-survival explanation, which cannot be called measured facts without a consistent clause-matching method. Final sections 6, 25, 29–31 and 235 retain qualified rights while certain interim safeguards were materially restructured. Whether the revised language, property and communal self-determination rights meet Afrikaner expectations remains a defensible editorial debate.
4. Replaced the unsupported "every measurable indicator" socioeconomic assertion with warnings on definition, dates, denominators and causal inference. No historical series was silently rewritten. Race-law totals, unemployment, violence and income/growth comparisons remain UNVERIFIED absent source-matched evidence.
5. Revised the homepage description to distinguish 1994 ballots, the May 1996 draft, initial refusal of judicial certification, amendments and final certification; disputed headline percentages flagged as UNVERIFIED.
