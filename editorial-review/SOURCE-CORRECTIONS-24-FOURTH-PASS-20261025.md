## Corrections and reasons
1. Separated the 8 May 1996 first draft (421–2, 10 abstentions) from the final operative text. The Constitutional Court rejected parts of the May text on 6 September; the Assembly adopted an amended text 11 October; certification followed 4 December and commencement 4 February 1997.
2. Corrected the high-visibility NP vote and withdrawal sequence. NP supported the May text while objecting to future power-sharing limits and several other issues; de Klerk announced withdrawal on 9 May effective 30 June. This is not proof that every one of the article's fourteen supposed protections disappeared without replacement.
3. Corrected the unsupported exact claim that community self-determination lost '83.3%' of textual protection. The dedicated interim Volkstaat Council was not continued as such, but final section 235 recognises the possibility of community self-determination, including territorial arrangements determined by national legislation; it does not itself establish one.
4. Recast the '100% Asymmetry Index' assertion and its chart caption as a **UNVERIFIED editorial hypothesis**, not a replicated statistical result. No chart data or original quantitative datasets silently changed; full clause mapping is required before percentages may be certified.
5. Seven scoped prose, context and infographic-caption edits only. Original JS, SVG, img/media, all IDs and existing URLs preserved.
