**Red-team challenge:** A critic can legitimately argue that losing compulsory 1993 power-sharing and language non-diminution was substantial, even if courts subsequently certified the final Constitution and other rights survive. Court certification is *not* a 1996 national referendum; nor does a 2024 coalition recreate mandatory 1993 Cabinet seats. Conversely, the removal of particular interim clauses does not by itself prove 100% ANC gains, no enforceable minority protections, a 145-statute inventory or specific state-capture causation. Both positions require the constitutional text, implementing laws, party proposal registers and actual outcomes. The article retains its criticism of the settlement while clearly separating law, measurement and political interpretation.
