## Corrections and evidence class
1. **No prior equality redress — FALSE:** The 1993 interim Constitution already authorised measures designed to improve the position of persons disadvantaged by unfair discrimination in **section 8(3)(a)**. The 1996 section 9(2) builds on rather than originates the concept. The 2004 Constitutional Court *Van Heerden* judgment supplies judicial treatment.
2. **Procurement did not exist — FALSE:** The old section 187 established independent, impartial tender boards and fair, public, competitive procurement. The 1996 section 217 reformulated procurement, including express categories of preference. The 2006 Constitutional Court *Steenkamp* judgment quotes the two provisions side by side. Clarified the old-versus-new narrative and one HTML comparison-table row. R1.8tn alleged annual fiscal base and 122-statute causal claim remain **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **Universal 12/12 NDR alignment — UNVERIFIED inference:** Two prominent paragraphs presented the author's selected 12-item table as independently proven exhaustive classification and said the final text was not negotiated. Recast this as an editorial hypothesis pending exhaustive coding, counterexamples, original party submissions and the Court's binding Constitutional Principles/certification record. The table's existing model remains preserved, not certified.
4. **Every deletion removed a protection — UNVERIFIED:** Changed categorical direction-of-effect into an evidence-sensitive comparison recognising retained/new rights as well as lost guarantees. Narrowed race-law causal claims; recorded absence of a consistently dated legislative register. The pro-Afrikaner inquiry into minority safeguards and power-sharing remains intact.
