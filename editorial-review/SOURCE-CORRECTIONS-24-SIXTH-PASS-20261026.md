## Scoped factual corrections

1. **Zero-Afrikaans-university insinuation corrected.** A prominent 2026 display implied no Afrikaans instruction in public universities. Stellenbosch University's current official guidance explicitly confirms Afrikaans and English as languages of learning and teaching, with programme-dependent Afrikaans classes/assessments. In **Gelyke Kanse v Stellenbosch University** ([2019] ZACC 38), the Constitutional Court upheld the 2016 policy prioritising wider English access while recognising the continued Afrikaans option and concerns about minority-language erosion. This does **not** mean every public degree can be studied fully in Afrikaans; whether any exclusively Afrikaans public university existed in 2026 requires a defined, comprehensive census: **UNVERIFIED**. The visual display now conveys both points without editing underlying graphic data.
2. **Objective-scorecard inference corrected.** The categorical prose asserted an ANC 14/14 win, NP 0/12, all non-ANC parties 0%, and an historically unique asymmetry as established numerical fact. The party objective classification lacks a published source register, coding rubric and independent replication. Reclassified those figures in body prose and adjacent chart caption as **UNVERIFIED author's scores**, keeping the disputed plotted chart unchanged pending transparent data. The 8 May 1996 adoption was **421 in favour, two against, ten abstentions** per independent contemporaneous UPI reporting, indicating cross-party adoption but not unanimous satisfaction or a referendum. This matters for claims that the 1996 text was never negotiated.
3. **"Every row" assumption qualified.** The comparison of interim and final constitutional provisions now invites clause-level text/case-law verification rather than asserting that every final provision serves an opposing purpose.
