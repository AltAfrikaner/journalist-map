## Specific corrections

1. **Homepage archive numbering:** dossier displayed #23, corrected to #24 to agree with the 31-card order. Does not change URL, dataset or any other investigation.
2. **17 March 1992 referendum:** contemporary final tallies are **1,924,186 yes** and **875,619 no** of **2,799,805 valid votes**; the page's “2.3 million whites vote for” was incorrect. The original question permitted continued negotiation, not a vote on the eventual 1996 text. The reported **62% Afrikaner yes** was an analyst's estimate in contemporaneous reporting, *not* an official vote by home-language field. (The 68.73% computed from the printed tallies differs slightly from contemporaneous media's rounded 68.6%; no claim of precise subgroup census is made.)
3. **1994 vote:** an April 1994 election of representatives under the interim law is not a referendum endorsing all interim clauses or a final constitution adopted two years later. The story may question substantive public consent while accurately describing representative and court-certification procedure.
4. **8 May 1996:** contemporaneous recorded **421 yes / 2 no / 10 abstaining** refers to Assembly's proposed text. Certification was first denied in parts and amended final text was certified 4 December. ANC “14/14” and NP “0/12” party achievement and 75% replacement figures require original party submissions, coding definitions and independent replication: **UNVERIFIED**, not measured verdicts.
5. **9 May National Party exit:** **announcement** on 9 May 1996 was effective **30 June 1996**, not immediate termination. Repaired timeline; compulsory Cabinet-seat entitlement's discontinuation is still a legitimate constitutional criticism.
6. **4 February 1997:** final Constitution commenced but **Schedule 6** retained specified interim provisions; “1993 Constitution entirely ceased to exist as law” was too absolute.
7. **8 May/24 July 2026 Phala Phala:** apex court set aside Parliament's December 2022 referral-blocking vote and referred the Section 89 report to inquiry. It **did not find Ramaphosa guilty or command removal**. The Western Cape High Court **temporarily barred public hearings on 24 July 2026** pending review; merits disposition requires the latest docket. Corrected prominent thirty-year parallel without erasing it.
8. **Procurement/state capture:** original 1993 **s187** tender safeguards preceded 1996 **s217**. Alleged **R1.8tn/year** and **R250bn+ attributed to the Zondo Commission** remain without matched fiscal-year denominator and specific commission passages; no single constitutional clause can be identified as having channelled both values. ## Red-team and publication gates

But an election is not a direct constitutional referendum, absence of referendum does not prove zero representative assent, new s217 did not create procurement ex nihilo, and a referral for inquiry is not a misconduct judgment.
