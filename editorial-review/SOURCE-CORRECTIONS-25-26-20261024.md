Primary sources: [Executive Order 14204, US Federal Register, 12 Feb 2025](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-02-12/html/2025-02630.htm); [Ninth Circuit `Pacito v Trump` opinion, 5 March 2026](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2026/03/05/25-1939.pdf); [IRAP lawyer-side docket chronology](https://refugeerights.org/news-resources/pacito-v-trump-challenging-trumps-suspension-of-usrap); [CWS plaintiff statement, 8 Apr 2026](https://cwsglobal.org/press-releases/refugees-challenge-discriminatory-use-of-admissions-program/). Independent sources: [Daily Maverick via allAfrica, 19 Aug 2025 — job losses and visa applications](https://allafrica.com/stories/202508200614.html); [Associated Press, Dec 2025 — immigration enforcement and diplomatic dispute](https://apnews.com/article/17dd9bf8596eb1b2cf8c1545c368e982); [Reuters, 22 Sep 2026 — policy preference and proposed admissions](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-administration-admit-primarily-afrikaners-under-new-refugee-proposal-2026-09-22/).

**Right of reply / red-team:** CWS maintains that the refugee admissions suspension harmed people seeking lawful protection, while plaintiffs challenge Afrikaner-favoring exceptions as discriminatory. The US administration prioritises Afrikaners on stated anti-discrimination grounds; South Africa disputes those grounds and treated the Kenyan visa situation as an immigration-law enforcement matter; US authorities protested. The disagreement is not resolved by equating plaintiff counsel, government contractor and a media network. Source-led criticism of why CWS handled staffing as it did remains legitimate.

**Numeric/method audit:** `100` and `30` are approximate reported job/visa-planning quantities, not verified individual names. Seven arrested are the December 2025 enforcement event, not proof of motive. Historical `$520m`/half-billion funding claims are **UNVERIFIED as to accounting period, award vs disbursement and contracting entity**, and have been omitted from the current hero/meta description pending ledger-based reconciliation. Source records should identify grant programme, fiscal period, prime/subrecipient, obligations versus outlays. Government visa admission totals and persons assisted also require independent denominators. No personalised access decisions can be inferred from these totals.
