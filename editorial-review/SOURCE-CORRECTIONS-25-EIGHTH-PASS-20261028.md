## Primary and independent evidence

- [IRR Index of Race Law](https://racelaw.co.za/) (last published index update 11 June 2025): **324** Acts ever counted, **122** adopted since 1994, **145** still on the statute book; notes **nine deracialised** Acts within the 145. These categories are not interchangeable.
- [Martin van Staden's 25 September 2026 explanation, published 14 October](https://www.lexlibertas.org.za/media/race-law-in-south-africa-an-american-brief) describes **136** of the remaining Acts as racially operative under his statutory definition. [Newsday, 26 October 2026](https://newsday.co.za/south-africa/30130/145-race-laws-in-south-africa-of-which-38-were-adopted-after-apartheid-ended/) independently reports this presentation.
- [eNCA, 24 March 2025](https://www.enca.com/videos/discussion-real-truth-behind-142-sa-race-laws-claims) documents criticism of the index's interpretation of remedial legislation. A statutory-reference count is not a court judgment that all counted laws are unlawful or discriminatory in effect.
