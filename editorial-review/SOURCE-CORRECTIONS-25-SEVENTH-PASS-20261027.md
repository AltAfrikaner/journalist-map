## Claim-level verification and contrary record

1. **Actual 2025 opposition to racial targets (primary record):** [FW de Klerk Foundation, 28 May 2025, issued by Christo van der Rheede](https://fwdeklerk.org/race-classifications-wont-fix-the-injustices-of-the-past-de-klerk-foundation-tells-ramaphosa/) criticises official race classification and employment-equity targets and calls for data-driven needs-based upliftment. It refutes a categorical claim that the 2024–26 leadership abandoned all race-target criticism.
2. **Documented 2026 dispute, not a proved abandonment:** [Van der Rheede's own 28 May 2026 commentary](https://www.thecommonsense.co.za/Editorials/why-does-hermann-pretorius-continue-fabricated-version) distinguishes pre-1994 exclusionary laws from post-1994 measures under constitutional equality section 9(2), while opposing permanent racial bureaucracy. [Hermann Pretorius, 30 May 2026, independent critical response](https://www.thecommonsense.co.za/Politics/problem-your-antidote-race-laws-mr-van-der-rheede) challenges that legal classification and argues for a strict inventory of all race-based legislation. These opposing public arguments are evidence of a **substantive disagreement** about what counts as a race law, not evidence that a German foundation or trustee directed a policy reversal. Neither source by itself substantiates a 118-law denominator, the practical operation of every measure, or a complete 1999–2026 Foundation publication census.
3. **Posthumous award chronology (primary institutional notice):** [Foundation's 16 December 2025 announcement](https://fwdeklerk.org/the-fw-de-klerk-foundation-is-pleased-to-announce-that-the-late-dr-franklin-sonn-will-posthumously-be-awarded-the-2025-fw-de-klerk-goodwill-award/) describes its late-2025 choice and planned 2026 presentation. It does **not** document an orchestrated UWC career succession, donor capture or motives hidden behind the award.
