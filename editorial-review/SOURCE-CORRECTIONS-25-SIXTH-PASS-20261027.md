## Primary, independent and contrary evidence

1. **Farm murders versus farm attacks, and competing series:** [News24's 31 May 2018 contemporary account of AgriSA's report](https://www.news24.com/farm-murder-rate-lowest-in-20-years-remoteness-the-reason-for-brutality-20180531) documents **66 to 47 farm murders** (2016/17 to 2017/18) while **reported attacks rose 478 to 561**. In [News24's 28 June contemporaneous coverage](https://www.news24.com/SouthAfrica/News/police-and-agrisa-wrong-about-decline-in-farm-murders-afriforum-20180628), AfriForum **disputed the fall** using its own estimates and six-month comparison. These are different counts, windows and definitions. A reader must not infer motive from a contested statistical estimate; the specific claim that a former AgriSA executive deliberately promoted an ANC line was unsupported. The time-span and victim-group methodology remain to be independently reconciled.
2. **2026 Foundation rights work:** [Foundation's 16 September 2026 report-card announcement](https://fwdeklerk.org/foundation-report-card-highlights-implementation-gap-in-cultural-religious-and-linguistic-rights/) records **11 assessed cultural, religious and linguistic rights: four improved, four stable, three deteriorated**. Its own assessment is primary evidence of publication and institutional criticism of implementation; it is not a neutral independent assessment of all rights outcomes. Independent [NovaNews, 30 September 2026](https://novanews.co.za/fw-de-klerk-foundation-van-der-rheede-resigns-immediately-as-executive-director/) confirms Van der Rheede's departure, and [Polity, 21 September 2026](https://www.polity.org.za/article/fw-de-klerk-foundation-distances-itself-from-outgoing-directors-us-policy-comments-2026-09-21) independently describes the board/director dispute. His individual remarks cannot automatically be treated as a subsequent board resolution.
3. **German bilateral support:** [South African National Treasury/Department of Government Communications, 3 December 2024](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/south-africa-and-germany-strengthen-development-cooperation-eur2765-million) identifies Berlin talks **25–27 November 2024**, a **EUR276.5m commitment for 2024–2026**, not a one-year transfer to the FW de Klerk Foundation. [SAnews, 3 December 2024](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/south-africa-germany-strengthen-development-cooperation) separately reports that statement. Neither release proves any payment to a trustee, KAS or Foundation; current donor rankings would require a dated OECD definition and are not asserted here.

## Red-team and outstanding evidence

A critic can still argue that the Foundation's approach to Afrikaner safety weakened under changed leadership. Nothing in this pass dismisses that viewpoint. But the documents show a real board/director disagreement, continued rights publications and divergent farm-violence measurement; they do **not** establish German direction, agency capture or personal political motives. **UNVERIFIED:** grant agreements and restrictions, trustee minutes and votes, NDR-policy output comparison by year, constitutional-litigation outcomes, all network/diagram relationships and 118-law statistical denominator. A range of possible explanations—leadership judgement, legal strategy, donor incentives, internal disagreement—must be tested against records rather than selected by insinuation.
