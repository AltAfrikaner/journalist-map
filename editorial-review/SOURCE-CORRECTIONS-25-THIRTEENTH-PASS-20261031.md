## Evidentiary finding and correction

1. **Commencement is not assent, and neither is delivery.** The Constitution Eighteenth Amendment Act 3 of 2023 recognising South African Sign Language was assented to in July 2023, but **Proclamation 321, Government Gazette 54901, 26 June 2026**, brought it into force with immediate effect. Subsequent publication corrected the missing signature details; that is not grounds for casually asserting all public services immediately became accessible.
2. **Foundation-specific minority-language action, contrary to categorical inactivity.** The Foundation's **16 September 2026** contemporaneous report-card release proposed ring-fenced language budgets, stronger Pan South African Language Board (PanSALB) compliance powers, and urgent court-interpreter capacity. This is source-authenticated evidence of concrete constitutional-language advocacy during Van der Rheede's tenure, contrary to a blanket claim that all work on minority-language rights stopped.
3. **Independent corroboration and important limits.** Fouzia van der Fort's **17 September 2026** independently reported launch account identifies the same funding, enforcement and interpreter proposals and records the implementation gap. Foundation recommendations are **not** enacted budgets, amended PanSALB powers, or proven improvement in Afrikaans-language access. The causal chain from advocacy to realised services, and the actual change in provision across courts, universities and public agencies, remain **UNVERIFIED**.
