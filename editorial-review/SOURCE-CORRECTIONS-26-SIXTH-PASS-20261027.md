## Primary records and independent checks

- **Corrected delegation date and signatories:** [CMEP's original 27 April 2026 statement release](https://cmep.salsalabs.org/prfreedomday04272026) gives **15–26 April 2026** for its South Africa church delegation. The separate, contemporary [Reformed Church in America report](https://www.rca.org/rca-delegation-in-south-africa-listens-and-re-commits-to-the-message-of-the-belhar-confession-with-ecumenical-partners/) independently confirms that visit window and identifies the RCA participants and meetings. The **27 April date is the joint statement's issue date**, not the day the delegation was first sent. Note: a narrative sentence within the joint statement describes a broader **19–27 April** visit, which is not fully reconciled with the release's 15–26 dates. Specific individual itineraries remain **UNVERIFIED**.
- **CWS membership versus signatory authority:** The official [CMEP team and membership directory](https://cmep.org/team/) identifies **Church World Service — George Devendorf**. The 27 April joint statement names representatives of **CMEP, RCA, SACC, URCSA and the Dutch Reformed Church**; it does **not** list CWS as an institutional signatory. Independent [Kerkbode's 27 April archive](https://kerkbode.christians.co.za/2026/04/27/elders-gesien-ng-kerk-praat-saam-met-kerke-in-vryheidsdagverklaring-oor-onreg-in-gasa-en-vals-narratief-oor-wit-volksmoord-in-sa/) preserves that named-signatory list. This establishes membership in the coalition, not a specific vote, funding instruction or participation by CWS in that delegation.
- **Substance of the contested public statement:** The signed declaration linked a rejection of the "white genocide" narrative with discussion of Gaza/Palestine. That overlap is a valid editorial subject. **It does not establish** that CMEP's CWS member directed the statement, that CWS made a case-level refugee decision for political reasons, or that CWS organised unrelated Palestinian flights. [ACI Africa, 28 April 2026](https://www.aciafrica.org/index.php/amp/news/21483/dismantle-systems-of-apartheid-christian-groups-launch-call-on-south-africas-freedom-day) independently confirms the church grouping and thematic joint declaration, without attributing it to CWS's management.
