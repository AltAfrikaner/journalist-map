## Claim-level corrections and corroboration

1. **Executive Order 14204 scope.** The article categorically described the **7 February 2025** order as creating a programme for *all white South Africans*. Section 4 of the [official Federal Register order](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-02-12/html/2025-02630.htm) expressly prioritises **Afrikaners in South Africa who are victims of unjust racial discrimination**, subject to law; section 5 disclaims a legally enforceable individual entitlement. Independently, [Reuters, 22 September 2026](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-administration-admit-primarily-afrikaners-under-new-refugee-proposal-2026-09-22/) reported that the later administration policy remained principally focused on Afrikaners. The existence of later admissions or guidance for other white or non-white minorities **cannot** be inferred from the wording of the February order; exact eligibility rules and individual outcomes **UNVERIFIED** here.
2. **Unsupported 7 March CWS instruction.** The prior narrative said the State Department ordered CWS to resume South African refugee processing on **7 March 2025** and CWS agreed the same day. The [original presidential 7 March public statement](https://iptp-production.s3.amazonaws.com/media/documents/2025.03.07_Trump_Offers_South_African_Farmers_Expedited_Citizenship_-_Truth_Social.pdf) concerned a **proposed rapid US citizenship pathway** for South African farmers and families, corroborated independently by [Reuters on 7 March](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-stopping-all-federal-funding-south-africa-trump-says-2025-03-07/). Neither proves any State–CWS agency correspondence, agreement or processing decision; those assertions are now flagged **UNVERIFIED** rather than repeated as facts.
3. **Different legal responsibilities.** The original 10 February 2025 *Pacito* suit challenged the broader refugee admissions suspension under EO 14163; the subsequent 2026 amended complaint addresses Afrikaner priority. A contractor's litigation advocacy does **not** itself establish agency-level eligibility decisions or denials against specific families. Existing substantive caution and source links remain.
