## Editorial rule and adversarial challenge
Preserve a sceptical pro-Afrikaner investigation of NGO funding, institutional influence and speech governance, but do not convert a grant into a command, a submitted complaint into a platform takedown, or a historical relationship into contemporaneous control without case-level evidence. Read each institutional source as a primary record of what its author discloses, not as proof of its asserted impartiality. Independently check numerators, dates, legal jurisdiction and contested interpretation. The red-team case is that outcome counts, grant restrictions and actual removal decisions may be markedly different from the high-level network map. The counter-case is that formal independence alone does not rule out coordinated grants or influence; the allegations remain testable. No allegation of illegality, personal direction or proven systemic bias was added.
