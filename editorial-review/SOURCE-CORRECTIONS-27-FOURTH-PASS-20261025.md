## Primary documents and independent checks

1. **Primary US inspector general:** [State Department OIG, AUD-FA-26-17, July 2026](https://www.stateoig.gov/report/aud-fa-26-17), transferred 1,504 awards, $51.5bn obligated, >$86bn total projected, 17 receiving offices/bureaus.
2. **Independent contemporaneous check:** [Reuters, 27 July 2026](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/watchdog-finds-staffing-guidance-gaps-transfer-usaid-programs-2026-07-27/) reports the same OIG snapshot and additional oversight weaknesses, rebutting any inference that transferred programmes were necessarily administered seamlessly.
3. **Primary funder/recipient documentation:** [OGP 2022 Annual Report](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/ogp-2022-annual-report/), showing annual **aggregate** revenue categories and a named donor roster; [OGP Transparency and Financial Information](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/about/transparency-financial-information/) lists donor-specific grant dates, currencies and amounts across different years.
4. **Independent programme document for co-funding context:** [Making All Voices Count programme archive](https://www.makingallvoicescount.org/) reports 178 grants (2013–2017); [IDS/Hivos programme year-4 report](https://www.makingallvoicescount.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Making-All-Voices-Count-Year-4-Semi-Annual-Report.pdf) distinguishes programme consortium, 12 focus countries and project categories. Nothing in the present correction treats the existence of joint funding as proof of instructions to individual journalists.
