## Corrections made
1. **Airport count and immigration status.** Earlier prose described approximately 130 'undocumented Palestinians' arriving in a purportedly Israeli-organised evacuation. The **13 November 2025** Border Management Authority statement records **153 Palestinian passport holders arriving**, **23 travelling onward**, and **130 admitted** under the **then-operative 90-day visa exemption**, after further verification and accommodation offered by Gift of the Givers. The later Home Affairs investigation and withdrawal of that exemption on **6 December 2025** are separate decisions. Whether each previously named airline/broker had the dossier's claimed legal role needs passenger manifests, charters and contracts; this remains **UNVERIFIED**. Neither standard entry under an exemption nor missing departure stamps means the travellers lacked valid passports.
2. **Wrong year.** The Part C introduction said 'late 2024'; the paired airport (13 November) and CWS enforcement (December) events were **2025**, roughly 33 days apart. Fixed the section year while retaining the comparison.
3. **Misleading bilateral analogy.** The comparison now distinguishes a short-stay exemption granted after checks from alleged work-condition breaches by Kenyan staff. The different legal frameworks alone cannot prove political bias; original immigration decisions and comparable-case treatment are required.
4. **Lawsuit-versus-church chronology.** The Episcopal Church letter was **12 May 2025** and specified winding down **Episcopal Migration Ministries' own federal grants** by September 2025, not every US/Anglican refugee service. The original **Pacito v. Trump** complaint was filed **10 February 2025**, challenging the January USRAP suspension; CWS/IRAP announced an amended complaint in **April 2026** addressing the Afrikaner priority. The original 'same month, funder financed a lawsuit to block the pathway' conflated the events and assigned unproved purpose to earlier grants.
5. **Causal restraint.** Historical Democracy Fund grants to CWS/IRAP, and overlapping Anglican/donor relationships, permit investigation into institutional incentives. They do **not** independently establish funding earmarked for a given pleading or funder-directed coordination. Such claims stay **UNVERIFIED**.
6. **Homepage consistency.** The #29 card no longer asserts a universal donor command or that identical NGOs 'quietly kept receiving USAID replacement funding' without recipient- and award-level proof.
