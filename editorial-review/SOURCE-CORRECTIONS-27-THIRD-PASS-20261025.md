## Corrections and adversarial findings
1. **US Episcopal decision wrongly assigned to worldwide Anglican Communion.** The previous opening and primer described the entire Communion's senior leadership as refusing Afrikaner refugee resettlement. A specific US denomination acted: Episcopal Presiding Bishop Sean Rowe announced on **12 May 2025** the intended end of that church's federal refugee-resettlement grants after the administration asked Episcopal Migration Ministries to resettle Afrikaner arrivals. NPR/Religion News Service reported Rowe's explanation and the intended wind-down by September 2025. No worldwide Anglican Communion resolution, instruction or collective refusal is established. Both prominent false generalisations were corrected, retaining the scrutiny of this real US decision.
2. **St Andrew's school affiliation mistaken for diocesan governance and philanthropy control.** The Episcopal Asset Map describes St Andrew's in Potomac as **independent**. Its own 2026 governance list names a board of trustees and an ex-officio bishop's representative. It is plainly Episcopal and diocesan-affiliated; these facts do not demonstrate that the Diocese of Washington operates the school's donations or directs Pierre Omidyar's giving. Two main-text paragraphs corrected; the reported historical donation itself awaits original donor-ledger reconciliation.
3. **Donor worldview/collective outcomes overstated as evidence.** Preserved the article's critical argument about ideological and grantmaking overlap, but marked a causal/instructional conclusion as **editorial inference**, not a finding that the Omidyars ordered a church, litigant or platform decision. Funding restrictions and communications needed to test that possibility remain UNVERIFIED.
