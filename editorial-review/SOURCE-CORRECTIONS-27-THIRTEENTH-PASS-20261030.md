## Primary-trail and independent-source checks

1. **Center UA versus Pact/USAID, 2012.** The article presented a direct *USAID 54%* grant and an exact `36% x $500,000 = roughly $200,000` Omidyar contribution. [Kyiv Post, 10 February 2014](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/9325), quoting the Center UA **2012 report**, describes **more than $500,000 total** and **54% via Pact Inc**, a USAID-funded implementing intermediary, and **nearly 36% via Omidyar Network**. It also names other donors. The directly paid source, accounting denominator, exact grant amount and asserted earmarking need the original audited Center UA statements and signed award instruments; those documents were **not retrieved**. At an illustrative *exact* $500,000, 36% = **$180,000**, not $200,000, while `more than $500,000` cannot yield a definitive dollar figure. Corrected the displayed narrative and marked the exact figures and operational commands **UNVERIFIED**.
2. **Independent adversarial reading.** [Emptywheel's contemporaneous March 2014 examination](https://emptywheel.net/2014/03/01/of-neo-fascists-and-smiley-face-neoliberals/) questions any causal leap from donor percentages to command of Ukrainian politics. A grant, fiscal intermediary or co-funding does not demonstrate a jointly ordered political operation. Retained the funding-network critique as analysis rather than established intent.
3. **Family chronology and inferred motive.** The article asserted the founding generation had a shared **first-hand memory** of the **1921 and 1953 coups** and that this determined its later philanthropic priorities. [PBS's Pierre Omidyar biography](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/theymadeamerica/whomade/omidyar_hi.html) records birth in **Paris in 1967**; [AP's historical 1953 account](https://apnews.com/article/6ae8700ec74ba7d2ff04a40aa3f1f4d7) distinguishes the 1953 coup from the **1979 revolution**. These dates alone rule out Pierre personally witnessing 1921 or 1953, and history does not independently authenticate his family's lived motives. Changed that passage to context and labelled inherited intention **UNVERIFIED**.
