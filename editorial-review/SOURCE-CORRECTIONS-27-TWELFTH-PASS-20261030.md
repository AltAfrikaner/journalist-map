## Claim-level correction

1. **False coalition name.** The entity card described PIIE as the “Programme on Independent and Investigative Election-reporting.” That is not the organisation's published name. [Moxii's 26 March 2026 launch announcement](https://moxiiafrica.org/media-releases/multi-stakeholder-partnership-on-information-integrity-in-south-african-elections-piie-launched-ahead-of-local-government-elections-2026) identifies **Partnership on Information Integrity in Elections (PIIE)** as a coalition steered by Moxii, Africa Check and SANEF. [SANEF's 26 June 2026 account](https://elections.sanef.org.za/2026/06/26/election-disinformation-is-a-newsroom-issue-piie-wants-to-help/) independently identifies the same leadership and information-sharing activities.
2. **Two institutions, not one enforcement agreement.** A later list erroneously attributed the Real411-to-IEC process to “PIIE memoranda.” The [operator-published IEC–Moxii–platform voluntary framework](https://elections.real411.org.za/downloads/iec-framework-of-cooperation-2026.pdf), already inspected in the 28 October tenth-pass review, and the [20 October IEC announcement reproduced by the South African government](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/iec-enters-partnership-counter-disinformation) concern a **separate IEC, Moxii and platform arrangement**; coalition partners Africa Check and SANEF are not thereby framework signatories. The operator-posted PDF has blank signature fields; an independently executed complete copy and service-level annexes remain **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **Cross-document attribution.** Replaced the incorrect bibliography description of “IEC PIIE memoranda” with the separate October framework. A shared institutional ecosystem is documented, but a donor/PIIE command into individual IEC referrals, court rulings or platform removals is **UNVERIFIED**.
