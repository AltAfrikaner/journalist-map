## Corrections to `real411.html`

1. **The 2026 election code's two different 36-hour duties:** The previous legal explainer summarised mandatory reports of suspected disinformation by covered political parties/candidates, but did not distinguish their separate obligation to **retract/correct material they distributed** within 36 hours of becoming aware. The added paragraph distinguishes those obligations from voluntary complaints by the public. Independent legal reporting specifies knowledge/reasonable-belief and election-related intent requirements for disinformation prohibited by the code; a filed complaint alone does not prove an offence.
2. **Procedural stages:** Intake of a suspected claim, Real411 assessment, possible IEC escalation, Electoral Court sanctions and discretionary platform action are distinct. None provides a denominator for actual 2026 takedown rates, error rates or viewpoint bias. Political-asymmetry allegations remain **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **Confidence labels on safeguard cards:** The **Public Archive** card had a "Confirmed safeguard" tag even though its own paragraph expressly says completeness is not independently audited. Changed tag to **Operator commitment; completeness UNVERIFIED**. The **Right of Appeal** card had the same confidence label despite unresolved actual case dispositions and current membership. Changed to **Published policy; actual outcomes UNVERIFIED**. Published procedures are real; complete implementation is not proven.
4. Left untouched the original source documents, grant counts, graph/diagram relationships, four-platform list and previous October partnership/update chronology.

## Red-team challenge and release hold

Election integrity measures have a legitimate anti-disinformation purpose and express protections for lawful criticism, including pro-Afrikaner political speech. Conversely, independence self-descriptions and prioritised platform access do not establish equal treatment. Both should be evaluated through published complaint IDs, dispositions, reasons, appeal reversals, reviewer conflicts, IEC referral rates and platform actions grouped by political viewpoint. **UNVERIFIED:** every-case archive completeness, appeals operation, individual 2026 legal decisions, alleged systematic suppression, signed annexes and donor restrictions.
