## Claim corrected

1. **Overbroad "official" portal description.** Both metadata and hero called Real411 South Africa's *official public-complaints portal for online misinformation*, without delimiting the legal instrument. The [Electoral Commission's official 8 September 2026 account](https://www.sanews.gov.za/node/84131) specifies the **4 September 2026 election Disinformation Code** under section 88(a) of the Municipal Electoral Act and **designates Real411 for suspected election-related disinformation**. This does not designate its separate general online-harms complaints service as a universal state regulator or grant the private operator binding content-removal power.
2. **Who has legal duties?** The code addresses **political parties, candidates and those acting for them** during the specified election period, ending at declaration of results. The public may use Real411 to report suspected election disinformation, but the party/candidate 36-hour correction/reporting duties do not thereby apply to every complainant. [Independent legal discussion, Polity, 29 September 2026](https://www.polity.org.za/article/the-electoral-code-of-conduct-on-countering-disinformation-2026-09-29) corroborates this narrower scope; [Moxii's own portal description](https://moxiiafrica.org/resources/online-tools) documents wider harm-reporting activity.
3. **Escalation is not enforcement.** The [IEC's 20 October partnership announcement](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/iec-enters-partnership-counter-disinformation) names Google, Meta, Microsoft, TikTok and Moxii; [East Coast Radio's independent reporting](https://www.ecr.co.za/news/news/iec-partners-to-tackle-election-disinformation/) quoted the operator on final-signature uncertainty. The page retains distinction between IEC referral, independently enforced platform rules and Electoral Court sanctions. Full signed framework, annexes, actual referral/removal rates, political balance and alleged discriminatory effect remain **UNVERIFIED**.

The on-page correction is substantive: the visible hero and introductory explanation now make the limited statutory mandate unmistakable, while the metadata accurately describes the broader privately run service.
