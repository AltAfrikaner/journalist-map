## Red-team and boundaries

Documented: the 2026 IEC cooperation was announced, platforms were named, and Moxii publicly describes a reviewer/lawyer procedure and appeals. **Not proven:** that every platform entered identical specific priority-processing terms, that private donors have governance over reviewers, that every complaint is referred, that a referral caused unjustified censorship or that identifiable political viewpoints are treated disproportionately. No post-level decisions, referral logs, original contracts, conflict declarations, matched control samples or full appeals archive examined. An independently governed review process may protect speech as well as restrict particular falsehoods; those competing outcomes must be measured rather than inferred from institutional overlap. A distinct statutory election-code pathway is not equivalent to the committee having court powers.
