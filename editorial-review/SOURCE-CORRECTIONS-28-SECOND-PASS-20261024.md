## Corrections made
1. **Actual escalation authority.** The 20 October 2026 IEC statement says Real411 **assesses and recommends**, the **IEC may refer** a suitable case to a platform, and the platform handles the referral under its own policies. The old assertion that every Real411-reviewed report automatically entered a 'priority queue no ordinary citizen can access' was **UNVERIFIED**: the public records provide no comparator queue metrics, automatic forwarding rate, or removal rate. The updated headline and article retain scrutiny of privileged institutional access while separating it from a compelled takedown. The October partner list also includes **Microsoft**.
2. **2026 framework chronology.** An older Moxii webpage contained 'signed' and 'still under negotiation' wording. The IEC announced a renewed agreement on **20 October 2026** (contemporaneous independent SABC report dated 19 October). Historic webpage uncertainty must not be repeated as the present status. The **executed agreement**, annexes and actual referral logs have not been inspected, however.
3. **PAIA correspondence.** The article reported **two requests** filed with the IEC, but acknowledgements and contents were not independently available in this pass. The requests are retained as a reported editorial action, **UNVERIFIED**, not represented as independently confirmed. PAIA has procedural requirements and exemptions, not automatic public posting.
4. **Parent versus project funding.** At least **$825,000** of cited historical grants went to parent Media Monitoring Africa/Moxii, not proven exclusively to Real411. The homepage card and hero now distinguish this from a project-level budget or donor control over an outcome.
5. **Trust governance.** Qualified the absolute 'no public ownership record exists' claim. Certified deed, contemporary trustees, and decision-rights information remain **UNVERIFIED** pending records from the Master or the organisation.
6. **Complaint publication and appeals.** Real411/Moxii publicly assert every complaint outcome is available and an appeal channel exists. This pass did **not** reconcile all reported case IDs to published outcomes; a blanket completeness claim therefore remains **UNVERIFIED**. Official procedure permits written appeals; named ongoing oversight by Justice Zak Yacoob was not independently corroborated as a current appointment.
7. **Homepage #30.** Corrected contradictory claims that no outcomes are published, unverified direct project funding, and guaranteed invisible fast-track queue. Original card link and archive position retained.

## Red-team and outstanding verification
Supporters emphasise an independent multi-reviewer process, publication commitments, statutory free-expression safeguards, appeal routes and platform autonomy. Critics rightly ask whether the IEC and selected partners receive asymmetric access, whether funded governance affects incentives, and whether public case outcomes reveal viewpoint-specific error rates. Resolve with signed MOUs/annexes, ICC/IEC referrals, all complaints versus public decisions and reversals, actual platform outcome data, signed funding terms, trustee and appeal membership records. Neither the presence of foreign grants nor advocacy positions by themselves prove individual censorship decisions or donor control. Named case outcomes and classification datasets remain **UNVERIFIED**.
