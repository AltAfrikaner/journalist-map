## Counterinterpretation and outstanding evidentiary standard

A partnership announcement is evidence of cooperation and prospective prioritisation, not conclusive evidence that a specific final instrument had been signed or that a platform complied with any particular referral. Bird's qualifier may reflect a particular annex or signing step rather than the absence of a valid IEC arrangement. Conversely, an assertion of independence by the operator does not establish nonpartisan treatment. **UNVERIFIED:** executed framework and version trail; disaggregated cumulative complaints; 2026 reviewer conflicts; complaint-to-decision, decision-to-IEC, IEC-to-platform and platform-removal/appeal counts; donor-project earmarks and any viewpoint-asymmetric error rate.
