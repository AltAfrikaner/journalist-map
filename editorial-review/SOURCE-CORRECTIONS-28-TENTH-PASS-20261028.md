## Primary document discovered
The [operator-hosted 10-page PDF](https://elections.real411.org.za/downloads/iec-framework-of-cooperation-2026.pdf) combines the IEC's **19 October 2026 media statement** with **six pages of Voluntary Framework of Cooperation**. The page's earlier “framework unavailable” narrative was outdated. **Clause 2.2.1:** Moxii first reviews public complaints, the IEC *may* refer a matter on finding cause for concern, and the platform undertakes at least to investigate and/or apply its own rules. This is a conditional procedural path, not automatic deletion. **Clause 3.3:** the framework expressly is **not legally binding and creates no contract or financial obligation**. **Clause 3.5:** individual platform content and enforcement decisions remain independently discretionary. **Clause 1.3.3:** commencement dates from last signatory; **3.1:** termination when election results officially declared. The public PDF has **blank name/date/signature fields** for the IEC, Moxii, Meta, Google, Microsoft and TikTok; that is not proof no separately signed copy exists.
