## Verified corrections and contradictions
1. **Prominent flow falsely guaranteed universal outcome publication.** Moxii Africa stated in its **10 September 2026** explanation that all decisions are published and appealable, and that three independent reviewers report to a lawyer. Real411's public procedure describes written appeal routes and promises accessible outcomes. Neither source is an independently reconciled record of every submission. The hero workflow, Stage 3/Stage 5 boxes, and governance table now distinguish the **operator's claim** from the unperformed submitted-ID-to-decision audit, marked **UNVERIFIED**.
2. **Named current appeals chair asserted without dated appointment.** Stage 5 and the governance table certified Justice Zak Yacoob as the current overseer while the article's evidence section already admitted this was **UNVERIFIED**. The operative procedure lists an Appeals Committee, not a currently verified named chair. Removed unwarranted present-tense certification; historical assertions and records remain reviewable.
3. **Reviewer pool described as current census.** The 30-plus number came from reporting in **May 2024**, while Moxii's September 2026 account describes three reviewers without independently supplying a new full roster or conflicts-of-interest declarations. Dated the 30-plus figure, preserved three-person process as an attributed operator account, and labelled actual 2026 composition **UNVERIFIED**.
4. **October 2026 partnership roster and agreement status.** Main open-record questions still named MMA/IEC/Meta/Google/TikTok but omitted **Microsoft**; the IEC confirmed all five partners on **20 October 2026** and says it may send prioritised platform referrals under each service's policy, not compulsory removals. Recast signed-framework and PAIA assertions as uninspected records, retaining the question whether the agreement affords asymmetric access.
