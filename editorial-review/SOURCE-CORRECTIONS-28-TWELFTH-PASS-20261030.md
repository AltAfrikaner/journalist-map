## Source hierarchy and substantive change

1. **Coalition versus signed-framework attribution.** The existing HTML network diagram stated **“PIIE FORMAL COOPERATION AGREEMENTS WITH”** and that PIIE **“operates under a Framework of Cooperation”** with platforms. The evidence supports two different structures: [Moxii's 26 March 2026 PIIE launch](https://moxiiafrica.org/media-releases/multi-stakeholder-partnership-on-information-integrity-in-south-african-elections-piie-launched-ahead-of-local-government-elections-2026), confirmed separately by [SANEF's 26 June explanation](https://elections.sanef.org.za/2026/06/26/election-disinformation-is-a-newsroom-issue-piie-wants-to-help/), describes the civil-society coalition; the [IEC's 20 October announcement](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/iec-enters-partnership-counter-disinformation), corroborated by [East Coast Radio's same-day reporting](https://www.ecr.co.za/news/news/iec-partners-to-tackle-election-disinformation/), names a **separate IEC–Moxii–Google–Meta–Microsoft–TikTok voluntary framework**. The operator's published framework was independently examined in the tenth-pass audit; a fully executed copy, appendices, priority-service metrics and any coalition-level independent signed agreements remain **UNVERIFIED**.
2. **Corrected the visible HTML diagram**, not merely an endnote: relabelled the framework row as a separate IEC–Moxii arrangement, restored **Microsoft** among the four named platform partners and replaced the unsupported PIIE framework-operation claim. The diagram now distinguishes information-sharing coordination from statutory IEC referrals and independent platform enforcement. All original SVG, 3D data and JS were preserved.
3. **Training denominator conflict:** [Moxii's own impact page](https://moxiiafrica.org/about/our-impact) simultaneously presents **700 journalists trained** in its headline metric and **600** in the accompanying programme description. [SANEF's 8 March 2026 announcement](https://sanef.org.za/sanef-iec-salga-mdda-and-moxii-africa-launch-nationwide-elections-training-for-journalists/) verifies a joint training programme, not the later final count. The graphic now identifies the numerical reconciliation as **UNVERIFIED**. Neither number is treated as a measure of PIIE platform removals.
