## Documents consulted, including contrary evidence
1. **OSF primary 2025 retrospective** [publication](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/publications/30-years-of-the-open-society-foundation-for-south-africa) and [PDF](https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/uploads/569f1000-68d9-4346-9a75-eca92d5f2b94/30-years-of-osf-south-africa-20250310.pdf), p.30: $238,635,789 1997–2023 (initial $15m endowment excluded). A self-reported figure, not independent audited grant-level reconciliation.
2. **Constitutionalism Fund first party** [overview](https://constitutionalismfund.co.za/) (more than 60 partners) vs [partners page](https://constitutionalismfund.co.za/our-partners/) (more than 200 organisations); the apparent inconsistency needs primary unique-recipient list and definition. [Atlantic Philanthropies' 5 February 2015 release](https://www.atlanticphilanthropies.org/news/new-25m-fund-sa-civil-society-groups-working-advance-constitutionalism) independently confirms original $25m launch rather than the later $29m programme.
3. **US Department of State Inspector General**, [AUD-FA-26-17](https://www.stateoig.gov/report/aud-fa-26-17), July 2026: 1,504 transferred awards under 17 State bureaus/offices (September 2025 snapshot); original and transferred sums have separate definitions.
4. **US GAO**, [GAO-26-108607](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108607), 30 September 2026: thousands of USAID awards terminated, some remaining contracts transferred; incomplete State oversight records. This rebuts both “all aid continues” and “all awards instantly stopped” readings.
5. **Luminate's own governance/financial disclosure portal**, [tax returns and audited accounts](https://luminategroup.com/our-governance). This supports existence of published filings, not completeness of every South African subgrant or a coordinated motive.
6. **South African Electoral Commission statement reproduced by official SAnews**, [20 October 2026](https://www.sanews.gov.za/node/85074): Real411 publicly accessible; IEC/platform referral roles distinct. **Independent corroboration:** [News24's 5 July 2023 agreement reporting](https://www.news24.com/politics/meta-tiktok-google-agree-to-help-iec-combat-election-disinformation-20230705) describes policy-dependent platform action, not automatic content removal.

## Adversarial red-team assessment
**Strongest adverse interpretation:** Donors publish grants because civil-society work and election integrity are their stated missions; co-funded organisations do not thereby demonstrate a central political-command structure. A funder may have policy preferences, but financial support alone cannot establish instructions to suppress Afrikaner speech. The named organisations and platforms retain legally and operationally distinct roles. Conversely, public filings need not reveal informal influence, precise grant conditions or every subrecipient; those remain appropriate investigative questions.

**Material limitations:** The legacy table's 73+35 rows, STATUS values, dates, sources, duplicated/overlapping entities and grant claims were **not** re-certified line by line. Tax forms show different legal entities and fiscal years. “STACKED” must be confirmed from contemporaneous accounts rather than a mixed-period total. No source has been located here proving the alleged exclusive US-private-replacement scheme, Real411 earmarking, or recipient-wide political control. No request for external right of reply was sent; public organisational disclosures provide preliminary contrary evidence only.
