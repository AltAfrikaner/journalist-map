## Red-team reading and editorial stance

**Direct documentary facts:** the three foundations jointly announced a $25m launch in 2015; donor ratification was specified **in that launch announcement**; the CF later described a committee-based grant procedure; GroundUp disclosed historical receipts and an independent review but no audit opinion; an independent reviewer recorded an NVF-specific deferred balance; OSF-SA described a legal/organisational transition. **Attributions:** OSF-SA's and recipients' own histories are institutional self-reporting; the 2026 amandla listing is likewise a self-report. **Inference/UNVERIFIED:** whether donor ratification produced political, editorial or platform instructions; whether funders restricted a particular article or litigation; whether the 2026 recipient line represents 2026 disbursement; whether inherited grant rows name the correct successor payor. Scrutiny of grant-selection rights and donor influence is legitimate. Equating grant authority with covert censorship would overstate the evidence, just as treating “independent” status as proof that funding had no influence would understate the issue.
