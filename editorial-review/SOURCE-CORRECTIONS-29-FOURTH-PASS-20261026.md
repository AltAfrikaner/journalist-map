## Independent red-team reading and preservation

A commissioned academic study does not require Africa Check to use a universal ratings taxonomy; a published methodology does not by itself prove that standards are applied impartially. Scrutiny of donor conditions, treatment of Afrikaner concerns and appeal processes is legitimate, but proving improper content suppression needs original rating records, platform notices, time and country rules, independently matched reach data and donor/editor correspondence. All such operational allegations remain **UNVERIFIED**. The IEC may refer a matter to the Electoral Court but the Real411 intake service does **not** itself wield statutory censorship authority.

**Preservation:** all original 73 OSF and 35 Omidyar array tuples, entire JavaScript blocks, SVG, CSS, image tags, embedded media, original DOM IDs and existing links preserved; historical PDF **unchanged**. The homepage card *description* alone changed; 31 routes remain identical. Corrected text includes new documentary links. The protected Rooivalk HTML remains untouched. This pass is a scoped editorial correction, not certification of all 108 entries.
