## Red-team / adverse reading

A donor or co-investor may favour a policy area, and independent South African media projects can reasonably disclose such funding while still controlling editorial decisions. The original article's three-channel narrative obscured that **Luminate is not the Open Society Foundation** and that **investment syndicate total is not a specific investor's amount**. Conversely, an absence of documentary proof of control cannot itself rule out undisclosed private conditions. Specific donor instructions, grant restrictions, content moderation notices, correlated IEC referrals and financial year-by-year transactions remain **UNVERIFIED**. The pro-Afrikaner rationale for independent scrutiny of influence and representation is retained; these records do not establish a conspiracy or instruction chain.
