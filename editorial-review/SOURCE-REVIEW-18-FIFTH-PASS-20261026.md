## Chronology: primary and independent records
- [UN Security Council Resolution 1929, 9 June 2010](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/683939): **binding prohibition on specified heavy conventional arms**, including attack helicopters, entering Iran. It cannot automatically be backdated to a purported **2009** transaction.
- [SIPRI sanctions archive, updated 16 October 2025](https://www.sipri.org/databases/embargoes/un_arms_embargoes/iran): in **December 2006**, specific nuclear/ballistic technologies were prohibited; **March 2007** included an arms-export ban *from* Iran and caution on supply *to* Iran; the broad prohibition on supplying major conventional weapons *to* Iran followed in **June 2010**. The UN embargo was subsequently amended and revived; dates and item classifications matter.
- [Parliamentary statement from opposition MP David Maynier, 14 March 2012](https://www.polity.org.za/article/da-statement-by-david-maynier-democratic-alliance-shadow-minister-of-defence-and-military-veterans-calling-on-the-ncacc-to-investigate-iran-helicopter-scandal-14032012-2012-03-14): alleged a **2009 Bell 212** transfer through a South African intermediary and requested investigation; an allegation by an opposition politician is **not** an official finding of breach.
- [Contemporaneous Mail & Guardian, 15 March 2012](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2012-03-15-committee-investigates-sa-iran-arms-claims/) independently reports NCACC inspectorate confirmation of an inquiry and Justice Minister Jeff Radebe's assertion that government did not authorise military equipment exports to Iran. The [28 March follow-up](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2012-03-28-state-endorsement-of-sa-firm-in-iran-to-be-probed/) documents a separate government inquiry into trade-support letters. Neither amounts to a final NCACC contravention finding.
- [Official Constitutional Court hearing collection, CCT 119/25, 121/25, 124/25](https://collections.concourt.org.za/items/fddc72ce-da2b-4990-bee1-8a541cd69633/full) records 31 August–1 September 2026 hearings over **jurisdictional/procedural issues** in the separate MTN–Turkcell matter. [Independent September reporting](https://iol.co.za/sunday-tribune/2026-09-06-concourt-hears-mtn-appeal-in-r74bn-turkcell-iran-dispute/) confirms judgment reserved then; no subsequently authenticated dispositive ruling was located in this review. No liability or exoneration inferred.
