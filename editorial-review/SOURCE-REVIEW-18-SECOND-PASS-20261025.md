## Corrected in the published HTML (five scoped text replacements)
1. **Aircraft design lineage.** Changed the conspicuously false claim "based on the US Apache platform" in the alleged Iranian equipment list. The actual Rooivalk programme used **Puma/Super Puma/Oryx dynamic components**, as discussed by Denel technical staff in [defenceWeb's 2016 reporting](https://defenceweb.co.za/aerospace/aerospace-aerospace/feature-denel-pushing-rooivalk-mk-2-export-orders/) and independently detailed in [Times Aerospace's aviation history](https://www.timesaerospace.aero/sandbox/features/defence/alliance-plots-the-new-route-for-rooivalk). These are design facts, **not** confirmation that any Rooivalk was exported to Iran. Its South African engineering achievements remain explicitly recognised.
2. **$4.2bn Turkcell claim is not automatically trebled under the US ATA.** The former mega-stat incorrectly imposed a **US statutory treble-damages regime** on East Asian Consortium/Turkcell's **South African civil action**. The [Supreme Court of Appeal's 29 April 2025 judgment, `[2025] ZASCA 50`](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZASCA/2025/50.html) identifies the pleaded claim as $4.2bn plus interest for lost business opportunities and addresses jurisdiction/choice of law. Changed the mega-stat's explanatory text and linked the primary decision. **No judgment on liability or damages followed from that SCA procedural ruling.**
3. **Updated legal posture and right of reply.** The April 2025 SCA decision allowed South African adjudication but also affirmed **Iranian law** on relevant issues. MTN subsequently sought leave to appeal and Turkcell opposed it in June 2025; [independent Engineering News report, 5 June 2025](https://engineeringnews.co.za/article/turkcell-files-opposing-papers-to-mtns-constitutional-court-appeal-2025-06-05). **UNVERIFIED:** any further October 2026 Constitutional Court order, merits finding or actual damages payment. The updated prose retains MTN's denial.
4. **Separate US ATA litigation.** Original wording claimed five ATA lawsuits were all filed in 2025, and linked a speculative combined **$5bn** penalty without a case-by-case pleading reconciliation. Reporting on [MTN's 2025 lawsuit update](https://businessreport.co.za/business-report/companies/5675535935995904/) confirms earlier **Chand and Davis proceedings filed in March 2022** and subsequent claims. Trebling may apply to qualifying ATA claims after satisfaction of statutory elements; it does not automatically apply to the Turkcell action. The unproven aggregate exposure is now **UNVERIFIED** in the article.
5. **Speculative sanctions cascade.** Removed presentation of an adverse *civil* judgment as a criminal "guilty verdict" and distinguished a hypothetical financial impact from an established automatic ban on US dollar payments, rating downgrade or loss of AGOA benefits. These possible consequences remain **UNVERIFIED** absent separate regulatory decisions.
