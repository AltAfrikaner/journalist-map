## High-priority publication corrections identified, NOT YET APPLIED
1. **The helicopter is not "based on the US Apache platform."** The live feature's shopping-list card calls the Rooivalk Apache-based, contradicting its introductory account of a Puma-derived design. [Denel's own technical briefing as reported by defenceWeb in 2016](https://defenceweb.co.za/aerospace/aerospace-aerospace/feature-denel-pushing-rooivalk-mk-2-export-orders/) identifies Super Puma/Oryx dynamic components. Independent aviation histories from [Times Aerospace (Guy Leitch)](https://www.timesaerospace.aero/sandbox/features/defence/alliance-plots-the-new-route-for-rooivalk) and [defenceWeb's Rooivalk fact file](https://defenceweb.co.za/aerospace/aerospace-aerospace/fact-file-denel-csh2ah2-rooivalk-attack-helicopter/) confirm the lineage. **Correct the Apache statement in the actual feature when a safe full-file read/edit path exists**, preserving its account of South African engineering innovation.
2. **The Turkcell US Anti-Terrorism Act multiplier is conflated with other MTN suits.** Under the "$4.2 billion Turkcell damages claim" section, the live feature says damages are automatically trebled under a US anti-terrorism statute. In contrast, the [South African SCA's 29 April 2025 decision, `[2025] ZASCA 50`](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZASCA/2025/50.html) describes the **East Asian Consortium/Turkcell $4.2bn plus interest** action as a South African delictual/competition claim. It addressed jurisdiction and governing law, **not damages merits**. The US Anti-Terrorism Act cases involve **different plaintiffs and alleged harms**. MTN's own [2025 proceedings update summarised in contemporaneous reporting](https://techafricanews.com/2025/04/30/turkcell-lawsuit-against-mtn-moves-forward-after-sca-ruling/) and [June 2025 coverage](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/companies/telecoms-and-technology/2025-06-05-turkcell-opposes-mtns-constitutional-court-appeal-over-iran-licence-case/) independently corroborate the procedural, not merits, outcome. **The automatic trebling of $4.2bn in that claim is unsupported**.
3. **Documentary claims ≠ completed arms transfers.** The dossier describes alleged offers to Iran, including a Rooivalk. A proposed, discussed or promised export does not prove that a helicopter was sold or delivered, that an export permit was granted, or that named officials committed bribery. Resolve these levels with original Turkcell pleadings, the National Conventional Arms Control Committee records and actual transfer licences. [SIPRI's updated arms-transfer database](https://www.sipri.org/databases/armstransfers) can help cross-check major conventional deliveries but cannot prove that private discussions did or did not occur. **UNVERIFIED:** concluded aircraft transaction, supply to Iranian armed forces, sanctions breach and individual criminal liability.
4. **Current procedural status unknown.** The 2025 SCA judgment opens the possibility of a South African trial; it does not itself prove the plaintiff's claims. Find Constitutional Court 2025–2026 leave-to-appeal results and trial docket before asserting a final win, criminal conviction or judicial exoneration. Distinguish company Hoffmann inquiry conclusions from judicial findings.
