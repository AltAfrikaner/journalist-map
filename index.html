Nature of Content

All material published on this platform constitutes investigative journalism, analytical commentary, and editorial opinion. Allegations vs Established Fact

Where this platform reports on allegations, indictments, charges, or legal proceedings, those matters are presented as allegations only. An indictment, charge, or criminal referral is not a finding of guilt. All persons and entities named in connection with legal proceedings are presumed innocent until proven otherwise by a court of competent jurisdiction.

Sources and Verification

Claims made on this platform are sourced from publicly available documents, court records, parliamentary records, commission findings, official government publications, licensed news agencies, primary documentation, and verifiable open-source intelligence. Where claims are disputed, that dispute is noted. Where a matter is unverified, it is labelled accordingly.
