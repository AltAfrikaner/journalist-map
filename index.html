Nature of Content

All material published on this platform constitutes investigative journalism, analytical commentary, and editorial opinion. Nothing published here constitutes legal advice, professional counsel, or verified legal findings. This platform operates as an independent press entity under the protections afforded to journalism and free expression.

Allegations vs Established Fact

Where this platform reports on allegations, indictments, charges, or legal proceedings, those matters are presented as allegations only. An indictment, charge, or criminal referral is not a finding of guilt. All persons and entities named in connection with legal proceedings are presumed innocent until proven otherwise by a court of competent jurisdiction.

Sources and Verification

Claims made on this platform are sourced from publicly available documents, court records, parliamentary records, commission findings, official government publications, licensed news agencies, primary documentation, and verifiable open-source intelligence. Where claims are disputed, that dispute is noted. Where a matter is unverified, it is labelled accordingly.

Jurisdiction

This platform publishes under South African constitutional protections (Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996, Section 16 -- Freedom of Expression), and the international norms applicable to press freedom under the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR), Article 19.

No Malice

No content on this platform is published with the intention to defame, harass, intimidate, or unlawfully harm any person. Where individuals are named, they are named in their capacity as public figures, in relation to matters of documented public interest, and on the basis of the public record.
