# Alt Afrikaner baseline intelligence protocol
Version: 2026-10-28 | Status: adopted methodology; no automated monitoring is claimed.

## Purpose
Track changes in public records relevant to Afrikaner language, history, minority rights, community safety, institutional accountability, education, economic opportunity, media reporting and relevant South African policy. Analyse all claims on their merits. A finding that contradicts an initial hypothesis must be preserved.

## Required procedure (all investigations)
1. Define the question and the subject's normal activity, time period, jurisdiction, comparison group and baseline data provenance.
2. Preserve versioned primary evidence: source title, URL, publisher, retrieval date, archive snapshot if available, relevant quotation/figure and context.
3. Compare like with like, normalise dates, financial units, coverage and name/identity collisions; separate administrative updates from material changes.
4. Classify change: financial, personnel, legal, language, policy, statistical, organisational, editorial or security.
5. Assign status OBSERVED, CORROBORATED, UNVERIFIED, DISPUTED, EXPLAINED, or REJECTED. Unverified allegations must never be presented as facts.
6. State at least two plausible hypotheses, including a non-adverse explanation, and the strongest evidence against the preferred hypothesis.
7. Verify material factual claims against two independent sources when possible. Repeated syndication of the same account is one source, not two.
8. Separate documented links (funding, affiliation, retweets, employment, shared event) from inferred coordination or control. No guilt by association.
9. Provide a right of reply for adverse publishable claims when feasible, and adhere to lawful public-interest research; no private data, doxxing or familial guilt.
10. Record a clear publication decision and the evidence that would change it. Update corrections and contradictions without silently altering earlier records.

## Suggested investigation register schema
id,subject,theme,baseline_period,baseline_evidence,observation_date,observed_change,change_class,source_one,source_two,alternative_explanation,counterevidence,status,confidence,editor,followup,reviewed_at,last_verified

## First-pass coverage
- Language and education: legislation, school governance, curriculum, language rights, court decisions.
- Community safety: incident series, denominator data, charge categories, police vs hospital records, geographic comparators.
- Economic inclusion and regulation: legal text, procurement, employment data, company policies, historical trend.
- Migration and demographics: Stats SA estimates, destination-country migration admissions, methods and denominators.
- Funding and institutional networks: annual reports, public grant disclosures, accounting periods, public directors, partnerships.
- Media and fact-checking: dated claims, corrections, sampling of comparable stories, editorial context.
- Afrikaner heritage and innovation: patent/priority records, archival sources, competing origin claims.
- Autarky, industry and national capability: output series, independent industrial data, adjusted constant-price comparisons.

## Publishing thresholds
- No finding or anomaly is proof of wrongdoing.
- State the denominator, time range, limitations and whether numbers are estimated or observed.
- Clearly label illustrative examples.
- No claim of active surveillance or automatic alerts unless an actual scheduled monitor has been configured and verified.
- Review the baseline after a major methodological or data change.

## Minimal recurring process
Monthly: refresh priority datasets and compare snapshot digests.
Quarterly: re-evaluate high-value open leads, unresolved contradictions and corrections.
Before publication: independent source validation, alternative-hypothesis check and responsible editorial review.
