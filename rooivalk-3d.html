Airframe

The Rooivalk was developed as a dedicated attack helicopter. Its narrow fuselage, stepped tandem cockpit, stub wings and tail rotor define its recognisable profile.

Research note

This is an original, procedural 3D reconstruction built for this page from published dimensions and photographic references. It is not Denel CAD data or a factory digital twin. External proportions are research-led; hidden structures and internal mechanisms are not represented as verified engineering.
